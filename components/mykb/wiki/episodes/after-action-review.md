# After Action Review

## Summary

After Action Review — Team reflection ritual: what was expected, what happened, why, next time.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Four questions: intended, actually happened, why difference, what to sustain/improve.
- Originated in the US Army and spread to organizations.
- Fast cadence keeps it close to the event and honest.
- Feeds reflective-practice and organizational learning.
