# AI Safety Policies

## Summary
AI safety policies are written commitments and rules: what will be built, tested, and shipped under which conditions.

## Details
- AI safety policies are written commitments and rules: what will be built, tested, and shipped under which conditions.
- Policy quality is judged by specificity, enforceability, and auditability.
- Policies without enforcement are marketing.
- RSIS3 relevance: the practices document plus checker is an enforceable safety policy.
