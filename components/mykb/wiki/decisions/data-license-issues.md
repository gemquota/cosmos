# Data License Issues

## Summary
Data license issues arise when training data is used beyond its license terms — scraping, redistribution, or derived models.

## Details
- Data license issues arise when training data is used beyond its license terms — scraping, redistribution, or derived models.
- Web-scale training makes provenance murky and disputes frequent.
- Mitigations: documented provenance, licenses at ingestion, and filtering.
- RSIS3 relevance: raw captures in the bundle should record their source licenses.
