# Trade Secrets in AI

## Summary
Trade secrets protect AI innovations through secrecy rather than publication: weights, data pipelines, and recipes.

## Details
- Trade secrets protect AI innovations through secrecy rather than publication: weights, data pipelines, and recipes.
- Secrecy conflicts with transparency, auditing, and reproducibility.
- Security and NDAs are the enforcement tools.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's private scripts and identity data are trade-secret-like assets.
