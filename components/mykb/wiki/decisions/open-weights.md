# Open Weights

## Summary
Open weights means publishing a model's trained parameters, allowing full local use, fine-tuning, and inspection.

## Details
- Open weights means publishing a model's trained parameters, allowing full local use, fine-tuning, and inspection.
- Benefits: transparency, research, and distributed innovation.
- Risks: misuse at scale, no recall, and liability diffusion.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's own outputs are openly readable (markdown), a weights-level transparency choice.
