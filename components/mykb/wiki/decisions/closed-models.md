# Closed Models

## Summary
Closed models are accessible only via the vendor's API: weights are secret, usage is governed by terms.

## Details
- Closed models are accessible only via the vendor's API: weights are secret, usage is governed by terms.
- Benefits: centralized safety controls and abuse monitoring.
- Costs: opacity, lock-in, and dependence on vendor policy.
- RSIS3 relevance: closed models trade auditability for control; the bundle favors open artifacts.
