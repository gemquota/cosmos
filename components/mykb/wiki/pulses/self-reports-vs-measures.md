# Self-Reports vs Measures

## Summary
Self-reports are what a system claims about its state or quality; measures are independent observations.

## Details
- Self-reports are what a system claims about its state or quality; measures are independent observations.
- The gap between them is a calibration and honesty signal, and a safety signal when self-reports flatter.
- Loops must be built on measures, with self-reports treated as hypotheses.
- RSIS3 relevance: the practices checker is the measure that arbitrates the loop's self-reports.
