# Self-Improvement Spirals

## Summary
A spiral is a recursive loop that is either compounding gains (virtuous) or compounding errors (vicious).

## Details
- A spiral is a recursive loop that is either compounding gains (virtuous) or compounding errors (vicious).
- Virtuous spirals need honest evaluation; vicious ones usually begin with gamed or corrupted feedback.
- Runaway spirals are the intelligence-explosion hypothesis; damped spirals are engineering practice.
- RSIS3 relevance: check-practices and rollback are the dampers on the bundle's spiral.
