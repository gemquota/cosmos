# Capability Measurement

## Summary
Capability measurement estimates a system's true abilities: benchmark scores, task batteries, and behavioral probes.

## Details
- Capability measurement estimates a system's true abilities: benchmark scores, task batteries, and behavioral probes.
- Good measurement separates capability from context: same task, many conditions.
- Metrics are capability proxies, not capabilities; Goodhart applies when they become targets.
- RSIS3 relevance: eval scores and graph coverage metrics are the bundle's capability measures.
