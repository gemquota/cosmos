# Improvement Velocity

## Summary
Improvement velocity is the rate of change of a system's quality metric across iterations.

## Details
- Improvement velocity is the rate of change of a system's quality metric across iterations.
- Measuring it requires stable benchmarks; unstable or gamed metrics produce fake velocity.
- High velocity is desirable only if the metric tracks real value (Goodhart caution).
- RSIS3 relevance: pass stats and graph-health trends are the bundle's velocity signal.
