# Self-Ratings

## Summary
Self-ratings are a system's qualitative or coarse judgments about its own performance.

## Details
- Self-ratings are a system's qualitative or coarse judgments about its own performance.
- Research on LLMs shows self-ratings often diverge from measured quality; they are biased by prompt and context.
- They remain useful as directional signals inside loops when paired with external checks.
- RSIS3 relevance: pulse reflections include self-ratings, which check-practices can override.
