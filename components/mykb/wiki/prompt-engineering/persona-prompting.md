# Persona Prompting

## Summary
Giving the model a defined persona to shape voice, values, and behavior

## Details
- Personas stabilize tone and domain stance.
- Overly rigid personas can harm task accuracy.
- Best combined with style-adaptation and tone-control.
- Widely used in customer-support and sales agents.
