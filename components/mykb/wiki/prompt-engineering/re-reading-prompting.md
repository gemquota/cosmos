# Re-Reading Prompting

## Summary
Prompting strategy that repeats the question or context before answering to reduce comprehension errors

## Details
- Re-presenting the query gives the model a second pass over details.
- Helps on long, dense, or easily misread prompts.
- Simple to apply; effect varies by task and model.
- One of many prompt-engineering micro-techniques.
