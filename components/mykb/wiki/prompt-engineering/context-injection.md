# Context Injection

## Summary
Inserting retrieved or computed context into prompts at the right position and granularity

## Details
- Injection order and framing affect model use of context.
- Clear delimiters reduce instruction confusion.
- Injected content must be provenance-tagged.
- Central to retrieval-prompting.
