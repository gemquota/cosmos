# Temperature Anisotropy

## Summary
Phenomenon where model context and probability distributions are directionally biased in embedding space

## Details
- Anisotropy concentrates representations in a narrow cone.
- It distorts similarity scores and retrieval quality.
- Mitigations include centering and contrastive training.
- Important for embeddings-and-vector-search design.
