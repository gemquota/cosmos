# Role Prompting

## Summary
Assigning the model a professional or functional role to guide its approach

## Details
- Role frames (analyst, critic, teacher) steer reasoning style.
- Effective for domain-specific tasks.
- Roles should not override factual accuracy.
- Complements persona-prompting.
