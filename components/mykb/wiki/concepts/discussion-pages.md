# Discussion Pages

## Summary
Discussion pages give articles a place for debate — questions, disagreements, proposed changes — without dirtying the article itself.

## Details
- They are where consensus forms before structural edits, and where controversial decisions leave their rationale.
- Discussion pages rot without process: every thread needs a resolution state and a link to the outcome.
- For mykb, discussion pages are optional and usually resolve into a proposal or an edit; unresolved threads get archived.
