# Decision Hygiene

## Summary

Decision Hygiene — Process practices that reduce bias contamination in decisions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Hygiene moves: independent estimates, consider-the-opposite, premortem, and journals.
- Focuses on cleaning the decision process rather than fixing the decision-maker.
- Especially valuable for group and high-stakes decisions.
- Pairs with red-teaming and adversarial review.
