# Curated Lists

## Summary
Curated lists are hand-picked collections — 'best articles on X', 'resources for Y' — where selection judgment is the content.

## Details
- A curated list's value is the curation: why each entry is included should be visible, or the list is just a directory.
- Lists need maintenance because relevance decays; a stale curated list quietly misleads.
- For mykb, curated lists are a form of hub page and are flagged for review on the freshness calendar.
