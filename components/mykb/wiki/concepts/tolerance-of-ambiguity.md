# Tolerance of Ambiguity

## Summary

Tolerance of Ambiguity — Comfort with uncertain, incomplete, or contradictory situations.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- High tolerance supports exploration, creativity, and calm under uncertainty.
- Low tolerance drives premature closure and black-and-white thinking.
