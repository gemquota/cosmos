# Selective Attention

## Summary

Selective Attention — Focusing processing on chosen stimuli while filtering others.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Selection operates at perceptual, spatial, and task levels.
- Filtering is partial: unattended material can still prime behavior.
- Measured with dichotic listening, visual search, and cueing tasks.
- Foundation for divided-attention and sustained-attention research.
