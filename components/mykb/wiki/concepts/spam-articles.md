# Spam Articles

## Summary
Spam articles are pages created to promote something — a product, a service, a person — rather than to inform.

## Details
- Spam is a quality problem and a trust problem: each spam page that survives teaches spammers to try again.
- The response is deletion or archiving under the delete-proposal process, plus prevention at the seeding gate.
- For mykb, spam articles fail the notability and relevance policies and are the deletion-criteria case.
