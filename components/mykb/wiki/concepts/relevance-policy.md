# Relevance Policy

## Summary
Relevance policy defines whether content fits the wiki's purpose — the topic must matter to the wiki's readers and goals, not merely be true.

## Details
- It is the filter that keeps the wiki focused: a correct article about the wrong thing still gets removed.
- Relevance is judged against the stated purpose, so the policy starts by stating the purpose.
- For mykb, relevance policy is applied at seeding and review, keeping the bundle aligned with the knowledge system's needs.
