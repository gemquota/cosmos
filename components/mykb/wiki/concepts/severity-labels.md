# Severity Labels

## Summary
Severity labels rank the harm a problem causes if unfixed — broken navigation is more severe than a casing inconsistency.

## Details
- Severity is independent of priority: a severe problem on a rarely-read page may wait, while a minor problem on the home page may not.
- The scale must be defined in words ('breaks the wiki', 'misleads readers', 'cosmetic') or labels drift into personal judgment.
- For mykb, severity labels are assigned at triage and drive the urgency half of scheduling.
