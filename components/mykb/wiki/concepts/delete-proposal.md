# Delete Proposal

## Summary
A delete proposal records the case for removing an article entirely: what it is, why deletion beats archival or merge, and who approved it.

## Details
- Deletion is the least reversible curation action, so proposals demand the highest bar and a visible approval trail.
- The proposal should also name the deletion's graph impact — every inbound link must be handled first.
- For mykb, delete proposals follow deletion-criteria and are reviewed before the article is actually removed.
