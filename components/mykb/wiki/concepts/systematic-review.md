# Systematic Review

## Summary

Systematic Review — Explicit, reproducible method for collecting and appraising all relevant studies.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Pre-specified search, inclusion criteria, quality appraisal, and synthesis.
- Reduces cherry-picking and narrative bias.
- PRISMA guidelines standardize reporting.
- Feeds meta-analysis when studies are sufficiently similar.
