# Minor Edits

## Summary
Minor edits are small, safe changes — typo fixes, formatting, link repairs — that do not change the article's meaning.

## Details
- Marking an edit minor lets reviewers skip it in detail, which keeps review load sane.
- The minor label is a promise: a content change disguised as minor breaks the trust in the label.
- For mykb, minor edits are the bulk of maintenance-days work and are batch-reviewed.
