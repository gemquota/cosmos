# Advertorials

## Summary
Advertorials are pages written to look like objective articles while actually promoting a product or organization.

## Details
- They are more corrosive than obvious ads because they exploit the reader's trust in the wiki's neutrality.
- Detection relies on source-checking: who is cited, what is omitted, and whether the praise is earned by evidence.
- For mykb, advertorials are removed or rewritten to NPOV under the promotional-content rules.
