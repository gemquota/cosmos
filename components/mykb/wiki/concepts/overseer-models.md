# Overseer Models

## Summary
Overseer models monitor other systems' outputs and flag anomalies, violations, or dangerous content.

## Details
- Overseer models monitor other systems' outputs and flag anomalies, violations, or dangerous content.
- They are cheap, parallel, and tireless — but they can be fooled or gamed.
- Defense in depth pairs overseers with rules, humans, and audits.
- RSIS3 relevance: the link checker and practice checker are overseer models for the wiki.
