# Adversarial Collaboration

## Summary

Adversarial Collaboration — Rivals jointly designing studies that could disconfirm one side.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Kahneman's practice: researchers with conflicting views co-design decisive tests.
- Reduces motivated reasoning and speeds scientific resolution.
- Pre-registration and shared measures make it credible.
- Model for productive disagreement inside teams.
