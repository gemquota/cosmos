# Information Hazards

## Summary
An information hazard is data or knowledge that causes harm simply by being known — recipe-level bioweapons guidance, novel exploit chains, or persuasion playbooks. Information-hazard analysis sorts knowledge into safe and dangerous classes and guides disclosure policy.

## Details
- **Classes** — data hazards (harmful datasets), idea hazards (harmful concepts), and signal hazards (harmful communication).
- **Dual-use tension** — the same knowledge (e.g., gain-of-function techniques) enables defense and offense.
- **Policy tools** — restricted dissemination, tiered access, and disclosure review at publication.
- **For AI** — capability evals and model weights are themselves information hazards; open-weights debates hinge on this.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the knowledge graph's sources include hazard-aware curation; provenance notes flag dual-use content.
