# Worst-Case Reasoning

## Summary
Worst-case reasoning chooses actions by considering the worst plausible outcome, used when stakes are catastrophic.

## Details
- Worst-case reasoning chooses actions by considering the worst plausible outcome, used when stakes are catastrophic.
- Maximin and minimax are its formalizations.
- It trades expected-value efficiency for robustness and is apt when failure is irreversible.
- RSIS3 relevance: irreversible graph operations (deletions, merges) use worst-case caution.
