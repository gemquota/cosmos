# Recent Changes

## Summary
Recent changes is the chronological feed of the latest edits — who changed what, when, and to which article.

## Details
- It is the first line of defense for spotting bad edits and the pulse of wiki activity.
- The feed needs filters (minor edits, patrolled state, namespace) or it drowns in noise.
- For mykb, recent changes feeds the review queue and the digest-pages that summarize activity.
