# Bans

## Summary
Bans remove an editor's ability to contribute, imposed for conduct that undermines the wiki — persistent vandalism, sockpuppetry, or repeated policy-breaking.

## Details
- A ban is a process outcome with evidence and a defined scope (temporary or permanent, page or site-wide), not a spontaneous act.
- Ban enforcement depends on the wiki being able to identify accounts, which is why registration exists.
- For mykb, bans follow the warning and dispute-resolution ladder and are logged with their rationale.
