# Instruction Robustness

## Summary
Instruction robustness is a model's reliable interpretation of instructions despite paraphrases, ambiguities, or hostile reformulations.

## Details
- Instruction robustness is a model's reliable interpretation of instructions despite paraphrases, ambiguities, or hostile reformulations.
- Instruction hierarchies and prompt discipline improve it.
- Failure shows up as instruction-following accidents.
- RSIS3 relevance: worker briefs are instructions; robust parsing of them is tested by pass outcomes.
