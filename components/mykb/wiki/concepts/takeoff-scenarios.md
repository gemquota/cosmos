# Takeoff Scenarios

## Summary
Takeoff scenarios are stylized futures for how quickly AI systems go from roughly human-level to far beyond: slow/continuous, fast/explosive, or moderate. They matter because they determine whether oversight, verification, and governance can keep pace with capability.

## Details
- **Slow takeoff** — capabilities rise smoothly with economic and regulatory adaptation; many fixes stay viable.
- **Fast takeoff** — recursive self-improvement compounds in weeks or days, outrunning any review process.
- **Moderate takeoff** — episodic jumps (new training paradigm, self-improving scaffold) separated by plateaus.
- **Policy import** — most governance levers (evals, audits, staged deployment) assume moderate-to-slow timelines.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the bundle's own improvement loop is deliberately slow: passes like this one add knowledge, not raw capability, keeping the loop inspectable.
