# Related Topics

## Summary
Related topics are the concepts an article sits beside: siblings, parents, and close neighbors that a reader might want next.

## Details
- Choosing related topics well is a synthesis skill — the list reveals how the author thinks the concept connects to the rest of the knowledge base.
- Related topics overlap with Related blocks but are more selective: quality over coverage.
- For mykb, related topics are reviewed at promotion time to make sure the article is woven into its cluster.
