# Evergreen Articles

## Summary
Evergreen articles are written so they stay accurate: they describe durable mechanisms and principles rather than current versions and news.

## Details
- Evergreen is a writing discipline — phrasing claims to survive version changes ('the current release as of 2026') keeps a page alive.
- Evergreen articles still need review, but on the longest cadence, which makes them the cheapest pages to own.
- For mykb, evergreen articles are the backbone of the wiki and the target state most promotions aim for.
