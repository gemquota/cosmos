# Need for Closure

## Summary

Need for Closure — Desire for a definite answer over ambiguity.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- High need for closure yields earlier, more confident, more stereotyped judgments.
- Situational pressures (time, noise) can induce it temporarily.
- Low need tolerates ambiguity and deeper information search.
- Relevant to decision hygiene and uncertainty tolerance.
