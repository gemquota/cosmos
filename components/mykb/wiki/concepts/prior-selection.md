# Prior Selection

## Summary

Prior Selection — Choosing the probability distribution that encodes prior knowledge.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Priors can be informative (domain knowledge) or weakly informative (safe defaults).
- Choice should be transparent and sensitivity-checked.
- Empirical Bayes borrows from data to set priors.
- Prior choice is a modeling judgment, not a bias to hide.
