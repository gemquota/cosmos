# Warnings

## Summary
Warnings are the escalating notice system for problem behavior: a first notice explains the issue, later notices escalate the stakes, and the final step is a block or ban.

## Details
- The ladder exists so that sanctions never come as a surprise — every editor knows where they stand.
- Warnings must be specific (which edit, which rule) to be fair and useful.
- For mykb, the warning ladder is documented in the editorial policy and applied by the quality team.
