# Completeness Score

## Summary
The completeness score rates coverage: does the article address the questions its scope implies — definitions, mechanism, tradeoffs, examples, and sources?

## Details
- Completeness is judged against the article's own scope, so the rubric starts from the summary's promises.
- A complete stub is impossible by definition; completeness is a full-article metric.
- For mykb, completeness scoring drives expansion-needed flags and the promotion queue.
