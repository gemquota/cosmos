# Reciprocal Links

## Summary
Reciprocal links are pairs where each article links to the other, creating a two-way edge in the knowledge graph.

## Details
- They are the strongest evidence that a relationship is real: both pages independently judged the other worth linking.
- Reciprocity is measured by the bidirectional-link-ratio and enforced as a soft convention in Related blocks.
- For mykb, reciprocal links make the graph more navigable and are a deliberate target of link-fix sprints.
