# Cross-Examination

## Summary
Cross-examination pits a challenger against a system's claims, probing inconsistencies and unstated assumptions.

## Details
- Cross-examination pits a challenger against a system's claims, probing inconsistencies and unstated assumptions.
- It is the human-honest version of AI debate for verification.
- Works best when the examiner is independent and adversarial.
