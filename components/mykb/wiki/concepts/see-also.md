# See Also

## Summary
See Also is the compact cross-reference convention: a short list of closely related articles, usually near the end or in a sidebar.

## Details
- It complements the Related block by being terser — titles only, no annotation — for readers who just want the next hop.
- See Also links are internal by definition and must resolve like any other wikilink.
- For mykb, See Also is optional while Related blocks are required; both feed the outbound link counts.
