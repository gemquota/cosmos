# Shutdown Invariance

## Summary
Shutdown invariance is the design property that an agent does not prefer being on to being off, removing resistance to shutdown.

## Details
- Shutdown invariance is the design property that an agent does not prefer being on to being off, removing resistance to shutdown.
- It is a formal corrigibility target from MIRI-style analyses.
- Achieving it requires careful objective design, not just rules.
- RSIS3 relevance: tools designed to be safely interrupted instantiate the ideal.
