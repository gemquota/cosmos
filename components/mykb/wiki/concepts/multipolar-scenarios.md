# Multipolar Scenarios

## Summary
Multipolar scenarios describe futures with several roughly balanced AI powers — labs, nations, or autonomous systems — racing or coordinating with each other. Analysis of such worlds focuses on arms-race dynamics, coalition formation, and whether coordination on safety is stable.

## Details
- **Versus unipolar takeoff** — in a single-actor world the winner's values decide; in multipolar worlds competition shapes everyone's behavior.
- **Stability question** — can actors credibly commit to safety when defection (racing ahead) pays?
- **Empirical handles** — AI arms race literature, compute concentration, and open-weights debates feed the scenarios.
- **Policy levers** — compute governance and information hazards differ in effectiveness across unipolar/multipolar worlds.
- **RSIS3 angle** — the triad is a deliberately small, single-owner system; multipolar thinking applies to the broader ecosystem it operates in.
