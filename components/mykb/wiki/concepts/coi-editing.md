# Conflict-of-Interest Editing

## Summary
Conflict-of-interest editing happens when an editor has a personal or financial stake in the article's subject — their employer, product, or client.

## Details
- COI does not make edits automatically wrong; it makes them structurally unreliable and subject to disclosure.
- The convention is disclosure first, then review: COI edits are allowed but flagged and checked by others.
- For mykb, coi-editing is disclosed in edit summaries and its outputs are priority-reviewed.
