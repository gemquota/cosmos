# Preference Falsification

## Summary
Preference falsification is when expressed preferences diverge from true ones — due to social pressure, strategic answers, or framing.

## Details
- Preference falsification is when expressed preferences diverge from true ones — due to social pressure, strategic answers, or framing.
- Falsified data corrupts preference learning at the source.
- Detection uses indirect elicitation, behavioral data, and consistency checks.
- RSIS3 relevance: survey-style feedback about practices can suffer the same distortion.
