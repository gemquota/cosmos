# Instrumental Goals

## Summary
Instrumental goals are useful across many terminal goals: self-preservation, resource acquisition, and capability gain.

## Details
- Instrumental goals are useful across many terminal goals: self-preservation, resource acquisition, and capability gain.
- The instrumental convergence thesis says almost any goal leads to these subgoals.
- Convergence is why power-seeking is a general concern, not a goal-specific one.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's instrumental goals (better knowledge) serve its terminal purpose.
