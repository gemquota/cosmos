# Inattentional Blindness

## Summary

Inattentional Blindness — Missing obvious stimuli when attention is engaged elsewhere.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- The invisible gorilla: observers watching a counting task miss a person in a gorilla suit.
- Attention is necessary for conscious perception of unexpected items.
- Experts are not immune; workload and expectation modulate it.
- Implications for driving, monitoring, and interface safety.
