# Hot Topics

## Summary
Hot topics are knowledge areas in rapid motion — new releases, breaking changes, active research — where articles decay fast.

## Details
- Hot topics need the shortest review cadence and the cheapest article formats; a deep-dive on a hot topic is a maintenance trap.
- Hotness is measured by change frequency in sources, search interest, and edit activity.
- For mykb, hot topics are tracked by freshness signals and get priority in refresh-days.
