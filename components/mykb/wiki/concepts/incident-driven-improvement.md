# Incident-Driven Improvement

## Summary
Incident-driven improvement upgrades a system because something actually failed: postmortems, lessons learned, and corrective actions turn outages and errors into durable fixes. It is the empirical complement to proactive design — and the honest way to close an improvement loop.

## Details
- **Postmortem practice** — blameless write-ups of what happened, why, and what changes prevent recurrence.
- **Why it works** — real incidents carry information no simulation has; near-misses are free warnings.
- **AI dimension** — eval failures, red-team findings, and jailbreak reports are incidents for model teams.
- **Risk** — without process, incidents are patched and forgotten (no loop closure); with process, each one compounds learning.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — check-practice failures and broken-link reports drive curation passes; the acquisition workflow records the fixes.
