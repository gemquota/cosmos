# Attentional Blink

## Summary

Attentional Blink — Missed second target when it appears ~200-500ms after the first in rapid streams.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- In rapid serial visual presentation, the second of two targets is often missed.
- Reflects a capacity bottleneck in temporal attention.
- Blink size varies with target similarity and training.
- Evidence for limited attentional resources over time.
