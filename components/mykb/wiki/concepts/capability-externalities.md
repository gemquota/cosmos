# Capability Externalities

## Summary
Capability externalities are the third-party effects of AI capability gains — risks and benefits experienced by people who did not choose the deployment. When a lab's capability advances create risks for everyone (or opportunities for bad actors), the market alone will not price them correctly.

## Details
- **Negative externalities** — increased cyber or bio risk, job displacement, and arms-race pressures imposed on others.
- **Positive externalities** — cheaper research tools and productivity spillovers.
- **Public-goods problem** — safety research is a public good; the market under-provides it, justifying coordination and compute governance.
- **Policy tools** — information-hazard disclosure rules, export controls on weights, and liability regimes internalize costs.
- **RSIS3 angle** — open publication of knowledge has dual use; the wiki's own sources include hazards guidance for this reason.
