# Belief Bias

## Summary

Belief Bias — Accepting conclusions that match prior beliefs regardless of validity.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- People rate believable conclusions as valid even when logic says otherwise.
- Strongest with complex or ambiguous arguments.
- Reduced but not eliminated by expertise and instruction.
- Interacts with dual-process accounts of reasoning.
