# Expected Utility

## Summary
Expected utility theory represents preferences as utility over outcomes, maximized in expectation under uncertainty.

## Details
- Expected utility theory represents preferences as utility over outcomes, maximized in expectation under uncertainty.
- It is the standard formal frame in economics, game theory, and AI value design.
- Violations (ambiguity aversion, risk attitudes) motivate bounded and robust variants.
- RSIS3 relevance: utility-function concepts in the graph sit on this foundation.
