# Moral Status Questions

## Summary
Moral status questions ask which entities have intrinsic moral standing and why: sentience, agency, interests, or species.

## Details
- Moral status questions ask which entities have intrinsic moral standing and why: sentience, agency, interests, or species.

- Status assumptions drive very different governance and treatment.
