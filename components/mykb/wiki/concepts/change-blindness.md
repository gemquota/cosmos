# Change Blindness

## Summary

Change Blindness — Failure to notice changes in a visual scene across disruptions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- People miss large changes when a flicker, blink, or cut interrupts the scene.
- Shows perception relies on sparse, task-relevant representation.
- Flicker paradigm and door studies are classic demonstrations.
- Important for eyewitness memory and interface design.
