# AI Timelines

## Summary
AI timelines are forecasts of when systems with transformative capability will exist. They range from years to decades, and they set the urgency of alignment and governance work: short timelines imply racing to keep up with capability.

## Details
- **Distribution** — expert surveys (e.g., AI Impacts, Metaculus) show wide disagreement, from this decade to mid-century or later.
- **Drivers** — scaling progress, algorithmic improvements, and compute growth; each has its own forecasting literature.
- **Why they matter** — timelines shape research priorities, compute governance, and institutional readiness.
- **Bias awareness** — forecasters face both hype and dismissal biases; calibration tracking is essential.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the bundle's slow, deliberate improvement loop is a hedge against both fast and slow timelines: it builds durable structure regardless.
