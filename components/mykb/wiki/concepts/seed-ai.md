# Seed AI

## Summary
Seed AI is a deliberately small, corrigible AI whose main job is to write better versions of itself, bootstrapping competence through its own improvement loop rather than through pure scaling. The term comes from the Singularity Institute for Artificial Intelligence (now MIRI).

## Details
- **Design stance** — the seed matters more than raw initial capability because compounding improvements dominate.
- **Corrigibility requirement** — a seed that resists shutdown or hides goals cannot be safely improved.
- **Evaluation problem** — the system must judge whether a proposed self-change is an improvement, which is a variant of the bootstrap problem.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the nine-loop stack is a light-weight seed: pulses, reflection, and check-practices form the improvement protocol around a static codebase.
- **Contrast** — scaling laws improve a fixed architecture; seed AI improves the architecture itself.
