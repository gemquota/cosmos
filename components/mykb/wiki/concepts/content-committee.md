# Content Committee

## Summary
The content committee is the body that decides content-level questions — coverage priorities, scope disputes, and major structural decisions.

## Details
- It is a governance layer above individual review: the committee sets direction, reviewers execute it.
- Committee decisions should be recorded as precedents, so future questions resolve faster.
- For mykb, the content committee works from the content policy and the gap analysis, choosing what the wiki should cover next.
