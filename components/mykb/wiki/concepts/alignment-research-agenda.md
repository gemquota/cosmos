# Alignment Research Agendas

## Summary
Alignment research agendas are structured plans proposing which problems to work on and why, from MIRI-style blueprints to OpenAI's superalignment agenda.

## Details
- Alignment research agendas are structured plans proposing which problems to work on and why, from MIRI-style blueprints to OpenAI's superalignment agenda.
- Agendas differ on timelines, tractability, and which subproblems are load-bearing.
- An agenda is healthy when it specifies falsifiable milestones.
