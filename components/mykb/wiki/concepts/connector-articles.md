# Connector Articles

## Summary
Connector articles bridge two clusters — a page that links a concept from one area into the vocabulary of another.

## Details
- Connectors are what keep the graph from fragmenting into disconnected islands; they are the highways between topic regions.
- A connector only works if it genuinely belongs to both sides; a forced bridge is just a bad link.
- For mykb, connectors are identified from graph structure and are natural homes for cross-domain synthesis.
