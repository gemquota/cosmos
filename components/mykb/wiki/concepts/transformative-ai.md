# Transformative AI

## Summary
Transformative AI is a capability threshold beyond which AI profoundly changes economies, science, and power structures — the kind of change comparable to the industrial revolution. It is the working definition used by timeline forecasters and governance researchers.

## Details
- **Definitions** — economic (GDP-level effects), scientific (automated research), or geopolitical (power shifts) transformations.
- **Forecast anchor** — most 'AGI timelines' are actually transformative-AI timelines; clarity about the target matters.
- **Risk framing** — transformative AI is not inherently catastrophic, but it concentrates the stakes of getting alignment right.
- **Policy** — compute governance and RSPs are often justified by proximity to transformative capability.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the triad's knowledge graph is a small-scale rehearsal of transformative knowledge work: automated acquisition, synthesis, and self-monitoring.
