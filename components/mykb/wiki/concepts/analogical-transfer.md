# Analogical Transfer

## Summary

Analogical Transfer — Applying knowledge from one situation to another via analogy.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Transfer succeeds when relational structure matches, even if surfaces differ.
- Gick & Holyoak: spontaneous transfer is rare unless the analogy is pointed out.
- Abstraction and multiple examples boost transfer.
- Core mechanism of learning by example and case-based reasoning.
