# Arbitration

## Summary
Arbitration is the binding dispute-resolution step for conflicts that discussion, mediation, and consensus have failed to settle.

## Details
- An arbitration body hears the case, applies policy, and issues a decision that is binding and recorded.
- Arbitration is expensive and slow, which is exactly why it is the last resort — its cost pushes earlier steps to work.
- For mykb, arbitration handles policy-breaking conduct and intractable content disputes, and its decisions become precedent.
