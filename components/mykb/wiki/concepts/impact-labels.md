# Impact Labels

## Summary
Impact labels rank the blast radius of a problem: one article, a cluster, or the whole wiki.

## Details
- Impact is about scope, severity about harm; a low-severity issue with wiki-wide impact can outrank a high-severity one in one article.
- Impact is usually measurable from the link graph — incoming-link-counts and popularity-weighting give the numbers.
- For mykb, impact labels are assigned at triage using graph metrics and reviewed when the queue is reprioritized.
