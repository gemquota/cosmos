# RLAIF (RL from AI Feedback)

## Summary
RLAIF replaces human preference labels with AI-generated feedback, typically by having a model evaluate candidate responses against explicit principles. Constitutional AI (Anthropic, 2022) showed this can scale harmlessness training and reduce reliance on human labeling.

## Details
- **Constitutional AI recipe** — a model critiques and revises its own outputs according to a written constitution, then a preference model is trained on AI-generated comparisons.
- **Why it scales** — AI feedback is cheaper and faster than human feedback, enabling larger training runs.
- **Risk** — the feedback model inherits the flaws of its source model; evaluation quality becomes a bootstrap problem.
- **Measurement** — RLAIF-trained models are judged by human evals and red-teaming, keeping AI feedback in the loop but not the final word.
- **RSIS3 parallel** — check-practices is a 'constitution' applied by an automated checker to the workspace, with humans auditing results.
