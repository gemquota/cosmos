# Devil's Advocate

## Summary

Devil's Advocate — Designated role of arguing against a proposal to test its soundness.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- The role frees members to challenge consensus without personal stake.
- Effectiveness depends on genuine alternatives, not ritual opposition.
- Weakest when the advocacy is performative or the group ignores it.
- A pillar of red-teaming and adversarial collaboration.
