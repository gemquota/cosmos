# Notability Policy

## Summary
Notability policy defines whether a topic deserves its own article — the bar between 'worth capturing' and 'worth a permanent page'.

## Details
- Notability is about durable relevance: a topic notable if it recurs, connects, and can be sourced.
- The policy exists to prevent the wiki from filling with one-off trivia that nobody will maintain.
- For mykb, notability policy operationalizes seed-article-criteria into a consistent, citable rule.
