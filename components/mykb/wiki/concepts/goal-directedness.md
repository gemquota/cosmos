# Goal-Directedness

## Summary
Goal-directedness measures how consistently an agent pursues an objective across situations, including novel ones.

## Details
- Goal-directedness measures how consistently an agent pursues an objective across situations, including novel ones.
- High goal-directedness is powerful and risky: robust pursuit amplifies both competence and misalignment.
- Probing goal-directedness in LLMs and RL agents is an active research area.
- RSIS3 relevance: evaluating the loop's goal-directedness tracks whether it stays on mission.
