# Exemplar Theory

## Summary

Exemplar Theory — Categorization by similarity to stored individual instances.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Categories are represented by remembered exemplars, not summaries.
- Explains typicality, exceptions, and context sensitivity.
- Computationally simple: nearest-neighbor style decisions.
- Competes with prototype theory in category-learning research.
