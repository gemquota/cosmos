# Adversarial Robustness

## Summary
Adversarial robustness is a model's resistance to inputs crafted to fool it, from pixel perturbations to jailbreaks.

## Details
- Adversarial robustness is a model's resistance to inputs crafted to fool it, from pixel perturbations to jailbreaks.
- Adversaries adapt; static defenses rot.
- Robustness is evaluated by attack-and-defend cycles, not single tests.
- RSIS3 relevance: checker bypasses are adversarial attacks on the bundle's rules.
