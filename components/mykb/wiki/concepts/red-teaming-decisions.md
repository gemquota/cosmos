# Red Teaming Decisions

## Summary

Red Teaming Decisions — Adversarial review of decisions by a designated critical team.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- A red team tries to break the plan, find blind spots, and test assumptions.
- Works best with independence and real authority to object.
- Structure: pre-mortems, devil's advocate, and war-gaming.
- Widely used in security, military, and product strategy.
