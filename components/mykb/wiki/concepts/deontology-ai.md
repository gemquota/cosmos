# Deontology for AI

## Summary
Deontological AI ethics binds systems to rules and duties rather than optimizing outcomes.

## Details
- Deontological AI ethics binds systems to rules and duties rather than optimizing outcomes.
- Rules give predictability and protect rights, but conflict with optimization and can be gamed literally.
- Modern hybrid approaches add side constraints to consequentialist objectives.
- RSIS3 relevance: the practices document is deontological — rules the loop must obey.
