# Moral Agency

## Summary
Moral agency is the capacity to act morally or be held responsible for actions.

## Details
- Moral agency is the capacity to act morally or be held responsible for actions.
- AI systems currently lack full moral agency (no autonomy, no moral understanding), but partial agency is debated.
- Attributing agency affects liability, rights, and control expectations.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's agents are tool-like; moral agency is future-facing.
