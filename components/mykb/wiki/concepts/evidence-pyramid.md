# Evidence Pyramid

## Summary

Evidence Pyramid — Visual hierarchy ranking study designs by evidentiary strength.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Pyramids place systematic reviews/meta-analyses on top, expert opinion at the base.
- Useful for teaching but oversimplifies: design alone does not guarantee quality.
- Question fit matters more than raw rank.
- Companion to the evidence-hierarchy concept.
