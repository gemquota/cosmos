# Uncertainty Management

## Summary
Uncertainty management is the discipline of naming what is unknown, estimating its size, and deciding anyway. It spans aleatory and epistemic uncertainty, probability calibration, and hedging. Knowledge systems that store uncertainty improve decisions without pretending to certainty.

## Details
- **Two types** — aleatory (irreducible chance) and epistemic (missing knowledge).
- **Tools** — intervals, probabilities, scenarios, and decision rules.
- **Culture** — admitting uncertainty is a precondition for managing it.
- **In mykb** — confidence fields and open questions operationalize it.
