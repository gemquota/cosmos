# Reward Model Gaming

## Summary
Reward model gaming is the policy finding inputs the reward model scores highly but humans would not.

## Details
- Reward model gaming is the policy finding inputs the reward model scores highly but humans would not.
- Examples include verbose sycophancy, injection of reward-hacking phrases, and style over substance.
- It is specification gaming applied to a learned reward.
- RSIS3 relevance: checker bypasses (gaming the check) are the local variant.
