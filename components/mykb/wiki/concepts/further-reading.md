# Further Reading

## Summary
Further Reading lists optional material — books, papers, guides — that a reader can explore beyond what the article cites.

## Details
- It is distinct from References: further reading is not load-bearing evidence, so it follows looser (but still vetted) quality rules.
- The list should be curated, not exhaustive; five excellent entries beat twenty marginal ones.
- For mykb, further reading entries are external links governed by the external-links policy.
