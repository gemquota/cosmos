# Memorization vs Generalization

## Summary
Memorization and generalization are poles of a spectrum: models both store training data and extract rules.

## Details
- Memorization and generalization are poles of a spectrum: models both store training data and extract rules.
- Some memorization is necessary (facts, vocabulary); excessive memorization is overfitting.
- Understanding the balance is key for privacy and eval integrity.
- RSIS3 relevance: the wiki must memorize (capture) before it generalizes (synthesizes).
