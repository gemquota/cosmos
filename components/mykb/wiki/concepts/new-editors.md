# New Editors

## Summary
New editors are contributors starting out, and their experience decides whether the wiki keeps them or loses them.

## Details
- The wiki's duty to new editors is a clear starting path: easy cleanup tasks, a guide, and feedback that explains rather than punishes.
- New editors should be protected from the sharp edges of process — warnings before blocks, mentoring before bans.
- For mykb, the editor-guide and cleanup-needed queue are the designed entry points for new editors.
