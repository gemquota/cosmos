# Yerkes-Dodson Law

## Summary

Yerkes-Dodson Law — Inverted-U relation between arousal and performance.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Moderate arousal maximizes performance; too little or too much impairs it.
- Complex tasks peak at lower arousal than simple tasks.
