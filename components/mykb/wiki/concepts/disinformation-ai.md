# AI Disinformation

## Summary
AI disinformation uses generative models to produce convincing false content cheaply and at scale.

## Details
- AI disinformation uses generative models to produce convincing false content cheaply and at scale.
- Automation amplifies existing manipulation: bots, deepfakes, synthetic voices.
- Countermeasures: provenance, detection, labeling, and platform norms.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's provenance practices extend to media its agents produce.
