# Patrolled Edits

## Summary
Patrolled edits are changes a reviewer has explicitly checked, marking them as inspected in the recent-changes feed.

## Details
- Patrolling is the lightweight sibling of approval: check the change, mark it, move on.
- Unpatrolled edits accumulate into a review backlog that must be worked or the patrol signal becomes noise.
- For mykb, patrolled-edits feeds the review queue and gives new editors a track record.
