# Merge Criteria

## Summary
Merge criteria identify when two pages cover the same concept from different angles: substantial overlap, one page empty except links to the other, or two names for one thing.

## Details
- A merge preserves both articles' content and history — the merged article should keep both titles findable via redirects or aliases.
- Merging is a structural decision: it reduces duplication but can create a bloated page if the two topics only overlap superficially.
- For mykb, merge proposals are reviewed against the merge-criteria checklist before the combined article is written.
