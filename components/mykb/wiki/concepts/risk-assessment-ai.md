# Risk Assessment for AI

## Summary
AI risk assessment identifies hazards, estimates likelihood and severity, and prioritizes mitigations.

## Details
- AI risk assessment identifies hazards, estimates likelihood and severity, and prioritizes mitigations.
- Methods include hazard analysis, red-teaming, evals, and scenario analysis.
- Assessment quality depends on measurement quality and honesty about uncertainty.
- RSIS3 relevance: the pass verifier is a small risk assessment for the knowledge loop.
