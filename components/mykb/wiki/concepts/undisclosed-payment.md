# Undisclosed Payment

## Summary
Undisclosed payment is the serious violation: editing for money while hiding that fact from the community.

## Details
- It breaks the trust model twice — the content is biased and the bias is concealed — so it is treated as deception, not just conflict.
- Enforcement is investigation plus sanctions: contributions reviewed, accounts warned or banned, and affected articles checked.
- For mykb, undisclosed-payment is handled through the arbitration path and the edit history is audited.
