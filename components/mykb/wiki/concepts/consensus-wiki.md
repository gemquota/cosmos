# Consensus

## Summary
Consensus is the wiki's default decision method: a proposal is adopted when discussion produces broad agreement with the strongest objections addressed.

## Details
- Consensus is not unanimity and not majority vote — it is the outcome of good-faith engagement with objections.
- Consensus decisions should be recorded so the reasoning survives and later challenges have a baseline to argue against.
- For mykb, consensus governs structural changes and policy, while routine edits proceed without it.
