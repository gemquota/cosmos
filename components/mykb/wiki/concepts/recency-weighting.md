# Recency Weighting

## Summary
Recency weighting gives newer articles and updates more influence in metrics and rankings, on the theory that recent content reflects current reality.

## Details
- It is the right bias for fast-moving topics and the wrong one for evergreen reference material.
- Recency weighting is usually a decay function — recency decays unless refreshed — which is where half-life-knowledge enters.
- For mykb, recency weighting shapes freshness signals and the recent-changes feed.
