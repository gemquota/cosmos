# Similarity-Based Reasoning

## Summary

Similarity-Based Reasoning — Using similarity between cases to infer properties and make decisions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Similarity drives categorization, analogical mapping, and case-based reasoning.
- Which features count is context- and knowledge-dependent.
- Surface similarity can mislead when deeper structure differs.
- Formalized by feature-overlap and structural accounts.
