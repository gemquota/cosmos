# Forward Models

## Summary

Forward Models — Internal models that predict the next state from current state and action.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Forward models simulate the sensory consequences of actions.
- Enable fast correction before feedback arrives.
- Underpin motor learning, agency, and motor imagery.
- Prediction-error training tunes them.
