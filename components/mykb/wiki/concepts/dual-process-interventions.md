# Dual-Process Interventions

## Summary

Dual-Process Interventions — Methods that encourage deliberative System-2 thinking over intuitive System-1.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Interventions include slowing down, considering alternatives, and incentive alignment.
- Effects on classic bias tasks are real but often small and task-specific.
- Debiasing transfers best when combined with feedback and practice.
- Frames reasoning improvement as engagement of deliberation.
