# Modus Tollens

## Summary

Modus Tollens — Valid inference: if P then Q; not Q; therefore not P.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Correct but harder for people than modus ponens.
- Errors increase with abstract or negative content.
- Backward-chaining and diagnostic reasoning rely on it.
- Central to falsification-style hypothesis testing.
