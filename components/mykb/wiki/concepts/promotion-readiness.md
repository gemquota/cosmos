# Promotion Readiness

## Summary
Promotion readiness is the binary gate between stub and growing: sources verified, body within word bounds, links resolving, and the framing questions actually answered.

## Details
- Readiness is checked with the promotion checklist, not vibes — the checklist makes promotion repeatable across reviewers.
- An article can be ready for promotion while still needing future work; readiness is about the current version meeting the bar, not perfection.
- For mykb, promotion-readiness is what the curation sprints operationalize: batches of stubs are reviewed, promoted, and the full-article-ratio ticks up.
