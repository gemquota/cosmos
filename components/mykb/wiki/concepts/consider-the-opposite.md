# Consider the Opposite

## Summary

Consider the Opposite — Debiasing technique of actively generating reasons the current view could be wrong.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Larrick's technique: force alternative explanations before committing.
- Reduces overconfidence and anchoring in forecasts and evaluations.
- Cheap, portable, and one of the better-supported debiasing moves.
- Pairs with devil's advocate and premortem analysis.
