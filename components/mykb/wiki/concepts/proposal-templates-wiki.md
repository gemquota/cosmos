# Proposal Templates

## Summary
Proposal templates give every curation proposal — merge, split, delete, redirect — a standard shape: context, rationale, plan, and impact.

## Details
- Templates make proposals comparable and reviewable: the reviewer looks for the same fields every time.
- The template is only as good as its fields; fields that never get answered should be removed.
- For mykb, proposal templates are the written form of the criteria articles and live with the review workflow.
