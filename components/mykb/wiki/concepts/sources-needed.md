# Sources Needed

## Summary
Sources-needed marks articles that make verifiable claims without citations — the evidence layer is missing even if the claims are true.

## Details
- The flag is usually triggered by citation-needed checks during review, and cleared only when sources are added and verified.
- Sources-needed articles are ranked by claim density and risk, so the most surprising claims get sourced first.
- For mykb, sources-needed pages feed the verification-needed queue and the verifiability-score.
