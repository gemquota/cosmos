# Perception as Inference

## Summary

Perception as Inference — Helmholtz's view that perception is unconscious inference from cues.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Sensory data are ambiguous; perception resolves them with priors.
- Illusions reveal the inference machinery, not just optical quirks.
- Modern form lives in predictive processing and the Bayesian brain.
- Links perception to epistemology and reasoning.
