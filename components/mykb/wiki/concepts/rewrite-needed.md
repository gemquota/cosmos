# Rewrite Needed

## Summary
Rewrite-needed marks an article whose problems are beyond patching — structure, voice, or framing are wrong enough that fresh writing beats repair.

## Details
- The flag is the most expensive state in the wiki, so it is set deliberately and the article is usually de-prioritized or demoted while awaiting it.
- A rewrite should be scoped in the flag note: what to keep, what to discard, what sources to re-check.
- For mykb, rewrite-needed articles are demotion-candidates and are handled in review sprints, not urgent edits.
