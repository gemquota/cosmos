# Underfitting in LLMs

## Summary
Underfitting is a model failing to capture structure present in its training data, usually from insufficient scale or training.

## Details
- Underfitting is a model failing to capture structure present in its training data, usually from insufficient scale or training.
- It shows as high error on training-like inputs and poor skill acquisition.
- In the era of big models it is rarer than overfitting but still common for niche skills.
- RSIS3 relevance: underfit graph clusters fail to represent their topics.
