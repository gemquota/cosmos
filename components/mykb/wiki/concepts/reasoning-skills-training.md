# Reasoning Skills Training

## Summary

Reasoning Skills Training — Instructional programs that improve general reasoning abilities.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Explicit instruction in argument structure and bias awareness shows transfer.
- Effect sizes are modest and decay without practice.
- Training in specific domains (stats, logic) transfers better than generic 'brain training'.
- Design: worked examples, feedback, and spaced practice.
