# Forecasting Research

## Summary
Forecasting research studies how well people predict future events and how to make them better. Trained forecasters beat experts and institutions, largely through calibration practice, aggregation, and continuous updating. It is the empirical backbone of superforecasting.

## Details
- **Findings** — granular, numeric, regularly-scored forecasts outperform verbal judgments.
- **Key practices** — base rates, outside views, updating on evidence, and team aggregation.
- **Scoring** — Brier scores measure accuracy; calibration curves check confidence.
- **In mykb** — a forecast log with scores trains the skill.
