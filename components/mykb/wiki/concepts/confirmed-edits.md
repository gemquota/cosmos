# Confirmed Edits

## Summary
Confirmed edits are changes that passed the pending-changes gate and are now part of the visible article.

## Details
- Confirmation is a workflow state, not a quality verdict — it means the edit survived review, not that it is perfect.
- The confirmation history is what the recent-changes and patrolled-edits reports consume.
- For mykb, confirmed edits are the default state for edits made under the normal workflow.
