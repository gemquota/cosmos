# Open Source AI Governance

## Summary
Open source AI governance asks who can release what, under which terms, and with which duties of care.

## Details
- Open source AI governance asks who can release what, under which terms, and with which duties of care.
- Frameworks include license conditions, release checklists, and misuse monitoring.
- Governance must not assume open and closed are binary.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's own release notes and licenses are mini-governance.
