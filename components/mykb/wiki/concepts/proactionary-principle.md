# Proactionary Principle

## Summary
The proactionary principle favors proceeding with innovation absent demonstrated severe harm, prioritizing opportunity cost.

## Details
- The proactionary principle favors proceeding with innovation absent demonstrated severe harm, prioritizing opportunity cost.
- It is the counterweight to the precautionary principle in risk governance debates.
- AI policy debates often reduce to calibrating between the two.
