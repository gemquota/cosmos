# Pillar Pages

## Summary
Pillar pages are long-form anchor articles that a content area is built around — the definitive treatment that other pages reference rather than duplicate.

## Details
- A pillar is both a destination and a hub: it carries the full argument and links the supporting articles.
- Pillar strategy fails when the pillar tries to be everything — its scope must be defended with split-criteria.
- For mykb, pillar pages are chosen deliberately during acquisition planning, not discovered by accident.
