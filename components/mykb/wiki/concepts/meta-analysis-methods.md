# Meta-Analysis Methods

## Summary

Meta-Analysis Methods — Statistical pooling of effect sizes across studies.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Fixed-effect vs random-effects models handle different heterogeneity assumptions.
- Heterogeneity (I-squared) decides how much pooling is sensible.
- Sensitivity analyses and publication-bias checks are mandatory.
- Quality depends on the studies pooled: garbage in, garbage out.
