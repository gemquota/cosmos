# Topic Pages

## Summary
Topic pages organize a subject area: a lead summary, the key articles, and links to subtopics, giving newcomers a place to start.

## Details
- Unlike category pages, topic pages are curated — they choose and order, rather than dump every tag match.
- A topic page is a small synthesis: it asserts how the area is structured, so it needs maintenance as the area grows.
- For mykb, topic pages are the top of the parent-child hierarchy and are written deliberately, not generated.
