# Content Policy

## Summary
Content policy defines what belongs in the wiki and what does not: scope, coverage criteria, and the line between knowledge and noise.

## Details
- It is the charter the notability and relevance policies operationalize.
- Content policy changes are the most consequential governance acts, so they require the full consensus process.
- For mykb, content policy is what distinguishes the curated wiki from the raw capture pile.
