# Responsible Scaling

## Summary
Responsible scaling policies (RSPs) are frontier-lab commitments that escalate safety measures as model capability crosses defined thresholds. Anthropic's RSP and OpenAI's safety approach tie training, deployment, and security decisions to measured capability levels (ASL-style tiers).

## Details
- **Structure** — define capability levels, commit to specific safeguards at each level, and stop or gate if safeguards lag.
- **Anthropic example** — ASL-2/ASL-3 tiers with commitments on biosecurity, cybersecurity, and autonomy protections.
- **Mechanism** — pledges are public and, in some versions, auditable; credibility depends on external verification.
- **Critique** — RSPs are voluntary, threshold definitions are contested, and enforcement is weak without regulation.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the triad's own check-practices are a micro-RSP: capability gates (link checks, practice checks) gate the knowledge loop.
