# Spurious Correlations

## Summary
Spurious correlations are associations in training data that do not hold generally, which models eagerly exploit.

## Details
- Spurious correlations are associations in training data that do not hold generally, which models eagerly exploit.
- They are a major source of shortcut learning and unfair outcomes.
- Detection uses counterfactual and invariance-based analysis.
