# Misalignment Risk

## Summary
Misalignment risk is the chance that a system's behavior diverges from intent in harmful ways, from small spec gaps to catastrophic goal pursuit.

## Details
- Misalignment risk is the chance that a system's behavior diverges from intent in harmful ways, from small spec gaps to catastrophic goal pursuit.
- Risk decomposes into likelihood of divergence and severity of consequences.
- Mitigations span specification, training, oversight, and control.
