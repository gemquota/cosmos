# Authority Weighting

## Summary
Authority weighting gives articles that are established — verified, reviewed, stable — more weight than new or unverified ones.

## Details
- It encodes the sensible bias that proven content should outrank unproven content.
- Authority must be earned through the review pipeline, or the weight simply rewards age.
- For mykb, authority weighting is built from verification history and reviewed status, and it feeds search ranking.
