# Cohen's d

## Summary

Cohen's d — Standardized mean difference between two groups.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- d = (mean1 - mean2) / pooled SD; d=0.2 small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large by convention.
- Unit-free, so comparable across studies and measures.
- Sensitive to design and variance definitions.
- Standard currency of meta-analysis.
