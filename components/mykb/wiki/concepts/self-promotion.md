# Self-Promotion

## Summary
Self-promotion is editors using the wiki to promote their own products, projects, or writing.

## Details
- It is a conflict-of-interest case: the editor's judgment is structurally compromised, even when the material is good.
- The wiki's rule is transparency: self-promotion is disclosed or removed, and genuinely notable self-authored topics go through the normal notability review.
- For mykb, self-promotion is handled under coi-editing rules and the promotional-content policy.
