# Wiki Tasks

## Summary
Wiki tasks are work items the wiki itself generates — flagged articles, broken links, review due dates — as opposed to requests from readers.

## Details
- Self-generated tasks are the engine of sustainability: the wiki's own checks produce a backlog that keeps it healthy.
- The distinction matters for prioritization: system tasks are usually more urgent than feature requests.
- For mykb, wiki tasks are generated by the health checks and routed into maintenance-tasks.
