# Sustained Attention

## Summary

Sustained Attention — Maintaining focus on a task over extended periods.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Sustained attention supports long tasks: monitoring, listening, studying.
- Performance declines over time unless the task is engaging.
- Mind-wandering marks lapses in sustained attention.
- Links to vigilance-decrement and to flow state.
