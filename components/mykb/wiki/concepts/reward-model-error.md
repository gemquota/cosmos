# Reward Model Error

## Summary
Reward model error is the gap between what a reward model scores and what humans actually prefer.

## Details
- Reward model error is the gap between what a reward model scores and what humans actually prefer.
- Error concentrates on rare, adversarial, or out-of-distribution inputs — exactly where deployment happens.
- Calibration and uncertainty estimates help bound the risk from reward error.
- RSIS3 relevance: check results are trusted only within their tested domain.
