# AI Fingerprinting

## Summary
AI fingerprinting identifies which model generated a piece of content, via statistical patterns or embedded marks.

## Details
- AI fingerprinting identifies which model generated a piece of content, via statistical patterns or embedded marks.
- It supports attribution, copyright, and abuse investigations.
- Accuracy varies; strong fingerprints are hard to remove.
- RSIS3 relevance: fingerprinting generated notes aids provenance audits.
