# Bayesian Updating Practice

## Summary

Bayesian Updating Practice — Applying Bayes' rule to revise beliefs with new evidence.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Posterior ∝ likelihood × prior; updating is iterative and principled.
- Practice: set priors, collect data, compute or approximate posteriors.
- Checks: sensitivity to prior, likelihood correctness, and calibration.
- Foundation for belief-updating and forecasting systems.
