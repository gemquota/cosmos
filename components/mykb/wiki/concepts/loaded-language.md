# Loaded Language

## Summary
Loaded language is wording that smuggles a judgment into a factual claim — 'notorious', 'claim', 'merely' — slanting the reader without an argument.

## Details
- Loaded terms are context-dependent: a word can be neutral in one sentence and loaded in another.
- Detection is partly wordlist (flag known terms) and partly human (does this sentence actually judge?).
- For mykb, loaded language is flagged by prose-linting and reviewed under neutral-language conventions.
