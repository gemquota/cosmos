# Reverts

## Summary
A revert undoes an edit, restoring the article to its previous revision — the standard response to bad or contested changes.

## Details
- Reverting is cheap and powerful, which is exactly why it needs discipline: a revert war is worse than the original mistake.
- The convention is to revert with a reason and, for good-faith edits, to explain and discuss rather than silently undo.
- For mykb, reverts are tracked in the recent-changes feed and edit-warring is handled by the dispute process.
