# Confidence Intervals

## Summary

Confidence Intervals — Range estimates that quantify sampling uncertainty.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- A 95% CI is a procedure property: repeated sampling covers the parameter 95% of the time.
- Wider intervals mean less precision, not less truth.
- Intervals carry more information than p-values.
- Visualized as error bars; misreading is common.
