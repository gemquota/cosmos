# Bounded Optimization

## Summary
Bounded optimization restricts an optimizer by budget, impact caps, or action sets, trading peak performance for safety and tractability.

## Details
- Bounded optimization restricts an optimizer by budget, impact caps, or action sets, trading peak performance for safety and tractability.
