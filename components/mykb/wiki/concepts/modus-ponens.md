# Modus Ponens

## Summary

Modus Ponens — Valid inference: if P then Q; P; therefore Q.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- The most natural and most endorsed deductive pattern.
- Errors are rare unless content or pragmatics interfere.
- Formal basis of forward-chaining rule systems.
- Contrast with affirming the consequent, its invalid neighbor.
