# First-Paragraph Links

## Summary
First-paragraph links put the article's most important relationships in the opening prose, where readers and search both look first.

## Details
- A strong first paragraph links the concept to its parent and its key contrast — the two neighbors that frame it.
- The convention forces authors to decide early what the article is about, which improves both links and prose.
- For mykb, first-paragraph links are encouraged for full articles and count toward the link-score.
