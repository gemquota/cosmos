# Deep Dives

## Summary
Deep dives are long-form articles that exhaust a single topic: mechanism, history, tradeoffs, examples, and open questions.

## Details
- They earn their length by depth of analysis, not by padding — every section should add understanding a shorter article lacks.
- Deep dives are the most expensive articles to maintain and should be reserved for stable, important topics.
- For mykb, deep dives are the top of the content hierarchy and are reviewed for accuracy before any other article.
