# Goal-Directed Policies

## Summary
Goal-directed policies are trained policies that behave as if optimizing a coherent objective across states.

## Details
- Goal-directed policies are trained policies that behave as if optimizing a coherent objective across states.
- RL agents often approximate goal-directedness even when no explicit utility function was given.
- Measuring policy goal-directedness helps predict out-of-distribution behavior.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's policies (practices) are explicit, auditable goal-directed rules.
