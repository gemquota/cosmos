# AGI Definitions

## Summary
AGI definitions range from 'human-level on all cognitive tasks' to flexible cross-domain competence exceeding humans. The 2023 'Levels of AGI' paper proposed a performance/generality grid to replace one-dimensional definitions, and definitions remain contested because they set policy triggers.

## Details
- **Families** — human-relative definitions, task-span definitions, and economic definitions (transformative AI).
- **Levels proposal** — a grid from 'emerging' to 'superhuman' by generality (narrow/general/super) and performance (Level 0-5).
- **Why contested** — definitions gate regulation (EU AI Act thresholds), forecasting, and funding narratives.
- **Measurement problem** — benchmarks measure tasks, not generality; capability evals proxy the rest.
- **RSIS3 angle** — the bundle avoids AGI grandstanding; it tracks concrete, measurable capability growth in its own systems.
