# Portal Pages

## Summary
Portal pages are large entry pages for a whole domain — curated collections of topic pages, key articles, and resources arranged for a reader exploring a field.

## Details
- Portals are rarer than hubs: a wiki needs one per major domain, not one per cluster.
- Their size makes them expensive to maintain, so portal content is assembled from topic pages rather than written from scratch.
- For mykb, portals are planned during acquisition waves when a domain reaches critical mass.
