# Autonomy Evals

## Summary
Autonomy evals measure an agent's ability to plan and execute multi-step tasks without human help.

## Details
- Autonomy evals measure an agent's ability to plan and execute multi-step tasks without human help.
- High autonomy is a capability and a risk multiplier.
- Tasks range from research to long-horizon operations.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's autonomy is deliberately gated by approvals and checks.
