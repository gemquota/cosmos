# Dormant Articles

## Summary
Dormant articles are pages untouched and unreviewed for a long period — not necessarily stale, but unexamined.

## Details
- Dormancy is a risk signal, not a verdict: an evergreen article can be correctly dormant for years.
- The dormant report is the input to the archival review — dormancy plus decay suggests archival.
- For mykb, dormant articles are surfaced by the freshness report and triaged into review or archive.
