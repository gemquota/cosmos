# Decision Journal

## Summary

Decision Journal — Log of decisions with context, options, expectations, and outcomes.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Record the decision, alternatives, reasoning, and expected outcomes before results.
- Review later to separate luck from skill.
- Improves calibration and reduces hindsight bias.
- A core tool of decision hygiene.
