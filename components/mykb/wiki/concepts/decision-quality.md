# Decision Quality

## Summary

Decision Quality — Evaluating decisions by process and information, not just outcomes.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Outcomes are noisy; quality means good process given what was knowable.
- Components: frame, alternatives, information, values, and reasoning.
- Decision journals enable retrospective quality review.
- Central to decision hygiene and debiasing.
