# Virtue Ethics for AI

## Summary
Virtue-ethical AI asks what dispositions a system should embody: honesty, care, temperance, and reliability.

## Details
- Virtue-ethical AI asks what dispositions a system should embody: honesty, care, temperance, and reliability.
- It complements rule- and consequence-based ethics by targeting character rather than acts.
- Operationalizing virtues into training objectives is difficult but increasingly explored.
- RSIS3 relevance: hha-standards (helpful, honest, harmless) are a virtue list.
