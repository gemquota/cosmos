# Tail Risks

## Summary
Tail risks are outcomes in the far tails of probability distributions: rare and severe.

## Details
- Tail risks are outcomes in the far tails of probability distributions: rare and severe.
- AI capability and harm distributions are plausibly fat-tailed, making tail events more likely than Gaussian intuition suggests.
- Tail-risk analysis changes decision rules (expected value, worst-case reasoning).
- RSIS3 relevance: the wiki's risk pages reason in tails, not averages.
