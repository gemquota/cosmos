# Explainers

## Summary
Explainers make a concept understandable: how it works, why it exists, what it trades off — with analogy, mechanism, and example.

## Details
- The success criterion is reader comprehension, not completeness; a short explainer that lands beats a long one that wanders.
- Explainers pair with how-tos: understanding (explainer) and doing (how-to) reinforce each other and should link.
- For mykb, explainers are the default format for full articles about concepts, with comprehension-score as their quality signal.
