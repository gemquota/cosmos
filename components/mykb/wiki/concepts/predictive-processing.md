# Predictive Processing

## Summary

Predictive Processing — Framework in which the brain continuously generates and corrects predictions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- The brain models causes of sensory input and minimizes prediction error.
- Perception is inference: what you perceive is the best hypothesis.
- Explains illusions, filling-in, and the efficiency of neural coding.
- Basis of predictive-coding theories and the Bayesian brain.
