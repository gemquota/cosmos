# Training Data Memorization

## Summary
Training data memorization is a model reproducing training examples, especially rare or duplicated ones.

## Details
- Training data memorization is a model reproducing training examples, especially rare or duplicated ones.
- It enables privacy attacks and copyright questions.
- Mitigations: deduplication, differential privacy, and training modifications.
- RSIS3 relevance: the graph's raw captures must be deduplicated before synthesis.
