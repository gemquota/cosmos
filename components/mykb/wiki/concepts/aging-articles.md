# Aging Articles

## Summary
Aging articles are pages whose freshness signals are declining — review dates passing, sources aging — but that are not yet stale.

## Details
- Aging is the window where refresh is cheapest: catch the article before its claims fully rot.
- The aging report is a prediction, powered by decay functions and half-life estimates.
- For mykb, aging articles are scheduled for refresh-days before they become stale.
