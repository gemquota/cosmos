# Mechanistic Locality

## Summary
Mechanistic locality is the empirical finding that behaviors are usually implemented by a small fraction of a model's components.

## Details
- Mechanistic locality is the empirical finding that behaviors are usually implemented by a small fraction of a model's components.
- It makes circuit analysis tractable: you can find the needle without reading the haystack.
- Counterexamples exist; locality is a claim to verify per behavior, not an axiom.
- RSIS3 relevance: locality in the graph means few links drive a synthesis; traceable and auditable.
