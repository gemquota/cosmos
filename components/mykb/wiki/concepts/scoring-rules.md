# Scoring Rules

## Summary

Scoring Rules — Functions that reward accurate probabilistic forecasts.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Proper rules (Brier, log) make honesty optimal: best expected score by reporting true belief.
- Log score is local and unbounded; Brier is bounded and decomposable.
- Choice of rule shapes risk attitude of the forecaster.
- Backbone of prediction-market and forecasting-tournament design.
