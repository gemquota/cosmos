# POV Practice

## Summary
POV practice is the writing discipline of keeping personal opinions and preferences out of articles — attribution over assertion, evidence over enthusiasm.

## Details
- It is practiced in word choice: loaded adjectives, weasel words, and peacock terms are the telltale leaks of a point of view.
- POV discipline is strongest in contested topics and weakest in comfortable ones, which is exactly where review matters.
- For mykb, POV practice is applied at promotion review and enforced by neutral-language checks.
