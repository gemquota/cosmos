# Satellite Articles

## Summary
Satellite articles orbit a hub — linked from it, but only loosely connected to the core cluster.

## Details
- Satellites are often the first attempt at a topic: a small page that links into a bigger area without committing to it.
- A satellite either integrates (gets linked back, gains siblings) or stays peripheral; both outcomes are fine if deliberate.
- For mykb, satellite articles are monitored by the orphan and one-way-link reports.
