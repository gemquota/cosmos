# AI Brittleness

## Summary
Brittleness is a system's tendency to fail catastrophically on slightly different inputs or contexts.

## Details
- Brittleness is a system's tendency to fail catastrophically on slightly different inputs or contexts.
- It is the practical face of distribution shift and shortcut learning.
- Robustness training and OOD evals are the standard mitigations.
- RSIS3 relevance: knowledge tools must degrade gracefully when queries leave the graph's coverage.
