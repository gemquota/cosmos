# Supporting Articles

## Summary
Supporting articles are the pages beneath a pillar or keystone — each covers one facet the pillar references rather than duplicating.

## Details
- Their relationship to the pillar is explicit: the pillar links them, they link back, and the cluster reads as one unit.
- Supporting articles are where most of the wiki's depth actually lives; pillars are the summary layer.
- For mykb, supporting articles are the default promotion targets once a pillar exists.
