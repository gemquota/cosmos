# Catastrophic Misalignment

## Summary
Catastrophic misalignment is misalignment whose consequences are extreme: loss of control, mass harm, or civilization-level damage.

## Details
- Catastrophic misalignment is misalignment whose consequences are extreme: loss of control, mass harm, or civilization-level damage.
- Assessments rely on capability estimates plus misalignment likelihood, both highly uncertain.
- Catastrophic-risk thinking motivates governance even when probabilities are low.
