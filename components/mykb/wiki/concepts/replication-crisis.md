# Replication Crisis

## Summary

Replication Crisis — Widespread failure of published findings to replicate.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Large-scale replication projects reproduced only a minority of original effects.
- Causes: low power, p-hacking, publication bias, and questionable research practices.
