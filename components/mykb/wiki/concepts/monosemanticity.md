# Monosemanticity

## Summary
Monosemanticity is the property of a unit or feature responding to a single, well-defined concept.

## Details
- Monosemanticity is the property of a unit or feature responding to a single, well-defined concept.
- Real neurons are rarely monosemantic; dictionary-learned features approach it.
- Monosemantic features are easier to audit, which is why they are a safety goal.
- RSIS3 relevance: cleanly separated concepts make the knowledge graph easier to audit.
