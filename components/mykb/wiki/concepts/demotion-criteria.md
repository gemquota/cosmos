# Demotion Criteria

## Summary
Demotion criteria describe when an article loses growing status: it can no longer be verified, its sources rot, or its claims become unsupported by current evidence.

## Details
- Demotion is not punishment — it is honesty about the article's current state, and it routes the article back into the promotion queue once fixed.
- Automatic demotion triggers include link-rot, flagged factual errors, and failed freshness reviews.
- For mykb, demotion keeps full-article-ratio honest: a stale full article is worse than an honest stub.
