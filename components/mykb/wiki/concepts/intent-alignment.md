# Intent Alignment

## Summary
Intent alignment is the property that a system's objectives match what the designer actually wanted, not just the written spec.

## Details
- Intent alignment is the property that a system's objectives match what the designer actually wanted, not just the written spec.
- It fails via specification gaming, goal drift, and value misspecification.
- Measuring intent alignment requires comparing behavior against intent, not just metrics.
- RSIS3 relevance: the practices document is the written intent; the checker verifies compliance.
