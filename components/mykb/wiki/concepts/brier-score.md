# Brier Score

## Summary

Brier Score — Mean squared error of probabilistic forecasts.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Brier = mean((forecast - outcome)^2) over forecasts.
- Lower is better; decomposes into calibration and resolution.
- Applies to any binary or categorical probability judgment.
- Standard metric in forecasting tournaments.
