# Protected Articles

## Summary
Protected articles require elevated trust to edit — usually keystones and policy pages where bad edits are expensive.

## Details
- Protection levels form a ladder: unprotected, semi-protected (autoconfirmed only), and fully protected.
- Protection is a response to risk, not a status symbol; it should be reviewed as the risk changes.
- For mykb, protected articles are a small set and their protection is logged with the reason.
