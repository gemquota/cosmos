# Editorial Policy

## Summary
Editorial policy is the rulebook for how the wiki is edited: who may edit what, how disputes resolve, and what happens to rule-breakers.

## Details
- It sits above the style guide: style governs how articles look, editorial policy governs how the community behaves.
- Policy must be written, public, and changeable through a defined process — unwritten policy is just power.
- For mykb, editorial policy is the umbrella under content policy, notability policy, and the dispute process.
