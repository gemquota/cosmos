# C2PA

## Summary
C2PA is an open standard for cryptographically signed provenance metadata attached to digital content.

## Details
- C2PA is an open standard for cryptographically signed provenance metadata attached to digital content.
- It records actor, actions, and assets in a tamper-evident chain.
- Adoption is growing across cameras, platforms, and AI tools.
- RSIS3 relevance: provenance-aware publishing aligns with the bundle's evidence practices.
