# Citation Needed

## Summary
Citation-needed marks a claim that requires a source and does not have one, turning an invisible gap into a visible, queueable item.

## Details
- The flag is a contract: the claim stays, the obligation to source it is recorded, and the flag is cleared only by adding the citation.
- Flags accumulate into a report that prioritizes which articles need source work first.
- For mykb, citation-needed flags feed the sources-needed queue and the verifiability-score.
