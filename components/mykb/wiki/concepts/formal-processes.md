# Formal Processes

## Summary
Formal processes are the codified procedures for consequential decisions — policy changes, disputes, bans, and structural actions — with defined steps and records.

## Details
- Their strength is predictability: everyone knows the path, the evidence required, and the appeal route.
- Formality has a cost in speed, so the wiki reserves formal process for decisions where reversibility is low.
- For mykb, formal processes are the written form of the editorial policy's enforcement half.
