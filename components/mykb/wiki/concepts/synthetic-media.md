# Synthetic Media

## Summary
Synthetic media is text, image, audio, or video produced or altered by generative AI.

## Details
- Synthetic media is text, image, audio, or video produced or altered by generative AI.
- It spans entertainment (legitimate) to deception (harmful).
- Labels and provenance distinguish synthetic from authentic.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle distinguishes generated notes from human captures.
