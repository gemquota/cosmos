# Conditional Reasoning

## Summary

Conditional Reasoning — Inference with if-then statements and their variants.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Core patterns: modus ponens (valid) and affirming the consequent (invalid).
- People endorse invalid forms readily when content is plausible.
- Abstract vs concrete content changes performance dramatically.
- Underlies the Wason selection task and rule-learning research.
