# Publication Bias

## Summary

Publication Bias — Preferential publication of significant or positive results.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- File-drawer problem: null results stay unpublished, inflating apparent effects.
- Detected via funnel plots and p-curve analysis.
