# Fat-Tailed Distributions

## Summary
Fat-tailed distributions assign meaningful probability to extreme events, unlike Gaussians.

## Details
- Fat-tailed distributions assign meaningful probability to extreme events, unlike Gaussians.
- AI capability growth and downside risks are widely argued to be fat-tailed.
- Fat tails undermine point forecasts and demand robust, precautionary decisions.
