# Verifiability Policy

## Summary
Verifiability policy requires that claims readers are expected to trust can be checked against cited sources.

## Details
- It is the wiki's evidence contract: surprising claims need sources, sources need to be real, and the match needs to hold.
- Verifiability is the floor under accuracy — a wiki cannot be accurate if its claims cannot be checked.
- For mykb, verifiability policy is enforced by the citation and verification workflow and scored by the verifiability-score.
