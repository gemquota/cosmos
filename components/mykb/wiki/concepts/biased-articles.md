# Biased Articles

## Summary
Biased articles present a subject in a way that unfairly favors one side — through selection, framing, or word choice.

## Details
- Bias is often structural: which facts are included and which are omitted is itself a point of view.
- Fixing bias requires the article to represent the other sides fairly, not to strip all opinions into mush.
- For mykb, biased articles are flagged at review, tagged needs-review, and reworked under the neutral-language conventions.
