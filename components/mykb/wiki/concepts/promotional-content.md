# Promotional Content

## Summary
Promotional content is material written to sell — a product, service, or agenda — rather than to inform, even when factually true.

## Details
- It is the article-level form of puffery and the main reason advertising and editorial must be separated.
- Promotional content is removed or rewritten into neutral, sourced coverage; if the topic is genuinely notable, the article survives the rewrite.
- For mykb, promotional content fails the neutral-language and verifiability bars at promotion review.
