# Model-Based Cognition

## Summary

Model-Based Cognition — Thinking that uses internal models of the world for prediction and planning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Internal models simulate outcomes before acting.
- Enable planning, counterfactuals, and mental simulation.
- Costlier than cached habits but flexible under change.
- Ranges from physics intuition to social models of others.
