# Global Catastrophic Risk

## Summary
Global catastrophic risk (GCR) is risk of harm severe enough to degrade civilization worldwide but not necessarily end it.

## Details
- Global catastrophic risk (GCR) is risk of harm severe enough to degrade civilization worldwide but not necessarily end it.
- GCR research covers pandemics, nuclear war, climate tipping points, and AI.
- AI's GCR contribution is through misuse, accidents, and destabilizing races.
- RSIS3 relevance: the wiki's risk pages link AI risk to the wider GCR field.
