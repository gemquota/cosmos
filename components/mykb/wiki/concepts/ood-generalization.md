# OOD Generalization

## Summary
OOD generalization measures performance on inputs outside the training distribution.

## Details
- OOD generalization measures performance on inputs outside the training distribution.
- It is the deepest open problem in applied ML and the crux of deployment safety.
- Benchmarks like domain-shift suites estimate it under controlled variation.
- RSIS3 relevance: generalization of the graph's retrieval to novel queries is continuously tested.
