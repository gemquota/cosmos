# Decision Guides

## Summary
Decision guides walk a reader through a real choice: the decision context, the criteria, the options, and a recommended path with reasoning.

## Details
- They convert a comparison into an action — the difference between 'here are the options' and 'here is how to choose'.
- A decision guide must state its assumptions so readers whose context differs can adapt the logic.
- For mykb, decision guides are high-value articles that are re-verified on freshness review because choices age.
