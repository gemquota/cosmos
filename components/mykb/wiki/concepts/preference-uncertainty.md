# Preference Uncertainty

## Summary
Preference uncertainty is the agent's uncertainty about human preferences, from ambiguous feedback and contested values.

## Details
- Preference uncertainty is the agent's uncertainty about human preferences, from ambiguous feedback and contested values.
- Agents that model this uncertainty are more cautious and more corrigible.
- Preference learning should output distributions, not point estimates.
- RSIS3 relevance: practices evolve as the workspace's preferences are clarified.
