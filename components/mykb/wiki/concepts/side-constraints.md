# Side Constraints

## Summary
Side constraints are hard limits an optimizer must respect regardless of objective gains: no harm to bystanders, no rights violations.

## Details
- Side constraints are hard limits an optimizer must respect regardless of objective gains: no harm to bystanders, no rights violations.
- They convert deontological rules into optimization constraints.
- Constraint violations are the classic failure when proxies miss side effects.
- RSIS3 relevance: scope discipline (only your files) is a side constraint on workers.
