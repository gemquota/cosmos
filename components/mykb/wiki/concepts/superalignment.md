# Superalignment

## Summary
Superalignment is the program of solving alignment for superhuman AI before it exists, using today's models as research instruments. OpenAI's 2023 agenda centers on weak-to-strong generalization, scalable oversight, and interpretability as the three pillars.

## Details
- **Motivation** — aligning systems smarter than humans cannot rely on human evaluation alone.
- **Pillars** — weak-to-strong generalization (can weak supervisors steer strong models), scalable oversight (AI-assisted review), and mechanistic interpretability (reading the model's circuits).
- **Controversy** — critics note the agenda's techniques are unproven at frontier scale and that governance cannot be reduced to research.
- **Relation to RSI** — superalignment assumes a fast capability race, making recursive self-improvement safety urgent.
- **RSIS3 framing** — the triad's layered checks (practices, tests, rollback) are a miniature, human-scale superalignment stack.
