# Summary Pages

## Summary
Summary pages condense a topic to its essential content — definitions, key facts, and pointers — for readers who want the answer, not the essay.

## Details
- They are distinct from article Summaries: a summary page is a standalone article whose whole job is compression.
- Summary pages must link to the full treatment so readers can go deeper when the summary is not enough.
- For mykb, summary pages serve as digest-pages and are a natural format for recurring reference topics.
