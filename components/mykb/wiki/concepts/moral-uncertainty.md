# Moral Uncertainty

## Summary
Moral uncertainty is the epistemic state of being unsure which moral theory or value system is right.

## Details
- Moral uncertainty is the epistemic state of being unsure which moral theory or value system is right.
- Decision theories under moral uncertainty weigh actions across candidate theories rather than picking one.
- It is increasingly studied as a real alignment input, not just philosophy.
- RSIS3 relevance: contested practices are handled with explicit uncertainty, not false certainty.
