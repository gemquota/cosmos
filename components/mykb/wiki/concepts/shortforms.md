# Shortforms

## Summary
Shortforms are articles deliberately held to a compact size — one idea, one answer, one reference — in contrast to longforms.

## Details
- Short is a design choice: a shortform does less so it does it crisply, and it links out for depth.
- Shortforms are cheap to maintain, which makes them the default for fast-moving topics.
- For mykb, shortforms sit between stubs and fulls in effort, and many promote into full articles as they grow.
