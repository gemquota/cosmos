# Nano Articles

## Summary
Nano articles are the smallest page: a term, a definition, and a link or two — smaller than a micro article.

## Details
- They serve as link targets: a nano article exists so other pages can link to a named thing without a broken path.
- Nanos are candidates for glossary entries — if a term is only a definition, a glossary may serve better than a page.
- For mykb, nano articles are rare and usually promote quickly or get merged into glossaries.
