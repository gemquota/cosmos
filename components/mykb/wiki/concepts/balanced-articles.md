# Balanced Articles

## Summary
Balanced articles represent the significant views on their subject fairly and proportionately, with each position attributed and weighted by its actual standing.

## Details
- Balance is not 50/50 — a fringe view gets a mention and a rebuttal, not equal billing with the mainstream.
- Balance is judged by the article's coverage: what is included, what is excluded, and how each is framed.
- For mykb, balanced articles are the target state for contested technical topics and are reviewed under NPOV policy.
