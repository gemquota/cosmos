# Umbrella Articles

## Summary
Umbrella articles cover a family of related concepts, providing the shared framing and the links to each member.

## Details
- They are the parent in the parent-child hierarchy: the umbrella gives the map, the children give the detail.
- Umbrellas fail when they absorb their children's content instead of linking it — the umbrella should summarize, not duplicate.
- For mykb, umbrella articles are a natural fit for topic pages and category pages.
