# Norm Setting

## Summary
Norm setting is how the wiki's unwritten rules form: repeated practice becomes expectation, expectation becomes convention, and contested convention becomes policy.

## Details
- Norms are the cheapest governance there is — they require no process, only consistency — and the most fragile, because they rely on memory.
- Norm change happens by practice and by discussion: new behavior repeated and accepted becomes the new norm.
- For mykb, norm setting is the informal layer beneath formal processes, and curation sprints are where many norms actually form.
