# Overview Pages

## Summary
Overview pages give the shape of a broad area — its parts and how they fit — before the reader dives into details.

## Details
- They are synthesis-first: an overview asserts a mental model, and its value is the model, not the coverage.
- Overview pages degrade when they grow into link dumps; the fix is splitting details into child articles.
- For mykb, overview pages are the natural parents in the parent-child hierarchy and pair with summary pages.
