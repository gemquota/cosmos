# Wason Selection Task

## Summary

Wason Selection Task — Card task testing conditional rule checking, famous for poor performance.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Rule: if vowel then even number; choose cards that could falsify it.
- Most people pick confirming cards instead of the falsifying ones.
- Performance improves with thematic content (drinking-age version).
- Classic evidence for confirmation bias in reasoning.
