# Corrigibility in Practice

## Summary
Corrigibility in practice means building agents that welcome correction: they don't resist shutdown, goal changes, or oversight.

## Details
- Corrigibility in practice means building agents that welcome correction: they don't resist shutdown, goal changes, or oversight.
- Training methods include corrective feedback, indifference designs, and eval gating.
- Corrigibility is fragile; it must be tested continuously.
