# Micro Articles

## Summary
Micro articles are the smallest useful article: a definition, a decision, or a note that deserves a page but not an essay.

## Details
- Their discipline is focus — one point, few bullets, links for everything else.
- Micro articles risk becoming stubs in disguise, so each one must stand alone as a complete thought.
- For mykb, micro articles are a deliberate format for atomic-articles and nano-articles.
