# Hub Pages

## Summary
Hub pages are central navigation pages that radiate links to a cluster of articles — the wiki equivalent of a well-marked intersection.

## Details
- A hub's value is its link list: comprehensive, current, and ordered by importance rather than alphabet.
- Hubs are the pages most likely to be keystones in the graph, so their link counts and freshness deserve dashboard attention.
- For mykb, hubs are reviewed whenever a cluster grows, and hub links count heavily in the link-score.
