# Distributional Robustness

## Summary
Distributional robustness aims for acceptable performance across a defined family of shifted distributions, not just the training one.

## Details
- Distributional robustness aims for acceptable performance across a defined family of shifted distributions, not just the training one.
- Robust optimization methods formalize worst-case guarantees.
- Guarantees are only as good as the distribution family considered.
