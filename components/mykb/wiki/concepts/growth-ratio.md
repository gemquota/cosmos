# Growth Ratio

## Summary
Growth ratio measures new articles over a window divided by the total, normalizing raw counts so a ten-article wiki and a thousand-article wiki are comparable.

## Details
- Fast growth without promotion is just stub accumulation; the ratio should be read against stub-ratio to see whether growth is depth or width.
- Sustained positive growth indicates the acquisition pipeline is working; a plateau invites a maintenance pass instead of more capture.
- For mykb, growth-ratio and stub-ratio together tell the curation story of each pass: capture surges then promotion catches up.
