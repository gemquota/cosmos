# Vigilance Decrement

## Summary

Vigilance Decrement — Declining detection performance over time in monitoring tasks.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Classic in radar monitoring studies: hits fall and response time rises over watch.
- Decrement is steeper for low-salience, low-frequency targets.
- Countermeasures: breaks, task variety, and signal enhancement.
- Applies to QA, review work, and system monitoring.
