# Dated Claims

## Summary
A dated claim carries its own as-of date — 'as of 2026', 'since v4.2' — so readers can judge its currency without archaeology.

## Details
- Dating claims is the cheapest freshness mechanism there is: it moves the burden from curator to reader and makes review scheduling mechanical.
- Undated claims are the risk: an old fact with no date reads as a current fact.
- For mykb, dated claims are encouraged in time-sensitive sections and feed the dated-claims review report.
