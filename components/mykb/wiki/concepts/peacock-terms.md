# Peacock Terms

## Summary
Peacock terms are overblown adjectives — 'revolutionary', 'world-class', 'cutting-edge' — that praise a subject without evidence.

## Details
- They are the promotional cousin of weasel words: both substitute vibe for verification.
- The fix is to cut the adjective or replace it with the fact that earns it.
- For mykb, peacock terms are flagged by prose-linting and treated as puffery in review.
