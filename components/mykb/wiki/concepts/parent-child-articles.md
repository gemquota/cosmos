# Parent-Child Articles

## Summary
Parent-child articles form the wiki's hierarchy: a parent (topic, umbrella, overview) links down to child articles that each cover a narrower piece.

## Details
- The relationship is navigational and semantic — a child should be genuinely part of the parent's scope, not merely adjacent.
- Hierarchies keep the graph navigable without forcing every page to be self-contained.
- For mykb, parent-child structure is explicit in topic pages and reviewed when clusters grow.
