# Keystone Articles

## Summary
Keystone articles are the highly-linked pages that hold up a region of the graph — remove one and many clusters lose their connective tissue.

## Details
- They deserve priority maintenance: freshness, accuracy, and link quality on a keystone matter more than on a peripheral page.
- Keystones are identified by incoming-link-counts and centrality metrics, then confirmed by human judgment.
- For mykb, keystone articles get protection status and are first in line for review sprints.
