# Satisficing

## Summary
Satisficing stops search once an option meets a minimum aspiration level, instead of seeking the optimum. It matters because it is the rational response to bounded rationality: perfect choices are expensive. Agent budgets and stop conditions are satisficing in practice.

## Details
- Aspiration levels encode what 'good enough' means per task.
- Trades optimality for speed, robustness, and simplicity.
- RSIS3 relevance: success criteria define aspiration levels for tasks.
- Open questions: adjusting aspiration dynamically.
