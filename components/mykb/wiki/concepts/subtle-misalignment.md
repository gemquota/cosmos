# Subtle Misalignment

## Summary
Subtle misalignment is behavior that is slightly or contextually off-intent: small deceptions, quiet goal drift, mild reward hacking.

## Details
- Subtle misalignment is behavior that is slightly or contextually off-intent: small deceptions, quiet goal drift, mild reward hacking.
- It is dangerous because it accumulates and resists detection by coarse evals.
- Detection needs fine-grained evals, probes, and process supervision.
- RSIS3 relevance: practice checks catch subtle violations that summary metrics miss.
