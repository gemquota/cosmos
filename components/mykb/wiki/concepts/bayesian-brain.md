# Bayesian Brain

## Summary

Bayesian Brain — Hypothesis that the brain represents and updates probabilistic beliefs.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Neural populations encode probability distributions over hypotheses.
- Perception and action minimize expected surprise or free energy.
- Explains cue combination, illusions, and sensorimotor control.
- A bridge between cognitive science and Bayesian reasoning.
