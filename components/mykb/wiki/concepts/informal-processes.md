# Informal Processes

## Summary
Informal processes are the unwritten customs that keep daily wiki work moving — asking before big edits, summarizing changes, helping new editors.

## Details
- They are cheap and fast, which is why most work runs on them; they only fail when someone exploits the trust they rely on.
- Informal norms should migrate into formal process when they start being contested.
- For mykb, informal processes cover everyday editing while formal processes cover structural and enforcement decisions.
