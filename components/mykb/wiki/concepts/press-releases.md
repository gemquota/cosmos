# Press Releases

## Summary
Press releases are publicity documents written by the subject itself; they are primary sources for what the subject claims and unreliable sources for how the world sees it.

## Details
- Citing a press release is legitimate for 'the company announced X' and illegitimate for 'X is the best' — the claim is the announcement, not the evaluation.
- Curation treats press releases as attribution material: name the source of the claim and let readers weigh it.
- For mykb, press-release citations pass source-vetting only when used as attributed announcements.
