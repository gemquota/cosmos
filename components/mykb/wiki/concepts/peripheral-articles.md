# Peripheral Articles

## Summary
Peripheral articles sit at the edge of a cluster — related but not central, linked lightly, and lower in the maintenance priority order.

## Details
- Periphery is a position, not a verdict: peripheral articles can be excellent and can grow into core members.
- Peripheral articles are the first candidates for archival or merging when a cluster is pruned.
- For mykb, peripheral status is derived from link counts and is reported so curators can decide whether to strengthen or retire the edge.
