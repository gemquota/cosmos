# Half-Life of Knowledge

## Summary
The half-life of knowledge is the period after which a fact or article is half as likely to still be accurate — the concept's decay timescale.

## Details
- Estimating half-lives per topic makes review scheduling rational: review twice per half-life, not annually for everything.
- Half-lives are estimates and should be reviewed themselves; a fast-moving topic's half-life shrinks over time.
- For mykb, half-life estimates parameterize the decay functions behind the timeliness score.
