# Semi-Protected

## Summary
Semi-protection restricts edits to established editors — those past the autoconfirmed threshold — while allowing anyone to read and discuss.

## Details
- It is the middle rung of protection, used for pages with recurring vandalism or frequent low-quality edits.
- The threshold should be low enough to not gate legitimate contributors and high enough to filter the noise.
- For mykb, semi-protection is applied to high-traffic hub pages and reviewed with the protection log.
