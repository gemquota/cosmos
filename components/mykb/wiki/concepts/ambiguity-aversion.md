# Ambiguity Aversion

## Summary

Ambiguity Aversion — Preferring known risks over unknown probabilities (Ellsberg).
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Ellsberg paradox: people avoid options with unknown probabilities even when expected value matches.
- Reflects dislike of uncertainty about uncertainty.
