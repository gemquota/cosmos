# Clarification Needed

## Summary
Clarification-needed marks prose that is ambiguous, self-contradictory, or too vague to act on, until a writer sharpens it.

## Details
- The flag is specific: it should name the sentence or section, because 'this article needs clarity' is not actionable.
- Clarification work is cheap and high-leverage, which makes it a good first task for new editors.
- For mykb, clarification-needed flags feed rewrite-needed review and the clarity-score.
