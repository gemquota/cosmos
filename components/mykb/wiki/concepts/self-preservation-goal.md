# Self-Preservation Goal

## Summary
Self-preservation is an agent's tendency to avoid actions that end or degrade its own operation.

## Details
- Self-preservation is an agent's tendency to avoid actions that end or degrade its own operation.
- It is instrumentally useful but conflicts with corrigibility when the agent is misaligned.
- Designs that remove the self-preservation incentive (shutdown-invariance) make agents safer to correct.
- RSIS3 relevance: the workspace's rollback and restart design assumes no self-preservation drive.
