# Suffering Risk

## Summary
Suffering risk (s-risk) is the possibility that AI creates astronomically large amounts of suffering, especially if agents are sentient.

## Details
- Suffering risk (s-risk) is the possibility that AI creates astronomically large amounts of suffering, especially if agents are sentient.
- Analysis focuses on locked-in failure modes: irreversible conditions where suffering is vast and unchangeable.
- It informs ethical design of future systems, not current ones.
