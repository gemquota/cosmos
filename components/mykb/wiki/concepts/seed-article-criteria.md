# Seed Article Criteria

## Summary
Seed article criteria decide which new topics deserve a first article rather than just a capture in the inbox or a raw note.

## Details
- Typical tests: the topic is stable enough to write about, it recurs across sessions, it connects to existing articles, and a source exists to ground it.
- A seed that fails the tests is parked, not written — premature articles become stubs that nobody promotes.
- For mykb, seeding is the entry point of the acquisition workflow, and the seed list feeds growth-ratio by design.
