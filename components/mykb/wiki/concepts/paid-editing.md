# Paid Editing

## Summary
Paid editing is editing done for compensation — someone pays the editor to create or change articles.

## Details
- Paid editing is not inherently forbidden, but it carries a hard disclosure requirement and strict boundaries on what can be edited.
- Undisclosed paid editing is the violation: it hides the conflict and corrupts the reader's trust model.
- For mykb, paid editing is disclosed per the coi-editing rules and never applied to articles where the payment creates a direct stake.
