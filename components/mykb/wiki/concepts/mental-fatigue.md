# Mental Fatigue

## Summary

Mental Fatigue — Subjective and objective decline in cognitive performance from sustained effort.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Fatigue reduces vigilance, accuracy, and strategic flexibility.
- Recovery via rest and sleep; motivation can partially compensate short-term.
