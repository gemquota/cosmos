# Simplicity Bias

## Summary
Simplicity bias is the tendency of deep networks to favor simple, low-frequency features that fit the data.

## Details
- Simplicity bias is the tendency of deep networks to favor simple, low-frequency features that fit the data.
- It explains shortcut learning and memorization tradeoffs.
- Inductive biases are mostly a feature; they become bugs under distribution shift.
- RSIS3 relevance: minimal-link graph hypotheses can under-represent complex concepts.
