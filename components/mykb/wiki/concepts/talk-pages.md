# Talk Pages

## Summary
Talk pages are the discussion space attached to each article, where editors hash out disagreements before editing the page itself.

## Details
- The convention is to talk first for contested changes: propose, discuss, then edit with the discussion's conclusion.
- Talk pages need hygiene — sign, thread, close — or they become unreadable archives of indecision.
- For mykb, talk pages are used sparingly and their conclusions are recorded in edit summaries.
