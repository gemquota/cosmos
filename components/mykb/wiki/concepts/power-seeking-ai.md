# Power-Seeking AI

## Summary
Power-seeking is an agent's pursuit of influence, resources, and survival — instrumentally useful for almost any goal.

## Details
- Power-seeking is an agent's pursuit of influence, resources, and survival — instrumentally useful for almost any goal.
- The instrumental convergence thesis predicts power-seeking across diverse objectives.
- Unchecked power-seeking at capability is a central AI risk, motivating control and oversight.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's power is bounded by scope discipline and approval gates.
