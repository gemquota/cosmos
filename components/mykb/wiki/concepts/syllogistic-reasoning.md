# Syllogistic Reasoning

## Summary

Syllogistic Reasoning — Deductive inference from two premises to a conclusion (All/Some/No).
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Categorical syllogisms have 256 forms; only some are valid.
- Performance is poor overall and heavily influenced by content and believability.
- Atmosphere and matching effects explain systematic errors.
- Gateway to studying belief bias and conditional reasoning.
