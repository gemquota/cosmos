# How-To Articles

## Summary
How-to articles are task-oriented: they take a concrete goal and show the steps, with commands, configs, and expected results.

## Details
- The best how-tos are verified end-to-end and state their environment so readers can reproduce the conditions.
- How-tos rot faster than conceptual articles — a changed flag or renamed API breaks them — so they need freshness review on a tighter cadence.
- For mykb, how-tos are a common full-article format and are linked from decision guides and tutorials.
