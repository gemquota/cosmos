# Steelmanning

## Summary

Steelmanning — Arguing the strongest version of an opponent's position.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Steelmanning beats strawmanning: engage the best version of the view.
- Requires genuine understanding and restatement before critique.
- Improves both reasoning quality and dialogue climate.
- Complement to devil's advocate and argument mapping.
