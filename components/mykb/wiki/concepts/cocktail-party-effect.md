# Cocktail Party Effect

## Summary

Cocktail Party Effect — Ability to focus one voice amid noise and detect one's own name.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Selective auditory attention filters competing streams.
- High-priority items (one's name, novel words) break through the filter.
- Classic dichotic listening studies by Cherry and Moray.
- Motivated early-selection vs late-selection debates in attention.
