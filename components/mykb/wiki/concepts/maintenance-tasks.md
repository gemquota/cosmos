# Maintenance Tasks

## Summary
Maintenance tasks are the ongoing work of keeping the wiki itself healthy: link checks, source reviews, tag hygiene, archive runs, and dashboard updates.

## Details
- They are recurring by nature, which makes them perfect for scheduled days and sprints.
- Maintenance tasks are the least glamorous and most important work; skipping them compounds into broken-link and stale-source debt.
- For mykb, maintenance tasks are generated by the health dashboard and grouped into maintenance-days.
