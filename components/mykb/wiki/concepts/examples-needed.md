# Examples Needed

## Summary
Examples-needed marks articles whose claims are abstract but demonstrable — the concept needs a worked example to be usable.

## Details
- A missing example is a usability gap, not a correctness gap; the flag usually appears at promotion review.
- The flag note should say which part needs the example, so the add lands where readers stumble.
- For mykb, examples-needed flags are common on full articles about protocols and patterns, and they feed the content-score.
