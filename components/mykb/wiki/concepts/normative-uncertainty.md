# Normative Uncertainty

## Summary
Normative uncertainty covers doubt about ethical theories, decision criteria, and evaluative standards.

## Details
- Normative uncertainty covers doubt about ethical theories, decision criteria, and evaluative standards.
- It generalizes moral uncertainty to all normative domains (epistemic, aesthetic, practical).
- Handling it well requires decision rules that work across candidate standards.
- RSIS3 relevance: practice revisions treat normative questions as open until evidence accumulates.
