# Comparison Articles

## Summary
Comparison articles evaluate options on shared dimensions, using tables and criteria so the reader can see tradeoffs rather than a verdict.

## Details
- The comparison is only fair if the dimensions are chosen before the verdict — criteria-first writing prevents cherry-picking.
- A comparison article should name its audience's decision so the reader can tell whether it applies to them.
- For mykb, comparison articles are common in tooling clusters and benefit from tables-in-wiki conventions.
