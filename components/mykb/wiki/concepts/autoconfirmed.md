# Autoconfirmed

## Summary
Autoconfirmed is editor status earned automatically once an account passes an age-and-edit threshold, unlocking higher-trust actions.

## Details
- Automation removes judgment from the gate: the threshold is fixed, objective, and reviewed periodically.
- Autoconfirmation is a floor, not a badge — it filters casual noise, not sophisticated bad actors.
- For mykb, autoconfirmed status gates semi-protected pages and is recorded in contribution history.
