# Link Placement

## Summary
Link placement is the choice of where links sit: first paragraph, prose body, tables, or the Related block — each position carries different weight.

## Details
- Early placement (first paragraph, lead sentence) signals the most important relationships; late placement signals supporting material.
- Consistent placement rules make scanning predictable: readers learn where to find the cross-references.
- For mykb, placement conventions are part of the style guide, with first-paragraph links encouraged on full articles.
