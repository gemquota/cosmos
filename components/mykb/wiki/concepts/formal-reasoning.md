# Formal Reasoning

## Summary

Formal Reasoning — Reasoning with explicit rules, such as logic and mathematics.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Formal systems make validity independent of content.
- Includes propositional and predicate logic, probability, and math proof.
- Humans find formal rules hard to apply consistently (Wason task).
- Computers excel where humans struggle; human-computer hybrid is strongest.
