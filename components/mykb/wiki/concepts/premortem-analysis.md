# Premortem Analysis

## Summary

Premortem Analysis — Imagining a decision has already failed and explaining why.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Klein's premortem: assume failure happened and generate causes in advance.
- Lowers group overconfidence and surfaces risks before they bite.
- Works because it converts hindsight logic into foresight.
- Useful before large projects and irreversible commitments.
