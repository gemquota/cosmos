# Locked Articles

## Summary
Locked articles restrict editing to authorized editors, used for keystones, policy pages, and pages under active dispute.

## Details
- Locking protects stability but creates a bottleneck — a locked page with a slow owner becomes a stale page.
- Locks should be scoped and temporary by default; permanent locks are a governance decision.
- For mykb, locked articles are a small set — keystone and policy pages — and locks are reviewed periodically.
