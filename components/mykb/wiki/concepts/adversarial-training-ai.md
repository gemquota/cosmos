# Adversarial Training for AI

## Summary
Adversarial training exposes models to attacks during training so they learn to resist them.

## Details
- Adversarial training exposes models to attacks during training so they learn to resist them.
- It is the defensive twin of red teaming and the core of adversarial robustness work.
- Robustness rarely transfers to unseen attack types; diversity of attacks matters.
- RSIS3 relevance: practice hardening from check failures is adversarial training for the loop.
