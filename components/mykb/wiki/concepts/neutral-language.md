# Neutral Language

## Summary
Neutral language is word choice that presents facts without slant: verbs and adjectives that describe rather than judge.

## Details
- The practice is specific — 'argues', 'reports', and 'claims' attribute; 'proves', 'reveals', and 'admits' slant.
- Neutral language is a lintable surface: wordlists catch the worst offenders, but context still needs human judgment.
- For mykb, neutral language conventions are part of prose-linting and POV practice.
