# Kill Switch Design

## Summary
Kill switch design builds mechanisms that reliably stop an AI system: circuit breakers, isolation, and shutdown authority.

## Details
- Kill switch design builds mechanisms that reliably stop an AI system: circuit breakers, isolation, and shutdown authority.
- Kill switches must be simple, external, and tamper-resistant.
- A kill switch that can be disabled is not a kill switch.
- RSIS3 relevance: the checker's hard-fail behavior is a kill-switch pattern.
