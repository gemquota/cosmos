# Contribution History

## Summary
Contribution history is the record of everything an editor has done: edits, summaries, flags set, reviews performed.

## Details
- It is the basis of trust decisions — autoconfirmed status, protection thresholds, and reputation all read from it.
- Contribution history is also a review lens: a pattern of unexplained edits or repeated reverts is visible at a glance.
- For mykb, contribution history feeds the registered-editor and quality-team processes.
