# Precautionary Principle

## Summary
The precautionary principle says lack of certainty about harm is not grounds for inaction when harm could be severe.

## Details
- The precautionary principle says lack of certainty about harm is not grounds for inaction when harm could be severe.
- It underpins conservative AI, deployment gates, and biosafety norms.
- Critics warn it can block beneficial innovation; application must weigh both risks.
- RSIS3 relevance: conservative practices (check before change) are precautionary.
