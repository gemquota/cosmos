# Priority Labels

## Summary
Priority labels rank work items — P0 through P3 is a common scale — so the queue knows what to do first.

## Details
- Priority is about importance, distinct from severity (harm) and effort (cost), and the three must be tracked separately.
- Priority inflation is the classic failure: when everything is P0, nothing is, and the labels lose meaning.
- For mykb, priority labels are assigned at triage and revisited when queues are reviewed.
