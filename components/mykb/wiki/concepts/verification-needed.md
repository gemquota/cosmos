# Verification Needed

## Summary
Verification-needed marks content that must be checked — claims that are surprising, recent, or load-bearing — before it is treated as established.

## Details
- The flag is stronger than citation-needed: a source exists or is claimed, but the match between source and claim is unconfirmed.
- Verification work is read-and-compare, which makes it a good review-sprint task.
- For mykb, verification-needed flags clear to verified-tag and feed the verifiability-score.
