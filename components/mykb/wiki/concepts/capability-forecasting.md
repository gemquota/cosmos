# Capability Forecasting

## Summary
Capability forecasting estimates how AI capabilities will evolve — from extrapolating scaling curves to expert elicitation and biological anchors. It feeds timelines, compute governance thresholds, and responsible-scaling gates, and it is systematically hard because capability is multidimensional.

## Details
- **Methods** — scaling-law extrapolation (Epoch AI), expert surveys, task-specific benchmarks, and scenario analysis.
- **Uncertainty** — frontier capability is fat-tailed; point forecasts routinely miss paradigm shifts.
- **Use in safety** — thresholds for dangerous-capability evals and RSP tiers are forecasts operationalized as gates.
- **Self-fulfilling aspect** — public forecasts shape funding and races, blurring prediction and policy.
- **RSIS3 angle** — the knowledge loop's gap detector and trend analysis are lightweight capability forecasting for the wiki's own coverage.
