# Category Pages

## Summary
Category pages list every article in a category, usually generated from tags or frontmatter so the list never goes stale.

## Details
- They answer the question 'what does this wiki have on X?' and are the main alternative to full-text search for browsing.
- Category pages succeed when the category vocabulary is clean — that is why they depend on tags-practice and tag-hygiene.
- For mykb, category pages are hub pages fed by the tag set, and their completeness is a health-dashboard signal.
