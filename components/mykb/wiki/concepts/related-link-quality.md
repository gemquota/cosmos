# Related Link Quality

## Summary
Related link quality asks of every Related entry: is the target genuinely relevant, is the annotation informative, and would a reader clicking it be rewarded?

## Details
- Link farms fail this bar — a Related block stuffed with loosely related pages helps nobody and dilutes the links that matter.
- Annotations should say why the relationship exists, not restate the target's title.
- For mykb, related-link-quality review is part of promotion readiness and keeps the graph dense with meaningful edges.
