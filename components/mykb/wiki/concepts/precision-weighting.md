# Precision Weighting

## Summary

Precision Weighting — Scaling prediction errors by their reliability in predictive coding.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Precision is the inverse variance of a signal — its reliability.
- High-precision errors get large weight; low-precision errors are down-weighted.
- Attention may implement precision weighting across the hierarchy.
- Explains why surprise magnitude alone does not drive updating.
