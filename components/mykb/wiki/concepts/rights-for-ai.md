# Rights for AI

## Summary
Rights-for-AI debates consider whether systems with sentience or agency should hold rights protecting them from harm.

## Details
- Rights-for-AI debates consider whether systems with sentience or agency should hold rights protecting them from harm.
- Most current systems lack the properties rights protect, but the question scales with capability.
- Premature rights claims can also be a regulatory distraction or a safety risk.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle treats current agents as instruments; the question is tracked as open.
