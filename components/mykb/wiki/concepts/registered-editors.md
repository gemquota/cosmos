# Registered Editors

## Summary
Registered editors are contributors with an established identity and contribution record, qualifying for elevated rights like semi-protected editing.

## Details
- Registration is a trust milestone earned by history, not granted by declaration.
- The distinction exists so the wiki can gate riskier actions while keeping the barrier to starting low.
- For mykb, registered status is the middle rung between new editors and the quality team.
