# Redirect Proposal

## Summary
A redirect proposal records the case for turning a page into a redirect: the old slug, the target, and why a redirect beats keeping or deleting.

## Details
- Redirects are usually a consequence of renames or merges, and the proposal makes that consequence explicit.
- The proposal covers the redirect's lifetime — when it can be retired and who checks that no links depend on it.
- For mykb, redirect proposals follow redirect-criteria and are recorded in the link-updates log.
