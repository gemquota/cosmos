# Dated Sources

## Summary
A dated source has its publication or update date recorded, so the article can weigh how current its evidence is.

## Details
- Dated sources are the evidence side of dated claims: the claim says as-of, the source proves since-when.
- Curation checks date alignment — a claim dated 2026 must not rest solely on a 2019 source without acknowledgment.
- For mykb, dated sources are a source-formatting requirement and feed the source-dates and timeliness reports.
