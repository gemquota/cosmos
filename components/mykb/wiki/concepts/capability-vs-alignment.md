# Capability vs Alignment

## Summary
Capability is what a system can do; alignment is whether its objectives serve the designer.

## Details
- Capability is what a system can do; alignment is whether its objectives serve the designer.
- The distinction matters because capability gains without alignment are net risk.
- Evals must measure both axes independently to detect the misalignment behind competence.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop tracks capability proxies and alignment proxies (practices compliance) separately.
