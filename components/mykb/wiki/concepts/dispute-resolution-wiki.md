# Dispute Resolution

## Summary
Dispute resolution is the structured path for settling disagreements: discuss on talk pages, seek third opinions, mediate, and finally arbitrate.

## Details
- The ladder works because each step is cheaper than the next, so most disputes resolve early.
- A dispute is resolved when the outcome is recorded and the article reflects it — not when everyone is happy.
- For mykb, dispute resolution sits under the editorial policy and feeds the consensus and norm-setting processes.
