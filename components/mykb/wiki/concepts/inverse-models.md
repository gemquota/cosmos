# Inverse Models

## Summary

Inverse Models — Internal models that compute actions needed to reach a desired state.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Inverse models invert the dynamics: desired outcome → required command.
- Trained alongside forward models in motor control.
- Support goal-directed reaching and tool use.
- Basis of model-based control in agents and robots.
