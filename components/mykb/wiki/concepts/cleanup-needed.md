# Cleanup Needed

## Summary
Cleanup-needed marks mechanical problems — formatting, broken tables, stray headings, inconsistent lists — that do not change meaning.

## Details
- Cleanup is the cheapest maintenance task and the easiest entry point for new editors.
- Cleanup flags should be safe to batch: the work is mechanical and low-risk, so it fits cleanup-days and edit sprints.
- For mykb, cleanup-needed flags are generated by markdown linting reports and cleared in cleanup-days.
