# Intros

## Summary
Intros are brief opening articles that welcome a reader into a topic or section and set expectations for what follows.

## Details
- They differ from primers by scope: an intro introduces a section of the wiki, a primer introduces a field.
- Intros should be short, link-rich, and rarely need updating — if an intro drifts from the section, the section moved faster than the intro.
- For mykb, intros are a landing-page variant used at section fronts.
