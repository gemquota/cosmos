# Rankings

## Summary
Rankings order options — tools, approaches, patterns — on explicit criteria, turning comparison research into a reusable decision aid.

## Details
- A ranking without its criteria and weighting is opinion; with them it is a decision-guide artifact that readers can disagree with productively.
- Rankings rot faster than most articles because the field moves; each ranking needs a recorded as-of date.
- For mykb, rankings are comparison-articles with a score column, and dated-claims keeps their aging visible.
