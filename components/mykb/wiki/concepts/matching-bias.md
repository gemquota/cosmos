# Matching Bias

## Summary

Matching Bias — Selecting cards or evidence that match the named items in a rule.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- In the Wason task, people pick cards named in the rule rather than falsifying ones.
- Explains performance without invoking belief content.
- De-biased by explicit negation instruction and training.
- Closely tied to the confirmation-pattern of conditional reasoning.
