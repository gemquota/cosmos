# Prototype Theory

## Summary

Prototype Theory — Categorization by similarity to an abstract central example.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Rosch: categories are organized around prototypes, not necessary-and-sufficient features.
- Membership is graded: robins are 'birdier' than penguins.
- Explains typicality effects in recall and verification.
- Contrasts with classical and exemplar views of concepts.
