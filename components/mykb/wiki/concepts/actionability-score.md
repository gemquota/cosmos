# Actionability Score

## Summary
The actionability score rates whether a reader can act after reading: commands that run, decisions that can be made, steps that can be followed.

## Details
- It is high when the article's goal is procedural and the procedures are verified; low when the article stays abstract.
- Actionability is judged from the reader's goal, which the article should state.
- For mykb, the actionability score is the differentiator for how-to articles and decision guides.
