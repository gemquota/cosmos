# Watermarking AI Content

## Summary
Watermarking embeds detectable marks in AI-generated text, images, or audio.

## Details
- Watermarking embeds detectable marks in AI-generated text, images, or audio.
- Robust watermarks survive edits; fragile ones do not.
- Watermarks help attribution but are not absolute proof.
- RSIS3 relevance: marking generated notes supports provenance and honesty.
