# Structure Needed

## Summary
Structure-needed marks an article whose organization fights the reader — sections in the wrong order, missing headings, content in the wrong sections.

## Details
- Structure problems are distinct from content problems: the facts can be right while the shape is unreadable.
- Fixing structure is usually a re-outline-and-move job, best done with split-criteria and parent-child conventions in mind.
- For mykb, structure-needed flags feed rewrite-needed review and are prioritized by navigability-score.
