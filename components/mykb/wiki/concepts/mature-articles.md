# Mature Articles

## Summary
Mature articles have reached stable quality: complete coverage, verified sources, and a settled structure that edits no longer churn.

## Details
- Maturity is earned through the promotion and review pipeline, not declared.
- Mature articles still need review, but at the longest cadence and with the lightest touch.
- For mykb, mature articles anchor the full-article-ratio and are the model the promotion bar aims at.
