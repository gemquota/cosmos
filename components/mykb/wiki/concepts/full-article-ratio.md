# Full Article Ratio

## Summary
Full article ratio is the count of growing-status articles divided by total articles — the wiki's depth indicator.

## Details
- It is the complement story to stub-ratio: a healthy mature wiki has a high full ratio, while an acquisition phase naturally lowers it.
- Ratio alone hides absolute depth, so it is reported with the absolute counts of fulls and stubs.
- For mykb, this ratio is the headline number for promotion passes: a curation sprint should move it without sacrificing stub velocity.
