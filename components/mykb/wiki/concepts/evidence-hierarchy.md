# Evidence Hierarchy

## Summary

Evidence Hierarchy — Ordering of evidence types by resistance to bias.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Typical order: meta-analyses, RCTs, cohort, case-control, case series, opinion.
- Randomization controls confounds but limits generalizability.
- Hierarchies are decision aids, not proofs.
- Guides evidence evaluation in medicine, policy, and knowledge systems.
