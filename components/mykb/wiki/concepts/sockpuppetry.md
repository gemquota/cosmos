# Sockpuppetry

## Summary
Sockpuppetry is one person operating multiple accounts to evade scrutiny — stacking votes, evading bans, or making edits look community-driven.

## Details
- It corrupts consensus processes and the trust signals built on contribution history.
- Detection uses behavioral patterns and history analysis; the response is account action and review of the affected decisions.
- For mykb, sockpuppetry is an enforcement matter handled under the dispute-resolution and arbitration rules.
