# Article Quality Checklist

## Summary
An article quality checklist turns vague quality goals into a repeatable gate: frontmatter complete, description one sentence, body between the word-count bounds, every wikilink resolvable, and sources present and verified.

## Details
- The checklist is the operational core of promotion-readiness review: reviewers run it mechanically so taste only matters where the checklist deliberately leaves room.
- Items should be binary (pass/fail) where possible — a broken link is broken — and scored where judgment applies, such as summary clarity.
- For mykb, the checklist is what separates a stub from a growing article: missing sources or unresolved links fail the gate regardless of how good the prose is.
