# Dual-Use Research

## Summary
Dual-use research is work whose findings enable both constructive and destructive applications — synthetic biology, cyber exploits, or general-purpose AI methods. Governance seeks to preserve benefits while restricting the most dangerous applications.

## Details
- **Canonical case** — gain-of-function virology: understanding pandemic potential requires making pathogens more transmissible.
- **AI dimension** — capability research (self-improvement, persuasion, autonomy) is dual-use by nature.
- **Screening** — publication review, tiered access, and expert panels classify risk before release.
- **Responsible scaling link** — frontier labs treat dual-use capability thresholds as triggers for extra safeguards.
- **RSIS3 relevance** — the wiki documents dual-use concepts so future passes can classify knowledge; hazard-aware curation is a stated practice.
