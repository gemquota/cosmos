# Survivorship Bias

## Summary

Survivorship Bias — Drawing conclusions only from those who made it through selection.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Classic example: WWII planes returned with damage where they could survive hits.
- Hidden failures carry the information that success stories hide.
