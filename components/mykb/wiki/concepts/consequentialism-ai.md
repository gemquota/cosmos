# Consequentialism for AI

## Summary
Consequentialist AI ethics judges actions by outcomes: maximize expected good, minimize expected harm.

## Details
- Consequentialist AI ethics judges actions by outcomes: maximize expected good, minimize expected harm.
- It is the default frame in alignment (utility functions, expected value) but faces measurement problems.
- Debates include which consequences count and over what horizon.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's check-practices evaluate outcomes, a consequentialist posture.
