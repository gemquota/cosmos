# Training Data Extraction

## Summary
Training data extraction targets verbatim recovery of training examples from a model's memory.

## Details
- Training data extraction targets verbatim recovery of training examples from a model's memory.
- Classic studies extracted personal emails and PII from language models.
- Defenses trade off model utility and memorization.
- RSIS3 relevance: retention and curation decisions in the bundle weigh extraction risk.
