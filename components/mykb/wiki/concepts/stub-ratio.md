# Stub Ratio

## Summary
Stub ratio is the count of stub-status articles divided by total articles; it measures how much of the wiki is framing versus depth.

## Details
- A rising stub ratio during an acquisition wave is healthy; a permanently high one signals promotion throughput has stalled.
- The ratio is only meaningful alongside growth-ratio and full-article-ratio — the same number can reflect a young wiki or a neglected one.
- For mykb, the stub ratio is tracked on the health dashboard and drives promotion-readiness review queues.
