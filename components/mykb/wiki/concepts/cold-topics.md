# Cold Topics

## Summary
Cold topics are knowledge areas that move slowly — stable standards, established fundamentals — where articles stay accurate for years.

## Details
- Cold topics deserve the wiki's deep formats: longforms and deep dives are cheap to maintain here because they rarely need updating.
- A cold topic is not a dead topic; it still needs occasional review, just on a long cadence.
- For mykb, cold topics are classified by knowledge-temperature and scheduled for the longest review intervals.
