# Internal Models

## Summary

Internal Models — Learned representations of the world used for prediction and control.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Forward models predict outcomes; inverse models compute required actions.
- Internal models are updated by prediction error.
- They underlie motor control, mental rotation, and theory of mind.
- Central to model-based cognition and skill learning.
