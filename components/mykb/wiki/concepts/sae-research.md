# SAE Research

## Summary
Sparse autoencoder research trains overcomplete dictionaries to decompose activations into sparse, human-meaningful features.

## Details
- Sparse autoencoder research trains overcomplete dictionaries to decompose activations into sparse, human-meaningful features.
- It addresses polysemanticity by recovering features rather than neurons.
- Open questions: feature stability across models, feature double-counting, and scaling to frontier models.
- RSIS3 relevance: dictionary-style decomposition of graph embeddings could label semantic features.
