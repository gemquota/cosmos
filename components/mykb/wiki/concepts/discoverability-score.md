# Discoverability Score

## Summary
The discoverability score rates how findable an article is: incoming links, title and description quality, tag placement, and index membership.

## Details
- An excellent article with a zero discoverability score is a waste — the graph must route readers to it.
- The score is computed from the link graph plus metadata checks, and it is what the orphan report feeds.
- For mykb, discoverability scoring makes the link graph's blind spots visible.
