# Practical Significance

## Summary

Practical Significance — Whether an effect matters in real-world terms.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Depends on costs, benefits, stakes, and alternatives.
- A statistically reliable effect can be trivially small or vice versa.
- Decision theory formalizes the trade-off.
- Central to evidence-based policy and practice.
