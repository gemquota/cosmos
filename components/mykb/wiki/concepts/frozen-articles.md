# Frozen Articles

## Summary
Frozen articles are intentionally locked — no edits accepted, usually because they record a settled historical state or are under dispute resolution.

## Details
- Freezing is a governance tool with a cost: frozen articles cannot absorb corrections, so freezes must have an expiry or an appeal path.
- Frozen is distinct from stable: stable articles may be edited when needed; frozen articles may not.
- For mykb, frozen articles are rare and are tracked with their freeze reason and expiry.
