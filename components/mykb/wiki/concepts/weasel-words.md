# Weasel Words

## Summary
Weasel words are vague qualifiers — 'many people say', 'it is widely believed', 'some argue' — that state an opinion without naming who holds it.

## Details
- They fail verifiability: an unattributed claim cannot be checked.
- The fix is attribution: name who says it, or cut the claim.
- For mykb, weasel words are flagged by prose-linting and fail the citation-necessity review.
