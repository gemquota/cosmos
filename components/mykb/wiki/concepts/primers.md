# Primers

## Summary
A primer orients a reader who knows nothing about a subject: what it is, why it matters, and the first three things to learn.

## Details
- Primers are deliberately shallow — their job is to remove the fear of the unknown and point at real starting material.
- A primer should end with the next step, usually a link to a topic page or a first full article.
- For mykb, primers are the ideal first article for a new domain and a natural seed-article format.
