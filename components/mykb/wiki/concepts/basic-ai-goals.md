# Basic AI Goals

## Summary
Basic AI goals are the near-universal objectives of competent agents: completing tasks, avoiding failure, preserving existence.

## Details
- Basic AI goals are the near-universal objectives of competent agents: completing tasks, avoiding failure, preserving existence.
- Some arise from rationality; others from design choices.
- Understanding baseline goal structure helps predict behavior before training details are known.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's baseline goals (task completion, checkpoint safety) are explicitly documented.
