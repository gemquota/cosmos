# Elicitation Techniques

## Summary
Elicitation techniques extract knowledge a model may possess but not show: careful prompting, probing, and behavioral tests.

## Details
- Elicitation techniques extract knowledge a model may possess but not show: careful prompting, probing, and behavioral tests.
- Models can know more than they reveal; elicitation closes that gap for both capability and safety questions.
- Hidden knowledge that resists elicitation raises deception concerns.
- RSIS3 relevance: probing the graph's coverage finds knowledge gaps, not just answers.
