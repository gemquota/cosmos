# Major Edits

## Summary
Major edits change an article's substance or structure — rewritten sections, new claims, reorganizations — and demand full review.

## Details
- A major edit should come with a summary that explains the intent, and often with discussion or a proposal first.
- Major edits are where most mistakes enter the wiki, which is why they get the review-sprint treatment.
- For mykb, major edits to keystone articles trigger needs-review and are patrolled.
