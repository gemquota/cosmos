# Risk-Benefit Analysis

## Summary
Risk-benefit analysis compares expected harms and benefits of AI development and deployment choices.

## Details
- Risk-benefit analysis compares expected harms and benefits of AI development and deployment choices.
- Proper analysis is comparative: vs the status quo, not vs perfection.
- It is where precautionary and proactionary instincts meet numbers.
- RSIS3 relevance: each pass implicitly trades risk (churn, errors) against benefit (coverage).
