# Human Supervision Limits

## Summary
Human supervision limits include attention, speed, bias, and cost — humans are slow, few, and fallible reviewers.

## Details
- Human supervision limits include attention, speed, bias, and cost — humans are slow, few, and fallible reviewers.
- These limits are structural, not fixable by training better humans.
- Designs must route around them: automation, sampling, and AI assistance.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop automates checks and samples human review for exactly this reason.
