# Stable Articles

## Summary
Stable articles are pages that change rarely — not because they are ignored, but because they are correct and complete.

## Details
- Stability is the healthy end state of the lifecycle: low edit churn with occasional verification.
- Stable should never mean frozen; the article still gets reviewed and can be unfrozen by new evidence.
- For mykb, stable articles are the cheapest pages to own and the target state for evergreen content.
