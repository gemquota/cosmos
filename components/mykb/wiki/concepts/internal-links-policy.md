# Internal Links Policy

## Summary
The internal links policy sets expectations for wikilinks: every article must link to at least the required number of others, links must resolve, and link text should describe the target.

## Details
- Internal links are the wiki's connective tissue — the policy exists to keep the graph dense and navigable.
- The policy also covers link placement: links in prose carry more meaning than a bare list at the bottom.
- For mykb, the internal links policy is enforced by the global link check and link-score.
