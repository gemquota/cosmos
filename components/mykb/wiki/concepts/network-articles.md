# Network Articles

## Summary
Network articles derive much of their value from position: they sit between clusters, get linked from many directions, or carry navigation traffic.

## Details
- Position can be accidental — a well-linked page that deserves no links is a red flag for link quality.
- Understanding which articles are network-critical comes from centrality analysis, which informs maintenance priority.
- For mykb, centrality-weighting and incoming-link-counts identify network articles for protection and review.
