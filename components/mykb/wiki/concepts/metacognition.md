# Metacognition

## Summary
Metacognition is the agent's ability to monitor, evaluate, and regulate its own thinking — knowing when it is uncertain, stuck, or wrong. It matters because it is the substrate for reflection, calibration, and self-improvement. RSIS3's L2/L3 loops are metacognitive machinery.

## Details
- Sub-skills: monitoring confidence, detecting errors, choosing strategies.
- In agents: self-critique, confidence checks, and reflection passes.
- Poor metacognition produces confident failure; good metacognition catches it.
- Open questions: measuring metacognitive accuracy in agents.
