# First-Principles AI

## Summary
First-principles AI is an approach that designs systems from fundamental laws — logic, causality, utility — rather than from imitating human data or convention. It is often proposed as the safest path to strong AI because every component is derived and inspectable.

## Details
- **Contrast** — data-driven learning finds correlates; first-principles derivation explains why something works.
- **Strengths** — auditability, formal guarantees, and resistance to spurious correlations.
- **Weaknesses** — known problems resist closed-form solutions; real systems still need learning for perception and common sense.
- **Hybrid practice** — modern systems pair learned components with formal scaffolds (verifiers, planners, type systems).
- **RSIS3 angle** — the triad's practices and checkers are first-principles governance: rules derived from explicit principles, enforced mechanically.
