# Digest Pages

## Summary
Digest pages round up the current state of a topic — recent changes, new articles, latest findings — so a reader can catch up in one page.

## Details
- They are time-bound by nature: a digest without a period label ('July digest') drifts into meaninglessness.
- Digests are expensive to maintain and should be generated from change logs and recent-articles feeds where possible.
- For mykb, digests overlap with update-days output and are linked from topic pages when a topic is moving fast.
