# Informal Reasoning

## Summary

Informal Reasoning — Reasoning about everyday, ambiguous, or value-laden problems.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Involves weighing pros and cons without formal syntax.
- Prone to motivated reasoning and myside bias.
- Improved by considering alternatives and articulating criteria.
- Distinct from formal reasoning but trains the same skills.
