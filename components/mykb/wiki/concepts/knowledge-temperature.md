# Knowledge Temperature

## Summary
Knowledge temperature is a metaphor for how fast an area changes: hot topics move weekly, warm ones monthly, cold ones yearly.

## Details
- Temperature determines review cadence and article strategy — hot topics should be shortform and frequently refreshed; cold ones can be deep and stable.
- A topic's temperature can change, so the classification is reviewed rather than fixed.
- For mykb, knowledge temperature is assigned per topic cluster and drives its freshness policy.
