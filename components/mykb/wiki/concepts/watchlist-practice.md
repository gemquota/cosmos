# Watchlist Practice

## Summary
Watchlist practice is the convention of tracking a chosen set of articles, so changes to them surface instead of hiding in recent-changes noise.

## Details
- A good watchlist is small and purpose-built — keystones, pages you own, contested topics.
- Watchlists are personal but their practice is shared: reviewing watchlisted changes is how senior editors catch regressions.
- For mykb, watchlist practice complements the review queue for keystone and protected articles.
