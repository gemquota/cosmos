# Seasonal Articles

## Summary
Seasonal articles are tied to a recurring period — quarterly reviews, annual roundups, event writeups — and are expected to cycle.

## Details
- A seasonal article is either rewritten each cycle or versioned; a stale seasonal page is a silent misinformation risk.
- The curation calendar (review-days, update-days) is what keeps seasonal articles on schedule.
- For mykb, seasonal articles are rare and are flagged with dated-claims so their cycle is visible.
