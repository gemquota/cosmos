# Stub Criteria

## Summary
Stub criteria define the floor: a stub needs correct frontmatter, a one-sentence description, a short framing body, and a Related block of resolvable links.

## Details
- The stub is a promise to promote, so it should name the questions a full article must answer — that makes the future promotion cheap.
- Articles that cannot meet even the stub bar are better as raw captures or seed candidates than as wiki pages.
- For mykb, stub criteria are enforced by linting at write time, and stub-ratio is the metric that tracks how many promises are outstanding.
