# Longforms

## Summary
Longforms are the wiki's long articles — deep dives, syntheses, and case studies that run far past the normal full-article length.

## Details
- Length is a consequence of the subject's depth, not a goal; a longform that could be two articles should be split.
- Longforms need the strongest structure: a clear spine, signposting, and navigation so readers can enter and exit anywhere.
- For mykb, longforms are deliberate exceptions to the 400-word ceiling and are tracked on the health dashboard.
