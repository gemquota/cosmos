# Expansion Needed

## Summary
Expansion-needed marks stubs and thin articles that deserve promotion: the concept is real and valuable, but the body does not carry it yet.

## Details
- The flag works best with a note on what to add — sources, examples, a comparison — so the next writer knows the direction.
- Expansion flags are the raw material of promotion queues and curation sprints.
- For mykb, expansion-needed pages are ranked by article-health-scores to set promotion order.
