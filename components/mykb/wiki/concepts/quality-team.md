# Quality Team

## Summary
The quality team is the group responsible for the wiki's quality bar: setting the criteria, reviewing the queue, and making the final calls on promotion and demotion.

## Details
- Its legitimacy comes from procedure, not rank — decisions follow the published criteria and leave records.
- A small quality team is a bottleneck; the team's job includes growing reviewers, not just reviewing.
- For mykb, the quality team operates the review queues and approval workflows.
