# Generative Models

## Summary

Generative Models — Models that capture how data are generated, enabling prediction and synthesis.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- A generative model p(x) or p(x|z) explains how observations arise from latent causes.
- Used for simulation, counterfactual reasoning, and representation learning.
- Contrast with discriminative models that only draw boundaries.
- In cognitive science, the brain is cast as a generative model of the world.
