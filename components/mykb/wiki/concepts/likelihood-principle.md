# Likelihood Principle

## Summary

Likelihood Principle — Statistical evidence resides in the likelihood function of observed data.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- All evidence about a parameter, given the model, is contained in the likelihood.
- Underpins Bayesian and likelihoodist inference.
- Contrasts with frequentist procedures that depend on unobserved data.
- Guides how evidence should update belief.
