# Puffery

## Summary
Puffery is exaggerated or unverifiable praise — the marketing voice leaking into article prose.

## Details
- It damages the wiki twice: it misleads readers and it erodes trust in the whole corpus.
- Puffery in articles usually arrives via promotional-content sources and self-promotion.
- For mykb, puffery is flagged at review and fails the neutral-language and accuracy checks.
