# Tag Hygiene

## Summary
Tag hygiene is the ongoing work of keeping the tag set small, consistent, and meaningful: no singular/plural pairs, no typos, no near-duplicate tags.

## Details
- A dirty tag vocabulary silently fragments clusters — two tags that look alike never group their articles together.
- Hygiene is enforced by reports (tag counts, near-duplicate detection) and repaired by tag merging and tag renaming.
- For mykb, tag hygiene runs as a periodic audit between curation sprints.
