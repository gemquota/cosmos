# Surface Similarity

## Summary

Surface Similarity — Similarity based on superficial features rather than structure.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Surface similarity is fast, salient, and often misleading.
- Novices rely on it; experts learn to look past it.
- Classic problem-solving studies: story solutions transfer only when surface stories match.
- Contrast with relational similarity and deep structure.
