# Verifiability Score

## Summary
The verifiability score rates how checkable the article is: sources for claims, working URLs, citations placed on the claims they support.

## Details
- It is distinct from accuracy — a well-sourced article can still be wrong, but its claims can at least be checked.
- The score combines citation coverage, source health, and access-date freshness.
- For mykb, the verifiability score is raised by verification work and is part of the promotion gate.
