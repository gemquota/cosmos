# Merge Proposal

## Summary
A merge proposal records a proposed article merge: the two sources, the target, the rationale, and the open questions — before any edit happens.

## Details
- Proposals separate the decision from the execution: review the rationale first, execute only after approval.
- A good proposal includes what happens to both slugs afterward (redirect, alias) so the graph impact is visible.
- For mykb, merge proposals are tracked in the review queue and follow merge-criteria.
