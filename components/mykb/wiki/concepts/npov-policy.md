# Neutral Point of View Policy

## Summary
The neutral point of view policy requires articles to represent significant views fairly and proportionately, without asserting one as the truth.

## Details
- Neutrality is about presentation, not absence of opinion: contested claims are attributed and weighed.
- NPOV is the hardest policy to apply because everyone's biases hide from themselves — it is enforced through review and peer critique.
- For mykb, NPOV matters for technical debates (architecture choices, tool wars) where articles can quietly take sides.
