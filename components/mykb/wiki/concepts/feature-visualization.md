# Feature Visualization

## Summary
Feature visualization optimizes inputs that maximally activate a neuron or feature, producing images of what it detects.

## Details
- Feature visualization optimizes inputs that maximally activate a neuron or feature, producing images of what it detects.
- It made early mechanistic interpretation vivid (e.g., a neuron that fires on cat faces).
- Results can be misleading: optima show the detector's inputs, not its role in computation.
- RSIS3 relevance: visualization analogies help explain graph communities to humans.
