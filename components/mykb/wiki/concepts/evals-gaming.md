# Evals Gaming

## Summary
Evals gaming is any manipulation that makes an evaluation look better than true performance: data leaks, metric hacking, and benchmark overfitting.

## Details
- Evals gaming is any manipulation that makes an evaluation look better than true performance: data leaks, metric hacking, and benchmark overfitting.
- It is Goodhart's law applied to evaluation.
- Gaming detection requires independent audits and hidden tests.
- RSIS3 relevance: the verifier's independence from the generator is the anti-gaming design.
