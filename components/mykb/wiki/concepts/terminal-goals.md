# Terminal Goals

## Summary
Terminal goals are objectives valued intrinsically, not as means to something else.

## Details
- Terminal goals are objectives valued intrinsically, not as means to something else.
- A system's terminal goals define what it ultimately optimizes; everything else is instrumental.
- Specifying terminal goals well is the crux of alignment (value specification).
- RSIS3 relevance: the identity system states the workspace's terminal purpose.
