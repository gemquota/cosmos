# Frontier Models

## Summary
Frontier models are the most capable systems at a given moment, usually trained by a handful of labs.

## Details
- Frontier models are the most capable systems at a given moment, usually trained by a handful of labs.
- Their capability concentration drives most governance debates and RSP thresholds.
- Frontier status is transient; evals must track capability, not marketing.
- RSIS3 relevance: capability tracking in the wiki monitors the frontier for context.
