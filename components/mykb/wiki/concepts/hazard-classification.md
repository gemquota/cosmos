# Hazard Classification

## Summary
Hazard classification groups AI systems by the potential harm they enable, from benign to catastrophic.

## Details
- Hazard classification groups AI systems by the potential harm they enable, from benign to catastrophic.
- It feeds thresholds in policies, evals, and deployment gates.
- Classification is only as good as the underlying capability estimates.
- RSIS3 relevance: the wiki's risk taxonomy classifies concepts, not just systems.
