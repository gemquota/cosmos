# Prediction Error Signal

## Summary

Prediction Error Signal — Difference between expected and actual outcomes used to drive learning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Prediction errors update internal models in predictive processing and RL.
- Signed errors drive correction; surprise magnitude sets learning rate.
- Dopamine encodes reward prediction errors in the brain.
- Formal basis of error-driven and statistical learning.
