# Analogical Reasoning

## Summary
Analogical reasoning maps structure from a source situation to a target, transferring solutions or predictions. It matters because analogy is how agents apply past experience to novel but structurally similar problems. It underlies case-based reasoning and few-shot learning.

## Details
- Requires identifying relational structure, not surface similarity.
- Strong analogy = deep structural alignment.
- In LLMs: analogy is implicit in few-shot prompting.
- Open questions: evaluating the soundness of model analogies.
