# Mediation

## Summary
Mediation is a neutral third party facilitating a dispute — helping the parties find common ground without imposing a decision.

## Details
- Mediators manage process, not content: they keep the discussion structured and move it toward a written resolution.
- Mediation fails gracefully into arbitration when the parties cannot agree despite facilitation.
- For mykb, mediation is the middle rung of the dispute-resolution ladder, applied before arbitration.
