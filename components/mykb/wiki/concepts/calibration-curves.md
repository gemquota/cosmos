# Calibration Curves

## Summary

Calibration Curves — Plots of predicted probability versus observed frequency.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Points near the diagonal mean well calibrated; bowing reveals over/underconfidence.
- Bin forecasts (e.g., 60-70%) and plot mean forecast vs outcome rate.
- Complement to Brier score decomposition.
- Used for forecasters, models, and self-assessments.
