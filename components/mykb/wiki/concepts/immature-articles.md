# Immature Articles

## Summary
Immature articles are pages still developing — recently created, recently rewritten, or flagged for expansion — not yet settled.

## Details
- Immaturity is normal and healthy; the curation goal is moving articles through maturity, not holding them at birth.
- Immature articles should avoid being over-cited by other pages until they stabilize.
- For mykb, immature articles are the seed and stub population, tracked by the stub-ratio.
