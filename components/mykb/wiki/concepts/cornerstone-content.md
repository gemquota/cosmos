# Cornerstone Content

## Summary
Cornerstone content is the small set of foundational articles a whole area rests on — the concepts every other page assumes.

## Details
- Cornerstones earn their status by being the most-linked, most-cited, and most-read pages in their region.
- They demand the highest writing and maintenance bar because their flaws propagate into every article that links to them.
- For mykb, cornerstone articles are a curation focus: promotion of a cornerstones-first set is how new areas are seeded.
