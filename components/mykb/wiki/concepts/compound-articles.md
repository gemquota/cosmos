# Compound Articles

## Summary
Compound articles deliberately cover several related ideas in one page, trading atomicity for coherence — a family of patterns, a pipeline, a chapter of a story.

## Details
- They are justified when the ideas are read together and splitting would force readers to hop constantly.
- Compound articles must state their scope up front and link to atomic articles for the individual pieces.
- For mykb, compound articles are rare; the default is atomic with a topic page above.
