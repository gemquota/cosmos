# External Links Policy

## Summary
The external links policy governs when and how articles link outside the wiki: sources in references, official docs, and curated further-reading are allowed; spammy or tangential links are not.

## Details
- External links are the wiki's interface with the wider web, so they carry trust and maintenance costs that internal links do not.
- The policy should require a reason for every external link and prohibit link-stuffing and promotional links.
- For mykb, external links are concentrated in reference blocks and further-reading, keeping prose free of interstitial URLs.
