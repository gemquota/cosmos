# Stale Articles

## Summary
Stale articles are pages whose content has aged past accuracy — version claims outdated, sources dead, examples broken.

## Details
- Staleness is a state, not a judgment: the article may be salvageable with an update or due for archival.
- Stale is distinguished from dormant by decay: dormant is unexamined, stale is examined and found aged.
- For mykb, stale articles are the refresh-days workload and the demotion-criteria trigger.
