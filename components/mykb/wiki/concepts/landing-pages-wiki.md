# Landing Pages

## Summary
Landing pages are the wiki's entry points: the home page, section fronts, and the pages a new reader is most likely to reach first.

## Details
- A landing page earns its name by answering 'what is here and where do I go' in the first screenful.
- Landing pages need the most careful maintenance because they shape first impressions and are the heaviest-linked pages in the graph.
- For mykb, landing pages are generated where possible and reviewed during curation sprints.
