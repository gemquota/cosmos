# Atomic Articles

## Summary
Atomic articles cover exactly one idea, following the evergreen-note principle that each concept deserves its own addressable page.

## Details
- Atomicity makes links precise: a reader linking to a page knows what they are linking to, and the graph stays clean.
- Atomicity is enforced by split-criteria and merge-criteria — pages that violate it get restructured, not tolerated.
- For mykb, atomic articles are the norm, and compound articles are the deliberate exception.
