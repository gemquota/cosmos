# S-Risks

## Summary
S-risks are scenarios where outcomes are astronomically bad, typically vast suffering rather than extinction.

## Details
- S-risks are scenarios where outcomes are astronomically bad, typically vast suffering rather than extinction.
- Distinguished from x-risk by outcome type: suffering vs annihilation.
- Mitigations emphasize avoiding lock-in and designing for reversibility.
