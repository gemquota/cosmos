# CommonJS vs ESM

## Summary
Two JavaScript module systems and their resolution differences. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- CJS resolves synchronously; ESM is async and static
- Interop rules govern named and default exports between them
- Open question — how close is the ecosystem to ESM-only?
