# Implicit vs Explicit Feedback

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Explicit feedback: ratings, likes, thumbs — direct but sparse and biased.
- Implicit feedback: clicks, views, dwell time, purchases — abundant but noisy.
- Implicit signals need weighting and negative-sample handling.
- Both degrade over time; freshness weighting keeps models relevant.
