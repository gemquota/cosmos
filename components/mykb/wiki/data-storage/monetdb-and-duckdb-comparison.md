# MonetDB and DuckDB Comparison

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- MonetDB pioneered columnar vectorized execution in research.
- DuckDB applies the same ideas in-process with zero-config analytics.
- Both support standard SQL and analytical workloads without a server.
- Choose DuckDB for embedded/notebook analytics; MonetDB for standalone columnar OLAP.
