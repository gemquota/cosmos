# Precision, Recall, and NDCG

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Precision@k: fraction of top-k results that are relevant.
- Recall@k: fraction of all relevant results found in top-k.
- NDCG discounts gains by rank, rewarding relevant results near the top.
- Report at several k values; metrics trade off against each other.
