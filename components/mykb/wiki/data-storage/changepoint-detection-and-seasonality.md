# Changepoint Detection and Seasonality

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Changepoint detection finds moments when a series' distribution shifts (deployments, incidents).
- Seasonality decomposes series into trend, seasonal, and residual components.
- Decomposed residuals make anomalies easier to detect.
- Methods: CUSUM, Bayesian online changepoint, STL decomposition.
