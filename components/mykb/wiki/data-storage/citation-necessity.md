# Citation Necessity

## Summary
Citation necessity draws the line between claims that need sources and claims that do not: definitions, statistics, historical claims, and disputed facts need them; obvious common knowledge and the article's own reasoning do not.

## Details
- The rule is about verifiability and burden of proof — the more surprising or specific the claim, the stronger the citation required.
- Necessity is judged by a reader's question 'how would anyone check this?'
- For mykb, citation necessity is part of the verifiability policy applied at promotion review.
