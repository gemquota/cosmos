# Netezza and Vertica

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Netezza (IBM) combined columnar storage with FPGA acceleration in an appliance.
- Vertica is a software columnar MPP with compression and projection design.
- Both excel at high-throughput analytics with predictable schemas.
- Cloud migrations typically target Redshift, Snowflake, or lakehouse stacks.
