# FAQ Articles

## Summary
FAQ articles collect recurring questions and answer each one concisely, making the wiki the first stop for the questions people actually ask.

## Details
- The best FAQ entries come from real questions — search logs, session queries, support threads — not from imagined readers.
- Each Q&A should link to the full article that covers the topic, so the FAQ is a gateway, not a replacement.
- For mykb, FAQ entries are suggested by recurring questions and kept fresh by the same review cycle as other articles.
