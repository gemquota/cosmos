# Tutorials in the Wiki

## Summary
A tutorial is a step-by-step article that takes the reader from zero to a working result, with commands, expected outputs, and stopping points.

## Details
- Tutorials differ from explainers: tutorials teach by doing, explainers teach by understanding; a strong article often links the two.
- Tutorial conventions include: stated prerequisites, verified commands, and a clear exit — what the reader can do when finished.
- For mykb, tutorials are linked from how-to articles and must be re-verified on freshness review because commands rot fast.
