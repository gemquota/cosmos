# Forecasting Baselines and Models

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Start with baselines (naive, seasonal naive, moving average) before complex models.
- Classical models: ARIMA, exponential smoothing, Prophet; ML: gradient boosting on features.
- Evaluate with backtesting and out-of-sample error (MAE, RMSE, MAPE).
- Forecast uncertainty matters as much as the point estimate.
