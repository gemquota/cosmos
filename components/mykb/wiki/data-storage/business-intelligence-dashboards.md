# Business Intelligence Dashboards

## Summary

BI dashboards present metrics and trends to decision-makers.
They depend on trusted models underneath.
Good dashboards are curated, not just connected.
Dashboards are products: they need owners, versioning, and periodic pruning.

## Details

- Dashboard layers: data models, semantic layer, and visualization.
- Design for the audience: executive, operational, or analyst.
- Alerting and annotations add context to numbers.
- Embedded and self-serve BI extend reach.
- Trust comes from definitions, freshness, and lineage.
- A dashboard that nobody reads is a liability, not an asset.
- Pair charts with the definitions and lineage behind them.
- Dashboards succeed when they answer decisions, not when they display every number available.
