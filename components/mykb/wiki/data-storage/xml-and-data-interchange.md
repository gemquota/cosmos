# XML and Data Interchange

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- XML is verbose but schema-validated (XSD) and transformable (XSLT).
- Still dominant in finance, healthcare, and government exchanges.
- Parsing cost and verbosity push new systems to JSON/Parquet.
- Bridges matter: most data platforms import/export XML.
