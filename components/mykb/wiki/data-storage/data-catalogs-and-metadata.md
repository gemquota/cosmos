# Data Catalogs and Metadata

## Summary

Data catalogs inventory datasets, schemas, owners, and usage metadata.
They make data discoverable, trusted, and governable.
Modern catalogs automate metadata collection and lineage.
Catalogs turn data into an inventory an organization can actually manage.

## Details

- Technical metadata: schemas, locations, and freshness.
- Business metadata: definitions, owners, and quality ratings.
- Lineage and usage stats surface downstream impact.
- Active metadata powers automation: schema checks, access, and alerts.
- Catalogs are the foundation of self-serve analytics.
- Automated metadata harvesting beats manual documentation.
- Search and browse quality determine whether the catalog gets used.
- Metadata is the connective tissue of a governed data platform.
