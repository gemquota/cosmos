# Navigation Boxes

## Summary
A navigation box is a standardized cluster of links — usually at the top or bottom of an article — that orients the reader within a section or cluster.

## Details
- Navigation boxes work best when they are few, consistent, and generated from the same data as indexes.
- They are a compromise: they add discoverability but cost vertical reading space, so they must earn their place.
- For mykb, navigation boxes are optional conventions for large topic clusters, with placement governed by link-placement rules.
