# Periodic Snapshot Facts

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Snapshot facts record state (inventory, balances) at the end of each period.
- Measures are semi-additive across time, additive across other dimensions.
- They answer 'what was the state at period end'.
- Complement transactional facts rather than replacing them.
