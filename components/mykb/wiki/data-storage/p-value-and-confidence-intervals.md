# p-Values and Confidence Intervals

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- A p-value is the probability of data at least as extreme under the null.
- Confidence intervals show the range of plausible effect sizes.
- Intervals communicate precision; p-values communicate evidence against the null.
- Report both, plus the sample size, to make analyses auditable.
