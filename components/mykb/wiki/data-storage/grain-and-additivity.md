# Grain and Additivity

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Grain is the level of detail per row; it must be declared and unambiguous.
- Additivity rules say across which dimensions a measure can sum.
- Wrong grain assumptions cause double counting in reports.
- Document grain in the model and the metric catalog.
