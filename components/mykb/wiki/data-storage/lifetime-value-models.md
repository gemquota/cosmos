# Lifetime Value Models

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- LTV predicts total value a customer generates over their lifetime.
- Approaches: historical aggregation, cohort LTV, and predictive models.
- Modeling needs churn curves, revenue per period, and discounting.
- LTV gates acquisition spend and personalization decisions.
