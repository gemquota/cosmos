# Analytics

## Summary

Analytics is the practice of systematically converting data into insight and decisions.
It spans descriptive reporting, diagnostic analysis, and predictive modeling.
Strong analytics requires trusted data, clear metrics, and accessible tooling.
Analytics ROI shows up in decisions, not dashboards; measure whether insights change action.

## Details

- Descriptive analytics: what happened, via dashboards and reports.
- Diagnostic: why it happened, via drill-down and root-cause analysis.
- Predictive: what will happen, via forecasting and ML.
- Metrics governance keeps definitions consistent across consumers.
- Self-serve analytics scales insight beyond analyst teams.
- Governance and analytics maturity move together.
- A metric catalog prevents the most common analytics failure: disagreement on definitions.
- Analytics organizations should measure their own effectiveness: query adoption, decision impact, and time to answer.
