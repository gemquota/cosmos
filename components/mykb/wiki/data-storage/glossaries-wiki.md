# Glossaries

## Summary
A glossary is a reference page defining a domain's terms in one place, linking each term to its full article.

## Details
- Glossaries serve readers who hit an unfamiliar word mid-article and need the shortest possible definition before continuing.
- Glossary entries should be one or two sentences and link outward — the glossary is an index of understanding, not a book.
- For mykb, glossaries are hub pages that strengthen the graph by giving every domain term a canonical landing point.
