# Statistical Significance in Data

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Significance asks whether an observed difference is plausibly due to chance.
- p-values, confidence intervals, and effect sizes together tell the story.
- Multiple testing and peeking inflate false positives.
- Practical significance matters: tiny but 'significant' effects may be useless.
