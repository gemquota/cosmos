# Drill-Through and Slicing

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Drill-through links a dashboard metric to the underlying rows.
- Slicing filters the cube by dimension values; dicing selects subsets.
- Both require well-modeled grains and accessible detail tables.
- Design drill paths to respect row-level security.
