# Fivetran and Airbyte

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Fivetran is a managed ELT platform: hundreds of connectors with schema normalization.
- Airbyte is open-source ELT with connectors as code and self-hosting options.
- Both sync to warehouses/lakehouses where transformation happens in SQL (ELT).
- Connector maintenance is the real cost; choose managed vs self-host by volume and budget.
