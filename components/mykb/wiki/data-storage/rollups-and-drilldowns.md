# Rollups and Drilldowns

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Rollups pre-aggregate to coarser grains; drilldowns navigate to finer detail.
- Hierarchies (category > subcategory > product) define the path.
- Drill-through reaches the underlying transaction rows.
- Balance rollup coverage against storage and freshness cost.
