# Examples in the Wiki

## Summary
Examples translate a concept into a concrete case: a worked command, a mini walkthrough, or a realistic scenario the reader can replay.

## Details
- A good example is minimal, true to the article's claims, and labeled as an example so readers know what it is demonstrating.
- Examples are part of the content score and are a promotion-readiness expectation for full articles.
- For mykb, examples make abstract concepts (retry policies, graph metrics) tangible enough to apply.
