# Randomization and Stratification

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Randomization balances unknown confounders across variants.
- Stratification blocks on key segments (country, plan) for precision.
- Unit of randomization (user, session, account) must match analysis unit.
- CUPED-style variance reduction complements stratification.
