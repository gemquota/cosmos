## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Retrieval finds candidates (BM25, embeddings, hybrid); ranking orders them.
- Signals: text match, recency, popularity, personalization, and business rules.
- Two-stage pipelines (retrieve 1000, rank 100) balance quality and latency.
- Measure with offline relevance metrics and online click-through experiments.
