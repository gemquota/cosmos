# Evaluation Metrics for Recommendations

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Offline: precision@k, recall@k, NDCG@k, and coverage/diversity metrics.
- Online: CTR, engagement, conversion, and revenue deltas in A/B tests.
- Offline metrics correlate imperfectly with business outcomes.
- Watch for bias: popularity effects and position bias contaminate naive metrics.
