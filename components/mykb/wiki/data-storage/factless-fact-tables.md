# Factless Fact Tables

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Factless facts store the grain (who did what when) with no numeric measure.
- Uses: coverage (which products were in stock), events, and eligibility.
- Analysis counts rows instead of summing measures.
- They capture business processes that produce no numbers.
