# RFM Analysis

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- RFM scores customers on recency, frequency, and monetary value.
- Quintile scores (1-5) combine into segments like champions, at-risk, hibernating.
- It is simple, explainable, and widely actionable.
- Refresh cadence matters: RFM decays with customer behavior.
