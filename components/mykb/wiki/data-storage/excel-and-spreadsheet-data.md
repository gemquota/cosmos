# Excel and Spreadsheet Data

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Spreadsheets are the most common data source that never leaves the office.
- Issues: merged cells, formatting-as-data, hidden sheets, and manual edits.
- Treat spreadsheets as untrusted input: validate, normalize, and archive originals.
- Automate export/ingest to reduce hand-maintained copies.
