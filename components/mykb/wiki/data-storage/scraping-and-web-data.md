# Scraping and Web Data

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Scraping raises legal, ethical, and technical considerations.
- Check robots.txt, terms of service, and rate-limit politely.
- Design scrapers to break gracefully when markup changes.
- Cache raw responses; reprocess rather than re-scrape.
