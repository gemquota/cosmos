# Diagrams in the Wiki

## Summary
Diagrams in the wiki explain what prose cannot: architecture layers, data flows, state machines, and comparison structures.

## Details
- Text-based diagrams — ASCII art and Mermaid — are preferred because they diff, version, and lint like code.
- Every diagram needs a caption and a one-paragraph explanation, so the page never depends on the image alone.
- For mykb, diagram conventions favor Mermaid blocks over image files, keeping the bundle text-first.
