# Data Federation and Virtualization

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Federation pushes queries to source systems and merges results.
- Virtualization provides a logical layer over physical sources.
- Benefits: freshness and less duplication; costs: source load and latency.
- Best for lightweight integration, not heavy analytical joins.
