# Case Studies

## Summary
Case studies are narrative accounts of a real application: what was built, what broke, what changed — the kind of detail that survives only in experience.

## Details
- They ground abstract concepts in concrete outcomes and are the most cited-adjacent form of evidence an article can include.
- Case studies must be honest about failures or they become advertorials; the instructive value is usually in what went wrong.
- For mykb, case studies are rare and valuable; a curation note can flag candidate experiences from sessions for later case-study writing.
