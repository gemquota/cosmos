# Citation Placement

## Summary
Citation placement attaches each source to the exact claim it supports: at the end of the sentence, clause, or paragraph the citation covers.

## Details
- Placement discipline keeps citations honest — a source placed at the end of a paragraph is implicitly vouching for the whole paragraph.
- For multi-claim sentences, place citations at the specific clause, or split the sentence so each claim carries its own source.
- For mykb, citation placement is checked in review and by inline-citation-style linting.
