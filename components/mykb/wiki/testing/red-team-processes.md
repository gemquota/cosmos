# Red Team Processes

## Summary
Organized adversarial testing of AI systems to find failure modes before release

## Details
- Combine human attackers with automated suites and jailbreak tools.
- Scope includes safety, security, and misuse.
- Findings feed guardrails and training updates.
- Requires evaluation-sandboxes for safe testing.
