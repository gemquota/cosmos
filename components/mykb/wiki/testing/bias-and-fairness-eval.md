# Bias and Fairness Evaluation

## Summary
Measuring and mitigating demographic and representation biases in model outputs

## Details
- Test across groups for disparate performance and tone.
- Datasets must represent the served population.
- Mitigations include data rebalancing and post-processing.
- Particularly critical for recruiting and medical agents.
