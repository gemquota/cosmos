# Model Cards and Datasheets

## Summary
Structured documentation of model behavior, training data, and limitations

## Details
- Model cards summarize intended use, metrics, and known limits.
- Datasheets describe dataset provenance and composition.
- Support auditability and informed deployment.
- Recommended by responsible-ai frameworks.
