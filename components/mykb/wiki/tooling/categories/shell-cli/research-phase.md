## Research Phase
Research Phase appears in 1 session(s) categorized as API, Shell. Related topics: api, bash, cdn, cli.
The research phase is the stage of a project where the problem is understood before it is solved: gathering context, exploring options, and validating assumptions. It comes first in the workflow because the cost of fixing a wrong direction grows with every subsequent phase.
Discovery starts with the question to answer and the constraints that bound it. Reading documentation, inspecting existing code, and querying APIs build the factual base, while experiments test the risky assumptions early. Research is recorded as findings, sources, and open questions, so that the reasoning survives the session.
The phase is also about tooling: shell scripts fetch and inspect data, CDNs are evaluated for asset delivery, and APIs are probed for behavior and limits. A research phase that is too short produces designs built on guesses; one that is too long delays value, so time-boxing and explicit exit criteria keep it effective.
In agent sessions, the research phase often produces the context that later phases consume, connecting to the intent distribution and orchestration patterns recorded in the [[wiki/web-platforms/supercategories/tooling/categories/shell-cli/intent-distribution-engine-2|Intent Distribution Engine 2]] entry and the [[wiki/web-platforms/supercategories/tooling/categories/shell-cli/index|Shell Cli]] domain.
The entry is a reminder that research is a deliverable, not a delay: recorded findings, sources, and open questions are what later phases build on.
The phase also produces the exit criteria for the next phase: what must be known, decided, or demonstrated before implementation can start, and which risks remain open. Good research ends with a decision memo: the options considered, the recommended path, and the evidence behind each choice.
Research output should be explicit: a written record of the options, evidence, and open questions, so the next phase starts from a shared understanding rather than a fresh guess.
