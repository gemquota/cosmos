# Blind Injection

## Summary
Injection without visible output, inferred through timing or boolean side channels. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- No direct output forces inference via timing, errors, or boolean responses
- Out-of-band channels like DNS callbacks detect blind sinks reliably
- Open question — how do agents detect blind injection during automated testing?
