# Source Dates

## Summary
Source dates record when a source was published or last updated, giving every citation a temporal anchor.

## Details
- Dates matter because technical knowledge decays: a 2018 Android guide and a 2025 one may disagree, and readers need to know which they are reading.
- Source dates are separate from access dates — a source can be published long ago and accessed today.
- For mykb, source dates feed freshness signals and the timeliness score.
