# Bearer Tokens

## Summary
Authorization header tokens whose possession proves identity. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Bearer tokens grant access by possession
- Short lifetimes, scopes, and audience checks limit stolen-token blast radius
- Open question — how do systems revoke bearer tokens before expiry?
