## Summary
HTTP header controlling how much of the referring URL is shared across origins. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Referrer-Policy bounds what URL data leaves the page on navigation
- no-referrer and strict-origin-when-cross-origin are common defaults
