# Timing Attacks

## Summary
Side-channel leaks of secrets through measurable processing-time differences. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Comparisons, padding, and cache state leak secrets through timing
- Constant-time comparison and blinding neutralize measurement
- Open question — can remote timing attacks still recover keys over HTTP/2?
