# Archive Timestamps

## Summary
An archive timestamp records when a snapshot was captured, letting a reader judge whether the archived copy covers the cited claim.

## Details
- The timestamp must be checked when the archive URL is added: a snapshot taken after the claim was written may not support it.
- Timestamps are also the basis of archive quality reports — a source with snapshots only from a single day is fragile.
- For mykb, archive timestamps are validated when archive URLs enter reference blocks.
