# CSP Headers

## Summary
Content-Security-Policy directives that constrain what a page may load or execute. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- CSP directives restrict script, style, frame, and connect sources
- Nonces and hashes replace unsafe-inline for legitimate scripts
- Open question — can CSP become a practical default rather than a hardening layer?
