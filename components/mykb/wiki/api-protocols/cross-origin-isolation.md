# Cross-Origin Isolation

## Summary
COOP and COEP headers enabling shared memory and stronger origin isolation. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- COOP and COEP enable SharedArrayBuffer and stronger isolation
- Cross-origin-isolated pages pay the cost of credentialed cross-origin resources
- Open question — how widely can sites adopt isolation without breaking embeds?
