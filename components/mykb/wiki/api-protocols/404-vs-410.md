# 404 vs 410

## Summary
Not Found versus Gone and what each signals to clients and crawlers. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- 410 Gone signals deliberate removal and stops crawler retries
- 404 is the generic not-found default
- Open question — how long should 410s persist for soft-deleted resources?
