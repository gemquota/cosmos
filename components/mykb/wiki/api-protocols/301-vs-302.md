# 301 vs 302

## Summary
Permanent versus temporary redirects and their caching and method behavior. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- 301s are cached permanently and may change method to GET; 302s are temporary
- 303 and 307/308 refine redirect semantics for POST
- Open question — which redirect code should APIs use for moved resources?
