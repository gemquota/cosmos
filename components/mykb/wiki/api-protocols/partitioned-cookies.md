# Partitioned Cookies

## Summary
CHIPS: scoping cookies to the top-level site that embedded them. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- CHIPS key cookies by top-level site, ending cross-site tracking
- Partitioned cookies preserve embedded-session use cases
- Open question — how does the ecosystem migrate embeds to partitioned storage?
