# Knowledge Synthesis

## Summary
Knowledge synthesis combines evidence and ideas from many sources into conclusions no single source contains — a framework, a comparison, a decision. It is the highest-value step in a knowledge loop and the reason curation exists. In mykb, synthesis pages live in `wiki/syntheses/` and are what RSIS3 reads before acting.

## Details
- **Inputs** — multiple concepts, sources, and questions; the raw material comes from captured notes and linked concepts.
- **Methods** — narrative synthesis, structured comparisons, systematic review protocols, and argument mapping all qualify; the key is explicit linkage back to evidence.
- **Worked example** — synthesizing 'chunking strategies' draws on the chunking, tokenization, and embedding pages, then concludes which chunk size fits mykb notes and why.
- **Quality bar** — a good synthesis states its claims, cites provenance for each, and notes disagreements rather than hiding them.
- **Agent relevance** — RSIS3's L2/L3 improvements (prompt tuning, strategy evolution) are syntheses over past pulse outcomes; the wiki records them as first-class pages.
