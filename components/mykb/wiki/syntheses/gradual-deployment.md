# Gradual Deployment

## Summary
Gradual deployment releases AI capabilities in increasing scope, observing each stage before expanding.

## Details
- Gradual deployment releases AI capabilities in increasing scope, observing each stage before expanding.
- It bounds the blast radius of failures.
- Stages should gate on evidence, not just elapsed time.
- RSIS3 relevance: acquisition passes expand scope only after verification.
