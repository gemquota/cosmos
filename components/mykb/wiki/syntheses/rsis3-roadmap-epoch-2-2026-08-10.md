# RSIS3 roadmap epoch 2 (Phases 51–100)

Epoch 2 — the Age of Living Systems — moves from one lineage of
intelligence to a society of them. It is documented in
`components/rsis3/docs/roadmap-epoch-2.md`; both epochs are recorded in
`rack/epochs.json` (delivered with Phase 50 and extended by Phase 100).

## Sequels (XI–XX)

- **XI — Inter-Intelligence (51–55)**: 51 heterogeneous interoperability
  (quarantine-by-default) · 52 multi-intelligence coordination · 53
  coexistence & containment · 54 negotiation · 55 integration & the
  living system.
- **XII — Economic Agency (56–60)**: 56 machine contracts · 57
  transactions & payments · 58 markets for knowledge & compute · 59
  self-funding & reserves · 60 economic accountability.
- **XIII — Composite Intelligence (61–65)**: 61 composite architecture ·
  62 delegation & trusted subtasking · 63 specialization & expertise
  routing · 64 superorganism coordination · 65 composite identity &
  memory.
- **XIV — Societal Co-Evolution (66–70)**: 66 institutional interfaces ·
  67 social norms & etiquette · 68 participatory policymaking · 69 labor &
  economic co-evolution · 70 societal resilience.
- **XV — Planetary Intelligence (71–75)**: 71 planetary sensing · 72
  earth-system modeling · 73 stewardship actions · 74 restoration &
  remediation · 75 planetary stewardship.
- **XVI — Cultural Intelligence (76–80)**: 76 cultural memory · 77
  cultural transmission · 78 cross-cultural translation · 79 value
  adaptation · 80 cultural flourishing.
- **XVII — Multi-Intelligence Governance (81–85)**: 81 legal standing &
  personhood · 82 rights & obligations · 83 jurisdiction & conflicts of
  law · 84 dispute resolution · 85 constitutional layer.
- **XVIII — Long-Termism (86–90)**: 86 long-horizon planning · 87
  intergenerational equity · 88 preservation protocols · 89 succession &
  handoff · 90 enduring stewardship.
- **XIX — Frontier Intelligence (91–95)**: 91 autonomous exploration · 92
  hostile-environment resilience · 93 deep autonomy · 94 scientific
  discovery · 95 pioneer mission.
- **XX — Transcendent Autonomy (96–100)**: 96 society of minds · 97
  unified intent & diversity · 98 collective self-model · 99 transcendent
  autonomy · 100 enduring intelligence (epoch-2 capstone).

## Durable constraints

- Epoch 2 reuses Epoch 1 primitives as explicit dependencies: memory API
  (6), verification mesh (7), budgets (8), governance (9), self-model
  (10), federation (13), invariants (14), identity (21), commons (42),
  longitudinal metrics (46), endurance (30). Every phase ships telemetry
  events + a dashboard/web UI update — no silent phases (T0).
- The cross-roadmap invariant extends unchanged: autonomy is cumulative
  but never unconditional.
- Phase 12 (collaboration) is tightened to project/capability-scoped
  authorization; Phase 13 (federation) carries explicit provenance/trust
  semantics; Phase 14's verification ledger is the substrate for
  continual invariant attestation.
