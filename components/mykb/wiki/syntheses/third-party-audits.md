# Third-Party Audits

## Summary
Third-party audits are external evaluations of an AI system's safety, fairness, or compliance, conducted by independent experts.

## Details
- Third-party audits are external evaluations of an AI system's safety, fairness, or compliance, conducted by independent experts.
