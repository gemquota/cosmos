# Safety Case Approach

## Summary
The safety case approach documents, in an explicit argument structure, why a system is safe enough to deploy: claims, evidence, and reasoning.

## Details
- The safety case approach documents, in an explicit argument structure, why a system is safe enough to deploy: claims, evidence, and reasoning.
- It borrows from aerospace and nuclear engineering.
- Good cases surface assumptions and make disagreement tractable.
- RSIS3 relevance: pass verification artifacts are a small safety case for the bundle.
