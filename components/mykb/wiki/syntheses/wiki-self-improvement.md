# Wiki Self-Improvement

## Summary
Wiki self-improvement is the practice of a knowledge base refining itself: fixing dead links, filling gaps, merging duplicates, and improving its schema through structured passes. Digital-garden culture and link-graph analytics provide the norms and tooling.

## Details
- **Activities** — gap detection, orphan/duplicate resolution, link repair, and template standardization.
- **Loops** — each acquisition pass both adds knowledge and repairs the graph, so quality compounds.
- **Metrics with care** — link density and page counts are Goodhart-prone; health checks must sample quality, not just count.
- **Culture** — a self-improving wiki is a digital garden: continuously tended rather than published once.
- **RSIS3/mykb relevance** — the cosmos wiki is the concrete case: passes, graph checks, and consolidation syntheses.
