# Coordinated Disclosure

## Summary
Coordinated disclosure is a formal process where researchers, vendors, and platforms agree on timing and content of vulnerability publication.

## Details
- Coordinated disclosure is a formal process where researchers, vendors, and platforms agree on timing and content of vulnerability publication.
- It reduces the harm window while preserving credit.
- Deadlines and goodwill make the process work.
- RSIS3 relevance: multi-worker coordination on shared repos needs the same discipline.
