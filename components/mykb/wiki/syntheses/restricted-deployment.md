# Restricted Deployment

## Summary
Restricted deployment limits a system to approved users, contexts, or capabilities until risks are understood.

## Details
- Restricted deployment limits a system to approved users, contexts, or capabilities until risks are understood.
- Restrictions can be technical (sandboxes) or policy-based (approval).
- Restrictions need periodic review as evidence accumulates.
- RSIS3 relevance: worker scopes and approval gates are restricted deployment.
