# Bug Bounties for AI

## Summary
Bug bounties pay researchers for finding and reporting vulnerabilities, including AI-specific ones like jailbreaks and data leaks.

## Details
- Bug bounties pay researchers for finding and reporting vulnerabilities, including AI-specific ones like jailbreaks and data leaks.
- They scale security testing beyond internal teams.
- Scope, rules, and fair compensation determine effectiveness.
- RSIS3 relevance: incentive-based discovery of check gaps would harden the loop.
