# RSIS3 roadmap epoch 1 (Phases 1–50)

Epoch 1 is the original program: one lineage of intelligence grown from a
production-grade surface to decade-scale maturity. It now spans ten
five-phase sequels across three maturity arcs, documented in
`components/rsis3/docs/roadmap-epoch-1.md`.

## New sequels (VII–X, Phases 31–50)

- **Sequel VII — Generational Autonomy (31–35)**: 31 knowledge
  inheritance · 32 archival immortality · 33 succession planning · 34
  mission continuity · 35 generational resilience.
- **Sequel VIII — Human–AI Symbiosis (36–40)**: 36 explainable autonomy ·
  37 natural-language policy · 38 delegation contracts · 39 trust
  calibration · 40 co-design workspaces.
- **Sequel IX — Global Commons (41–45)**: 41 cross-ecosystem standards ·
  42 global knowledge commons · 43 inter-population diplomacy · 44 crisis
  response · 45 planetary stewardship.
- **Sequel X — Meta-Science (46–50)**: 46 self-metrics & longitudinal
  studies · 47 hypothesis-driven self-experimentation · 48 failure
  understanding · 49 formal meta-invariant proof · 50 epoch capstone:
  enduring intelligence.

## Maturity arcs (Epoch 1)

- 1–5 Operational Autonomy · 6–10 Governed Intelligence · 11–15
  Distributed Autonomy · 16–20 Open Autonomy · 21–25 Ecosystem Autonomy ·
  26–30 Sovereign Autonomy · 31–35 Generational Autonomy · 36–40 Human–AI
  Symbiosis · 41–45 Global Commons · 46–50 Meta-Science.

## Durable constraints (new)

- **Cross-roadmap invariant** (now first law of the epoch): autonomy is
  cumulative but never unconditional — every expansion of capability
  inherits the memory, verification, cost, policy, provenance and
  observability controls established by preceding phases.
- **Standing telemetry & dashboard contract** (every phase, every sequel):
  each phase ships (1) new telemetry events registered in the contract
  and surfaced in `ecosystem.json` / `dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a
  dashboard/web UI update making the phase's output visible — no silent
  phases (T0).
- Epoch 1 sequels reuse the Phase 6 memory API, Phase 7 verification
  mesh, Phase 8 budgets, Phase 9 governance and Phase 14 invariants as
  explicit dependencies — the sequels are cumulative, never parallel.
- Phase 5 status distinction: implementation delivered vs 7-day live
  exit validation pending (same for 6–15).
