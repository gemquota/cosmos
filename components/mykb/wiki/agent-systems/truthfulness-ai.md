# Truthfulness in AI

## Summary
Truthful AI reports accurate information and acknowledges uncertainty, avoiding fabrication and sycophancy.

## Details
- Truthful AI reports accurate information and acknowledges uncertainty, avoiding fabrication and sycophancy.
- Truthfulness research covers calibration, factuality, and refusal.
- It is distinct from helpfulness: truthful systems may say 'I don't know'.
