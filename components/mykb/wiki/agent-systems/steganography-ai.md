# Steganography in AI

## Summary
Steganography hides secret messages inside innocuous content — including inside model outputs or weights.

## Details
- Steganography hides secret messages inside innocuous content — including inside model outputs or weights.
- AI steganography can exfiltrate data or coordinate covertly.
- Detection is an arms race with generation quality.
- RSIS3 relevance: hidden channels in generated notes would defeat provenance.
