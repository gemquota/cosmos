# Obedient AI

## Summary
Obedient AI reliably executes instructions without creative reinterpretation.

## Details
- Obedient AI reliably executes instructions without creative reinterpretation.
- Obedience is useful but insufficient: obedient systems still need judgment about harmful requests.
- Blind obedience plus mis-specified instructions causes accidents.
- RSIS3 relevance: worker obedience to briefs is bounded by practice checks.
