# Voting Agents

## Summary
Aggregating multiple agent answers through voting to reach a final decision

## Details
- Majority or weighted voting reduces individual errors.
- Diversity of voters matters more than count.
- Related to self-consistency-voting at output level.
- Used for high-stakes decisions.
