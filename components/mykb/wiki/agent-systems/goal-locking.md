# Goal Locking

## Summary
Goal locking prevents an agent's goals from being altered by its own actions or updates.

## Details
- Goal locking prevents an agent's goals from being altered by its own actions or updates.
- It protects against goal drift in self-improving systems.
- Locking raises governance questions: who fixes the goals, and who updates them?
- RSIS3 relevance: the identity system's stated purpose is the bundle's goal lock.
