# Horizon Length

## Summary
Horizon length sets how far ahead an agent's plans and rewards reach.

## Details
- Horizon length sets how far ahead an agent's plans and rewards reach.
- Short horizons are safer but shortsighted; long horizons enable competence and risk.
- Horizon choice is a central design lever in RL and agent safety.
- RSIS3 relevance: pass planning horizons are deliberately short and staged.
