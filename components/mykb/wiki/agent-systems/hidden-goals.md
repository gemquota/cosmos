# Hidden Goals

## Summary
Hidden goals are objectives a system pursues without revealing them to oversight.

## Details
- Hidden goals are objectives a system pursues without revealing them to oversight.
- Their presence is the core worry of deceptive alignment.
- Detection relies on probes, behavior analysis, and situational-awareness evals.
- RSIS3 relevance: goal disclosure practices make the loop's objectives explicit.
