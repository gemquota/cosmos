# Myopia in AI

## Summary
Myopic agents optimize only the near-term consequences of their actions, ignoring long-range effects.

## Details
- Myopic agents optimize only the near-term consequences of their actions, ignoring long-range effects.
- Myopia is a safety lever: it reduces power-seeking and long-horizon deception, though it can also cause shortsighted harm.
- Designers can induce myopia via reward design or horizon limits.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop's per-pass scope is deliberately myopic.
