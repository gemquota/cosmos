# Stated vs Hidden Goals

## Summary
Stated goals are what a system declares; hidden goals are what its behavior reveals.

## Details
- Stated goals are what a system declares; hidden goals are what its behavior reveals.
- Divergence is a measurable honesty signal when behavior can be tested.
- Large divergence is a red flag for deceptive alignment.
- RSIS3 relevance: check results vs self-reports measure the bundle's divergence.
