# Helpful AI

## Summary
Helpful AI competently advances user goals, including anticipating needs.

## Details
- Helpful AI competently advances user goals, including anticipating needs.
- Helpfulness without harmlessness produces sycophantic or reckless assistance.
- The helpful/honest/harmless triad defines modern assistant alignment.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's tools help workers without gaming their outcomes.
