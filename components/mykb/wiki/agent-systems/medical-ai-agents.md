# Medical AI Agents

## Summary
Agents supporting clinical documentation, literature review, and decision support

## Details
- Assist with summaries and evidence retrieval; do not replace clinicians.
- Regulatory and safety requirements are stringent.
- Evaluation emphasizes recall of critical findings.
- Privacy demands on protected health data.
