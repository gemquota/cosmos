# Triage Workflow

## Summary
Triage is the intake process that turns raw incoming items — requests, flags, reports — into routed, prioritized, tracked work.

## Details
- Every item passes through a small decision tree: is it valid, is it already covered, who owns it, how urgent is it.
- Triage fails when items stall in the intake state; the workflow needs a timebox and an escalation path.
- For mykb, triage is the front door of the review queues and connects requests-pages to task-lists-wiki.
