# Research Agents

## Summary
Agents that gather, synthesize, and cite information across many sources

## Details
- Plan queries, browse or retrieve, then synthesize with citations.
- Quality depends on source reliability and verification.
- Hallucination risk drives grounding requirements.
- Support grounded-generation and citations-and-provenance.
