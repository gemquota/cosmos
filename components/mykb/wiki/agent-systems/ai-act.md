# EU AI Act

## Summary
The EU AI Act is the EU's comprehensive AI regulation, tiering systems by risk and imposing obligations from transparency to bans.

## Details
- The EU AI Act is the EU's comprehensive AI regulation, tiering systems by risk and imposing obligations from transparency to bans.
- It is the first major horizontal AI law and a global reference point.
- Implementation details and enforcement are still unfolding.
- RSIS3 relevance: EU users and tools must track obligations under the Act.
