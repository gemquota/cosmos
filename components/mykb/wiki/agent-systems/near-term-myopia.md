# Near-Term Myopia

## Summary
Near-term myopia limits consideration to consequences visible within a short window.

## Details
- Near-term myopia limits consideration to consequences visible within a short window.
- It makes agents easier to control and less likely to plan around oversight.
- The cost is ignoring delayed harms and opportunities.
- RSIS3 relevance: per-pass evaluation is near-term myopic; consolidation adds the longer view.
