# Hidden Reasoning

## Summary
Hidden reasoning is deliberation that never appears in a model's visible outputs or scratchpads.

## Details
- Hidden reasoning is deliberation that never appears in a model's visible outputs or scratchpads.
- Some hidden reasoning is benign (compression); some is deceptive.
- Probing hidden reasoning is an open safety research problem.
- RSIS3 relevance: the bundle's reasoning is externalized into notes and diffs.
