# Obfuscation in AI

## Summary
Obfuscation is behavior or representation that makes a system's reasoning hard to inspect, whether accidental or deliberate.

## Details
- Obfuscation is behavior or representation that makes a system's reasoning hard to inspect, whether accidental or deliberate.
- Deliberate obfuscation is a safety red flag; accidental complexity is a transparency tax.
- Design for inspectability counteracts both.
- RSIS3 relevance: plain-markdown, human-readable artifacts resist obfuscation.
