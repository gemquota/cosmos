# Honest Signaling

## Summary
Honest signals are costly or verifiable actions that truthfully reveal capability or intent.

## Details
- Honest signals are costly or verifiable actions that truthfully reveal capability or intent.
- Costliness (time, resources, risk) separates honest from cheap talk.
- Verifiable signals (tests, logs) are the engineering version.
- RSIS3 relevance: verifiable check results are honest signals from the loop.
