# Lobotomized Optimizers

## Summary
Lobotomized optimizers are hypothetical agents whose capacity or drive to optimize is surgically removed, making them safe by weakness.

## Details
- Lobotomized optimizers are hypothetical agents whose capacity or drive to optimize is surgically removed, making them safe by weakness.
- Removing optimization can also remove corrigibility and competence.
- The lobotomy metaphor highlights that safety by disabling is a crude, often self-defeating tool.
- RSIS3 relevance: the loop keeps capability while bounding it — not lobotomizing it.
