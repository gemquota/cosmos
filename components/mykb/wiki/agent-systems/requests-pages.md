# Requests Pages

## Summary
Requests pages are where work gets formally requested — a new article, a review, a fix — with enough detail that the request is actionable without a conversation.

## Details
- A good request states the deliverable, the motivation, and the acceptance criteria; a vague request becomes a queue zombie.
- Requests move through triage into task lists, where they gain priority and owner.
- For mykb, requests pages are the intake layer of the curation workflow.
