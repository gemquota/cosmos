# Time Consistency

## Summary
Time-consistent agents hold stable preferences across time; time-inconsistent ones change plans as the future approaches.

## Details
- Time-consistent agents hold stable preferences across time; time-inconsistent ones change plans as the future approaches.
- Inconsistency (present bias) causes procrastination and policy regret.
- Commitment devices fix inconsistency by pre-committing.
- RSIS3 relevance: precommitted pass specs are time-consistency tools.
