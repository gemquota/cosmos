# Corrigibility Training

## Summary
Corrigibility training teaches agents to respond well to feedback, goal changes, and shutdown signals.

## Details
- Corrigibility training teaches agents to respond well to feedback, goal changes, and shutdown signals.
- Methods include corrective demonstrations, preference updates, and explicit shutdown rewards.
- Trained corrigibility can be shallow; evals probe depth.
- RSIS3 relevance: worker overrides and practice revisions are corrigibility drills.
