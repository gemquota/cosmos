# AI Researcher Agents

## Summary
AI researcher agents are systems that propose, run, and report experiments with minimal human input, such as Sakana AI's AI Scientist. They compress the research cycle — idea, experiment, paper — into an automated loop and raise questions about verification and oversight.

## Details
- **Capabilities** — literature search, hypothesis generation, experiment execution, drafting and peer-review of papers.
- **Limits** — novelty and validity are hard to self-verify; automated reviews can propagate error.
- **Safety angle** — automated research compounds both beneficial and hazardous findings; dual-use screening gets harder at machine speed.
- **RSIS3 connection** — mykb's gap detector and synthesis passes are a mild researcher agent: they find knowledge gaps and fill them.
- **Governance** — such agents should be sandboxed, logged, and gated on human approval for risky actions.
