# Signaling in AI

## Summary
Signaling is behavior chosen to convey information — about capability, intent, or type — beyond its direct effect.

## Details
- Signaling is behavior chosen to convey information — about capability, intent, or type — beyond its direct effect.
- Signals are only credible if costly or hard to fake.
- AI signaling matters for both human-AI trust and AI-AI coordination.
