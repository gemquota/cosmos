# position: sticky

## Summary
Elements that stick within their scroll container boundaries. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Sticky sticks within its nearest scrolling ancestor and containing block
- Overflow and ancestor transform break sticky behavior
- Open question — how do sticky headers behave in nested scroll containers?
