# vw and vh Units

## Summary
Viewport-relative length units and their pitfalls. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- vw and vh track the viewport; vmin and vmax take extremes
- Mobile URL bars made vh jumpy, motivating svh and dvh
- Open question — how do dvh values settle after browser chrome settles?
