# Contrast Ratios

## Summary
WCAG luminance contrast for text legibility. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- WCAG requires roughly 4.5:1 for normal text
- Ratios compute from relative luminance, not color difference
- Open question — how does large-text contrast differ from normal?
