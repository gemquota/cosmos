# Icon Fonts

## Summary
Rendering icons as glyphs, with their trade-offs versus SVG. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Icon fonts render glyphs but lack multi-color and precise metrics
- Inline SVG or sprites give better accessibility and crispness
- Open question — when do icon fonts still win in 2026?
