# sRGB vs Display P3

## Summary
Wide-gamut color and browser support differences. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Display P3 covers about 25% more colors than sRGB
- Browsers on wide-gamut screens render P3 colors
- Open question — how do gradients interpolate across spaces?
