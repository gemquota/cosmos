# Dynamic and Small Viewport Units

## Summary
Handling mobile browser chrome with dvh and svh. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- svh is the small viewport; dvh animates with browser chrome
- dvh fixes full-height mobile layouts but can jump
- Open question — when should svh win for stability?
