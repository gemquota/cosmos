# XS-Leaks

## Summary
Cross-site information leaks through browser side channels. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Side channels like window length and error events leak cross-origin state
- COOP and cross-origin isolation reduce leak surfaces
