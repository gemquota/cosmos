# Color Blind Accessibility

## Summary
Designing palettes that work without relying on hue alone. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Red-green confusion is the most common color-vision deficiency
- Shape, pattern, and label encoding must back color alone
- Open question — how do data-viz palettes validate for CVD?
