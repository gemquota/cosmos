# Dark Mode Practice

## Summary
Implementing themeable dark interfaces without jarring contrast. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- Dark UIs need lowered luminance, not inverted colors
- Tokens, not hard-coded values, make theming maintainable
- Open question — how does dark mode affect battery on OLED?
