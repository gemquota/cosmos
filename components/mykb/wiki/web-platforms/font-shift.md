# FOIT and Font Shift

## Summary
Layout jank caused by webfont loading and swapping. A stub in the mykb wiki that frames the concept and the questions to expand into a full article.

## Details
- FOIT hides text; FOUT swaps metrics; both can shift layout
- size-adjust and metric overrides align fallback fonts
- Open question — how does font matching affect CLS scoring?
