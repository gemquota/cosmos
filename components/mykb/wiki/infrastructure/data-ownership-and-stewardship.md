# Data Ownership and Stewardship

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Owners answer for quality, access, and lifecycle of their data products.
- Stewards operationalize: metadata, issue triage, and user support.
- Ownership needs to be explicit in the catalog, not implicit.
- RACI-style clarity prevents governance theater.
