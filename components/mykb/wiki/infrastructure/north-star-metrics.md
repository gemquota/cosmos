# North Star Metrics

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- The north star metric measures the value a product delivers, not just activity.
- It unifies teams around one direction; guardrails protect the rest.
- Examples: weekly active listeners, completed rides, messages sent.
- Choose carefully; the wrong north star distorts the whole company.
