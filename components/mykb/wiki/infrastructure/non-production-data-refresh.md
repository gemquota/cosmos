A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Refresh policies balance freshness against privacy and cost.
- Mask or anonymize before copying production data.
- Automate refresh on a schedule with validation after load.
- Document what each environment contains and how stale it is.
