# On-Demand vs Reserved Compute

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- On-demand scales with usage but costs more per unit.
- Reserved/slot-based models discount for committed, predictable workloads.
- Hybrid: baseline reserved capacity plus on-demand overflow.
- Forecast from utilization history; right-size before committing.
