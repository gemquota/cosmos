# Nacos and Service Registry

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Nacos provides service registration/discovery, configuration management, and DNS-based naming.
- It is widely used in Alibaba-style microservice stacks (Spring Cloud Alibaba).
- Health checks and weight-based routing support blue-green and canary traffic splits.
- Compared to Consul/etcd: stronger config focus and Chinese ecosystem adoption.
