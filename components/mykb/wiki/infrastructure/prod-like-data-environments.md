# Prod-Like Data Environments

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Prod-like environments reproduce volumes, distributions, and edge cases.
- Sampling must preserve skew and rare values that break queries.
- Synthetic generation can reach parity where copies are restricted.
- Parity prevents 'works in staging' incidents.
