# Third-Party Data Sourcing

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Assess license terms, quality, freshness, and provenance before adoption.
- Vendor data needs the same governance as internal data.
- Validate against internal truth; third-party data often drifts.
- Document usage rights so downstream consumers stay compliant.
