## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Names encode entity, measure, and grain: orders_placed_daily_count.
- Avoid synonyms (users vs customers) by registering canonical terms.
- Conventions make metrics discoverable and comparable.
- Enforce with linting in semantic layers and catalog tooling.
