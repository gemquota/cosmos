# Data Maturity Models

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Maturity models (Level 1-5) score data management across dimensions.
- Dimensions: governance, quality, architecture, talent, culture.
- Assessments target the next bottleneck, not the highest level.
- Maturity is a diagnostic, not a badge.
