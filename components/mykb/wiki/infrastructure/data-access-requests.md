# Data Access Requests

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Formal requests capture who needs what, why, and for how long.
- Approval flows route to owners; grants expire by default.
- Automate provisioning and logging; audit regularly.
- Make access requests fast; slow access drives shadow IT.
