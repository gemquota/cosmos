## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Runbooks document detection, diagnosis, and remediation for known failures.
- Include escalation paths, rollback steps, and validation after fix.
- Practice runbooks in game days; untested runbooks fail under stress.
- Post-incident reviews feed new runbooks.
