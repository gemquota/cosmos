## Summary

## Details

- Models: central platform team, embedded analysts, domain pods, or hub-and-spoke.
- Centralization standardizes; decentralization speeds domain work.
- Platform teams serve; product teams consume with guardrails.
- Topology should follow the organization's coupling, not fashion.
