# Data Anonymization Techniques

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Anonymization removes or perturbs identifiers so individuals cannot be re-identified from the released data.
- Techniques include generalization, suppression, perturbation, k-anonymity, l-diversity, and differential privacy.
- Truly anonymous data is hard: auxiliary data can re-identify; pseudonymization (reversible) is not anonymization.
- Differential privacy adds calibrated noise and is the strongest formal guarantee for aggregates.
