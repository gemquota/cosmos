# Data License and Usage

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Data has licenses: open (ODbL, CC), commercial, or internal use-only.
- Track provenance and terms per dataset, including redistribution limits.
- License metadata belongs in the catalog next to the data.
- Using data beyond its license is a legal risk, not just a policy one.
