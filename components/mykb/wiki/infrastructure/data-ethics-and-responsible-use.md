# Data Ethics and Responsible Use

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Ethical data use goes beyond legal compliance: consent, fairness, transparency, and avoiding harm.
- Bias audits, explainability, and human oversight reduce the risk of discriminatory analytics or ML.
- Document decisions: purpose limitation, data minimization, and impact assessments for sensitive uses.
- RSIS3-style reflection fits here: periodically reviewing what the knowledge graph records about people and why.
