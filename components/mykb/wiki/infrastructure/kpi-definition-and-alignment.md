# KPI Definition and Alignment

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- KPIs translate strategy into measurable targets teams can act on.
- Good KPIs are specific, owned, time-bound, and hard to game.
- Align cascading KPIs: company, team, and individual metrics connect.
- Review KPI health regularly; stale KPIs misdirect effort.
