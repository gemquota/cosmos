# Data Sharing Agreements

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Agreements cover purpose, scope, retention, and security requirements.
- They apply to internal cross-team sharing too, not just external.
- Technical controls (access, lineage, audit) must match the terms.
- Breach of sharing terms is a legal and trust risk.
