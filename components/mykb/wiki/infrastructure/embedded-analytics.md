## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Embedded analytics delivers charts and dashboards inside customer products.
- Architecture: embed SDK, headless BI APIs, or iframe BI tools.
- Row-level security per end user is the hard requirement.
- It turns data products into product features.
