# Goal-Based Metrics

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Goal frameworks (OKRs, targets) attach measurable outcomes to objectives.
- Metrics should be definable before the goal is set.
- Track progress and trajectory, not just final attainment.
- Review cadence keeps goals honest and current.
