# Data Marketplaces

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Internal marketplaces catalog datasets with quality and ownership metadata.
- External marketplaces sell or share licensed datasets.
- Good marketplaces make discovery, request, and access self-service.
- Curate quality signals; a marketplace full of junk is ignored.
