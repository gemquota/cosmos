# Compliance and Audit Trails

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Audit trails capture access, change, and admin events with actor, timestamp, object, and outcome.
- They support investigations, access reviews, and regulator evidence for frameworks like SOC 2 and SOX.
- Tamper-evident storage, log retention, and alerting on anomalies make trails trustworthy.
- Balancing detail and cost: sample fine-grained access logs, keep policy-relevant events complete.
