# Data Privacy: GDPR and CCPA

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- GDPR (EU) grants rights: access, rectification, erasure ('right to be forgotten'), portability, and consent controls.
- CCPA/CPRA (California) focuses on disclosure, opt-out of sale/sharing, and deletion rights for consumers.
- Both require inventory of personal data, lawful basis or purpose tracking, and documented retention limits.
- Engineering impact: PII tagging, lineage for erasure, masking in dev, and contracts with processors.
