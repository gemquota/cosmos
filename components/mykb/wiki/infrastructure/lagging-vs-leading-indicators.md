# Lagging vs Leading Indicators

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Lagging indicators report outcomes (revenue, churn) after the fact.
- Leading indicators predict future outcomes (usage, engagement, pipeline).
- Lead with leading indicators for control; report lagging for truth.
- Map the causal chain between them explicitly.
