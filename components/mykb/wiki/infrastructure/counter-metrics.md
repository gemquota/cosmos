# Counter Metrics

## Summary

A stub to be expanded into a full article; frames the concept and open questions.

## Details

- Counter metrics measure costs or negatives: spam reports, refunds, churn.
- They balance incentives to optimize a primary metric.
- A good decision rule weights primary vs counter metrics explicitly.
- Track them per experiment and at the platform level.
