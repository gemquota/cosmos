# Android TV

Android TV targets the ten-foot UI: focus-based navigation, leanback components, and playback-focused experiences. Apps declare a leanback launcher intent and use fragments for browsing and playback.
- Focus navigation replaces touch; use D-pad movement and focused states.
- Media playback integrates with Cast and the media session framework.
- Android TV is now part of the broader Google TV platform.
- Test on the TV emulator and with remote-style input.
