# Verify Days

## Summary
Verify days are calendar blocks for re-checking the wiki's evidence layer: source URLs, citation-claim matches, and verification-needed flags.

## Details
- They are the human half of verification — automation finds the candidates, humans judge them.
- A verify day is planned from the verification-needed queue and ends with the queue measurably shorter.
- For mykb, verify days feed the verifiability-score and clear unverified flags.
