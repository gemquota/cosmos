# Instant Apps

Instant apps let users run a trimmed app experience from a link without installing, powered by Google Play Instant and dynamic delivery. They are the no-install step before committing to an app.
- Instant-enabled app bundles build a downloadable instant experience.
- Launch URLs come from deep links, QR codes, or Play listings.
- Instant apps cannot use all APIs; test on the instant runtime.
- Great for first-run demos and low-commitment acquisition.
