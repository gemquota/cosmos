# Author Credentials

## Summary
Author credentials ask who wrote the material and what qualifies them: direct involvement with the subject, a publication record, or a role that makes them accountable.

## Details
- For technical documentation the author matters less than the maintaining organization; for essays and analyses the author is nearly everything.
- Credentials should be verified when they are load-bearing — an anonymous how-to on a random blog needs more scrutiny than one by a named maintainer.
- For mykb, author credentials feed source-vetting and the reputation score.
