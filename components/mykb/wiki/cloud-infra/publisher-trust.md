# Publisher Trust

## Summary
Publisher trust assesses the organization that produced the material: its editorial process, correction history, and record on the specific topic.

## Details
- A publisher with a transparent review process and a habit of correcting errors earns trust that a content farm cannot match.
- Publisher trust is contextual — a publisher can be authoritative on one topic and unreliable on another.
- For mykb, publisher trust is recorded during source-vetting and reviewed when a source is re-scheduled.
