# Source Vetting

## Summary
Source vetting is the up-front judgment a citation deserves: who publishes it, what is their track record, is the content primary or derivative, and does it support the claim precisely?

## Details
- Vetting is not about prestige — a well-maintained blog can beat a stale official page for a fast-moving topic — it is about reliability.
- The vetting outcome is recorded (trust class, reason) so later curators inherit the decision.
- For mykb, source vetting precedes promotion: every full article's sources pass the trust bar before the status flips.
