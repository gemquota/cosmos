# OCR and Document AI

## Summary
Extracting text and structure from scanned documents and images

## Details
- Pipeline: detect layout, recognize text, parse tables and forms.
- Modern systems use vision-language-models end to end.
- Accuracy matters for legal and financial records.
- Feeds retrieval-augmented-generation pipelines.
