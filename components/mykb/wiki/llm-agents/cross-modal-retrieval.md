# Cross-Modal Retrieval

## Summary
Searching across modalities, such as finding images from text or video from audio

## Details
- Shared embedding spaces align different modalities.
- Enables text-to-image, image-to-text, and similar queries.
- Alignment quality limits retrieval accuracy.
- Related to embeddings-alignment research.
