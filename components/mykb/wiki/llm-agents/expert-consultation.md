# Expert Consultation

## Summary
Expert consultation is asking a specialist — a domain-tuned agent, a knowledge tool, a human — for an opinion before committing. It matters because one generalist agent cannot be expert everywhere. It is delegation in question form.

## Details
- Triggered when confidence is low or domain risk is high.
- The consultant returns an answer plus reasoning and sources.
- Complements debate: cooperative rather than adversarial.
- Open questions: choosing the right expert and trusting its answer.
