Bond angle is first a chemistry concept: the angle between two bonds that share a common atom. Molecular geometry follows from these angles, and the VSEPR model predicts them from electron-pair repulsion — approximately 180 degrees for linear geometries, 120 for trigonal planar, 109.5 for tetrahedral, and about 104.5 for water's bent shape. Hybridization refines the picture: sp, sp2, and sp3 carbon centers produce linear, planar, and tetrahedral arrangements respectively.
