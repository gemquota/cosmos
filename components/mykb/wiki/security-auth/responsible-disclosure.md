# Responsible Disclosure

- Responsible disclosure coordinates a fix window: researchers report privately, vendors patch, then details are published.
- Typical timelines (e.g. 90 days) balance researcher credit against vendor risk of partial fixes.
- Well-run programs publish security advisories and thank researchers, building trust and repeat reporting.
- For mykb: a documented security contact and disclosure policy make external reports actionable instead of scary.
