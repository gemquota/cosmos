# Mobile Device Management

- MDM enforces organizational policy on phones and tablets: passcodes, encryption, app allowlists, remote wipe, and compliance checks.
- It feeds device posture into access decisions — a managed, compliant device is trusted more than a jailbroken one.
- Enrollment and privacy trade-offs are sensitive: employers gain visibility while users retain personal space.
- For mykb: MDM compliance signals can be an environment attribute in zero-trust access decisions.
