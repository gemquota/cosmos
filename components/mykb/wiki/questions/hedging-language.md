# Hedging Language

## Summary

Hedging Language — Words and phrases that qualify the strength of claims.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Hedges ('suggests', 'may', 'probably') mark epistemic modality.
- Over-hedging hides signal; under-hedging overstates confidence.
- Calibrated hedging is a skill for reports and forecasts.
- Stylistic norms vary by field and audience.
