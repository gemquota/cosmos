# Uncertainty Communication

## Summary

Uncertainty Communication — Conveying probability and confidence to audiences accurately.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Verbal probability terms are interpreted inconsistently; numeric formats are clearer.
- Visualization (error bars, intervals, fan charts) helps when designed well.
- Communicators face pressure toward false precision.
- Relevant to forecasts, science communication, and AI system disclosures.
