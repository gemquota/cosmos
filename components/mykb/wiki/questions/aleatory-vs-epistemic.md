# Aleatory vs Epistemic Uncertainty

## Summary

Aleatory vs Epistemic Uncertainty — Distinguishing irreducible randomness from reducible ignorance.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Aleatory: inherent stochasticity of the world; epistemic: our lack of knowledge.
- Better models and data reduce epistemic uncertainty only.
- The boundary is model-dependent and can shift.
- Guides whether to invest in measurement or in robustness.
