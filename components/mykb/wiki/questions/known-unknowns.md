# Known Unknowns

## Summary

Known Unknowns — Gaps we are aware of and can formulate as questions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Rumsfeld's taxonomy: known knowns, known unknowns, unknown unknowns.
- Known unknowns are tractable: they can be searched, tested, and resolved.
- Their count is a useful metric of inquiry health.
- Contrast with unknown unknowns that hide until surprise.
