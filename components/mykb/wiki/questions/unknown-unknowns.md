# Unknown Unknowns

## Summary

Unknown Unknowns — Absences of knowledge so complete we cannot even formulate them.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Unknown unknowns are discovered only through surprise or failure.
- Red-teaming, adversarial review, and broad exploration raise their visibility.
- They motivate humility in planning and forecasting.
- Risk management distinguishes them from known risks.
