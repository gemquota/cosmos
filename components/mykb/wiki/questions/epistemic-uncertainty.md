# Epistemic Uncertainty

## Summary

Epistemic Uncertainty — Uncertainty that stems from limited knowledge rather than inherent randomness.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Epistemic uncertainty shrinks with better measurement and evidence.
- Distinguished from aleatory uncertainty, which is irreducible randomness.
- Forecasting and science concentrate on reducing it.
- Central to Bayesian updating and decision theory.
