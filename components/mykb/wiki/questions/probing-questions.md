# Probing Questions

## Summary

Probing Questions — Follow-up questions that push beneath surface answers.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Probes ask for evidence, examples, and mechanisms behind an answer.
- Sequencing probes from general to specific builds understanding.
- Frequent in interviews, tutorials, and debugging.
- Distinguish from leading questions that smuggle in the answer.
