# Essential Questions Design

## Summary

Essential Questions Design — Framing curriculum around open questions that organize inquiry.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Essential questions are open, arguable, and recur across the subject.
- They frame units and give inquiry a destination.
- Wiggins & McTighe's Understanding by Design popularized them.
- Contrast with factual recall questions.
