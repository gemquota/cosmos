# Question Design

## Summary

Question Design — Crafting questions that elicit the intended thinking or evidence.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Good questions specify scope, format, and the reasoning required.
- Avoid ambiguity, negative phrasing, and unintended cues.
- Design pairs with cognitive targets: recall, transfer, synthesis.
- Foundational to formative assessment and tutoring systems.
