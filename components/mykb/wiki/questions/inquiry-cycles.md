# Inquiry Cycles

## Summary

Inquiry Cycles — Iterative loops of questioning, investigation, and reflection.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Cycles: ask, investigate, interpret, conclude, ask again.
- Structure scaffolds open inquiry without dictating answers.
- Reflection closes the loop and generates the next question.
- Model for knowledge acquisition systems and RSIS3-style loops.
