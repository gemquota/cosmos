## Summary

Question Generation — Producing questions to drive inquiry, testing, and learning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

- AI can propose questions, but learner ownership boosts the benefit.
- Links question design to curiosity-driven learning.
