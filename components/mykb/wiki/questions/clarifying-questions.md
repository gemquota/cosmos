# Clarifying Questions

## Summary

Clarifying Questions — Questions that pin down meaning, scope, and definitions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Clarifying questions reduce ambiguity before judgment.
- Useful in meetings, requirements gathering, and research.
- Phrase neutrally to avoid embedding assumptions.
- First line of defense against reasoning on misread premises.
