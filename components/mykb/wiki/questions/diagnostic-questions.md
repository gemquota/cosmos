# Diagnostic Questions

## Summary

Diagnostic Questions — Questions designed to reveal misconceptions and understanding states.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Each option in a multiple-choice diagnostic maps to a known misconception.
- Answers reveal the learner's mental model, not just correctness.
- Used by formative assessment and cognitive diagnosis models.
- Feedback can then target the specific misconception.
