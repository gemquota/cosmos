# Socratic Questioning

## Summary

Socratic Questioning — Dialectical questioning that draws out and examines assumptions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Named for Socrates: questions that expose contradictions and prompt self-examination.
- Types: clarification, probing assumptions, reasons and evidence, implications.
- Effective when genuine and curious, not performative or leading.
- Widely used in law, therapy, and tutoring.
