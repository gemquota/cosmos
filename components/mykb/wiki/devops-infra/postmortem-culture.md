# Postmortem Culture

## Summary
A blameless postmortem analyzes what happened, why, and what changes prevent recurrence — without blaming individuals. The culture around them decides whether incidents become learning or fear.

## Details
- Structure: timeline, impact, root cause, contributing factors, action items with owners.
- Blameless framing ('what in the system allowed this') keeps engineers honest about details.
- Action items must land: tracked, prioritized, and closed, or the ritual decays.
- Open question: how to make postmortems effective without becoming bureaucracy.
