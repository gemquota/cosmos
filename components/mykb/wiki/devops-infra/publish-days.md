# Publish Days

## Summary
Publish days are when finished content goes out — the export, the web build, the synced bundle — separating 'done editing' from 'published'.

## Details
- Publishing is a release engineering step: build, verify, deploy, announce, with rollback if something breaks.
- The publish checklist is what makes the distinction safe; without it, publishing is just editing harder.
- For mykb, publish days follow release days and push the verified bundle to its consumers.
