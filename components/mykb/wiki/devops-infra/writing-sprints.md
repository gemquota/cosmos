# Writing Sprints

## Summary
Writing sprints are focused sessions for producing article drafts, usually on a chosen batch of stubs or a topic cluster.

## Details
- They succeed when writing is separated from reviewing: a sprint writes, the next sprint reviews, so neither activity crowds the other.
- Each draft should target the promotion bar so the writing sprint feeds directly into review queues.
- For mykb, writing sprints are how stub batches get promoted during curation waves.
