# Wiki Hackathons

## Summary
Wiki hackathons are intensive events where many contributors work on the wiki together — new article clusters, tooling, or graph improvements.

## Details
- They convert idle ambition into shipped work by pairing focus, time, and collaboration.
- A hackathon needs a prepared project list; unplanned hackathons produce social energy and little durable output.
- For mykb, hackathons are the event form of content-camps, usually aimed at acquisition or tooling backlogs.
