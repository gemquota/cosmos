# Cleanup Days

## Summary
Cleanup days are calendar blocks for the mechanical tidy work — formatting, list consistency, broken tables — flagged as cleanup-needed.

## Details
- The work is low-risk and batchable, which makes it perfect for new editors and quick sessions.
- Cleanup days are planned from the linting report and close when the flagged items are resolved or triaged up.
- For mykb, cleanup days feed the markdown-linting backlog and keep the corpus readable.
