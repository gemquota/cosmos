# Link-Fix Sprints

## Summary
Link-fix sprints are focused sessions on the link graph: broken links repaired, reciprocals added, stale targets updated after renames.

## Details
- The work is driven by the broken-link and one-way-link reports, so the sprint starts with a list, not a search.
- Automation (link-fix-automation) handles the mechanical bulk; humans decide the ambiguous cases.
- For mykb, link-fix sprints keep graph-density metrics healthy and are a recurring maintenance-days activity.
