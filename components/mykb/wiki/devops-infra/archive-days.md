# Archive Days

## Summary
Archive days are calendar blocks for moving retired content to the archive: demoted articles, superseded pages, and completed merges.

## Details
- The work follows archival-criteria and leaves the provenance trail intact.
- Archive days are also when archive URLs get refreshed for sources that need them.
- For mykb, archive days keep the active wiki focused and the full-article-ratio honest.
