# Content Camps

## Summary
Content camps are focused events where a team produces content on one theme — a domain, a format, a gap in coverage.

## Details
- The theme is chosen from gap analysis, so a camp closes a known hole instead of adding random pages.
- Camps mix writing, sourcing, and linking, and they end with a verification pass.
- For mykb, content camps are the event form of acquisition waves and produce seed clusters for later promotion.
