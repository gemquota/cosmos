# Refresh Days

## Summary
Refresh days are calendar blocks for updating content that has aged: version numbers, changed APIs, outdated examples.

## Details
- Refresh work comes from the freshness report, which ranks articles by how far past their review date they are.
- Refreshing is faster than rewriting when the structure is sound — the day should clear many items.
- For mykb, refresh days pair with update-days and are driven by the timeliness-score.
