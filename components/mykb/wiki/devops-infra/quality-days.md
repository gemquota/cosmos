# Quality Days

## Summary
Quality days are scheduled calendar blocks devoted to quality work — promoting stubs, adding examples, improving summaries — chosen from the health dashboard.

## Details
- They are recurring, so quality work does not depend on spare time; the calendar protects it.
- A quality day has a target metric (promote N stubs, clear M flags) so its effect is visible.
- For mykb, quality days are where promotion-readiness work actually happens.
