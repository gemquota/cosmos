# Curation Sprints

## Summary
Curation sprints are sessions that run the whole cycle on a batch: acquire, write, link, review, promote, verify.

## Details
- They are the operating rhythm of the wiki — the pass structure that converts captures into growing articles.
- A curation sprint needs defined scope (a batch, a cluster, a queue) and a definition of done (metrics moved, checks green).
- For mykb, curation sprints are the unit that the stub-ratio and full-article-ratio metrics track.
