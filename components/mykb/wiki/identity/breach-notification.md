# Breach Notification

- Breach notification requires reporting personal-data breaches to regulators (GDPR Article 33: within 72 hours) and to affected individuals when risk is high (Article 34).
- Other regimes (state laws, sector rules like HIPAA) impose similar duties with varying timelines and thresholds.
- Preparedness matters: notification is only possible if detection, classification, and data mapping already exist.
- For mykb: knowing what data is stored, where, and how sensitive (data classification) is the prerequisite for any notification obligation.
