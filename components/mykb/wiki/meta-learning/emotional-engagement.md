# Emotional Engagement

## Summary

Emotional Engagement — Affective reactions to learning: interest, enjoyment, anxiety, boredom.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Positive emotions foster attention and persistence; negative ones can undermine both.
- Emotions interact with cognitive engagement and self-regulation.
- Control-value theory links appraisals of control and value to achievement emotions.
- Feeds academic-emotions and test-anxiety research.
