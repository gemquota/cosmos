# Effort Praise

## Summary

Effort Praise — Praising effort and strategy rather than ability.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Effort praise encourages attributions to controllable causes.
- Studies show mixed effects: helpful when paired with strategy guidance, harmful when false.
- Best practice: praise specific, effective effort and revise strategy.
- Connects to growth-mindset and attribution interventions.
