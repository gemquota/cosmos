# Musician Practice

## Summary

Musician Practice — Practice patterns of expert musicians and their cognitive effects.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Elite musicians accumulate thousands of hours of deliberate practice.
- Practice quality (slow work, error correction) matters more than raw hours.
- Musical training shapes auditory, motor, and memory systems.
- Model domain for skill-acquisition research.
