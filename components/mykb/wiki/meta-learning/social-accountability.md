# Social Accountability

## Summary

Social Accountability — Using commitments to others to motivate follow-through.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Announced goals and check-ins raise follow-through via reputation and commitment.
- Accountability partners work when feedback is concrete and regular.
- Design should avoid shame spirals; focus on support and evidence.
- Underpins peer-assessment and after-action-review cultures.
