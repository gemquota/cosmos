# Attribution Theory

## Summary

Attribution Theory — How people explain causes of events and how explanations shape motivation.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Weiner's model: causes vary on locus, stability, and controllability.
- Attributing failure to stable, uncontrollable causes undermines motivation.
