# Confidence Ratings

## Summary

Confidence Ratings — Expressed probability judgments of one's own answer correctness.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Typically rated 0-100% or on verbal scales; converted for calibration analysis.
- Binning answers by confidence reveals over- and underconfidence regions.
- Foundation of calibration curves and Brier scores.
- Used in retrieval practice to select items for review.
