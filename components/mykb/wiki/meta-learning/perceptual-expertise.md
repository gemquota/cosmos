# Perceptual Expertise

## Summary

Perceptual Expertise — Expert-level discrimination and recognition within a visual domain.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Experts (radiologists, birders, fingerprint examiners) discriminate subtle differences.
- Develops via exposure, feedback, and motivation over years.
- Neural tuning: fusiform specialization for the expert domain.
- Faces limits when the domain changes.
