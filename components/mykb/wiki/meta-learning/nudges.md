# Nudges

## Summary

Nudges — Low-cost design changes that steer behavior while preserving choice.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Nudges alter the choice environment, not incentives or mandates.
- Examples: defaults, reminders, social proof, salience.
- Effect sizes vary; replication is uneven across contexts.
- Raise design and ethics questions for personal systems like mykb.
