# Tiny Habits

## Summary

Tiny Habits — Fogg's method of starting with micro-behaviors to build habits.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Shrink the habit until it takes under 30 seconds and is easy to celebrate.
- Success and celebration wire the behavior into routine.
- Scales up once the micro-version is automatic.
- Addresses task-initiation friction directly.
