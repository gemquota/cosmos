# Achievement Goal Theory

## Summary

Achievement Goal Theory — Framework explaining motivation through the goals students pursue in achievement settings.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Distinguishes mastery goals (develop competence) from performance goals (demonstrate competence)
- Goal contexts (classroom climate, feedback) shape which goals students adopt.
