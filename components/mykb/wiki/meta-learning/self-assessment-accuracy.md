# Self-Assessment Accuracy

## Summary

Self-Assessment Accuracy — How well learners judge their own performance and knowledge.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- People are overconfident on average; accuracy improves with feedback and calibration training.
- Self-assessment is more accurate for well-known domains and after practice tests.
- Incentives reduce but do not eliminate bias.
- Foundational to calibration-of-judgment and self-regulated learning.
