# Learned Optimism

## Summary

Learned Optimism — Seligman's claim that optimistic explanatory styles can be learned.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Optimistic people treat setbacks as temporary, specific, and external.
- Training teaches disputation and reattribution of pessimistic explanations.
