# Narrow Expertise

## Summary

Narrow Expertise — Deep skill confined to a specific domain or task set.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Most expertise is narrow: chess skill does not generalize to medicine.
- Narrow experts fail when conditions change (inert knowledge).
- Value comes from depth; risk from brittleness.
- Motivates hybrid generalist-specialist models.
