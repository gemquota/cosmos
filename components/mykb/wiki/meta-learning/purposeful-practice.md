# Purposeful Practice

## Summary

Purposeful Practice — Practice with clear goals, feedback, and comfort-zone stretching, short of full deliberate practice.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Ericsson's ladder: naive → purposeful → deliberate practice.
- Purposeful practice has specific goals, focused attention, and feedback.
- Missing element vs deliberate: expert-designed training methods.
- A practical target for self-directed learners.
