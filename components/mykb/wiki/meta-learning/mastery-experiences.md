# Mastery Experiences

## Summary

Mastery Experiences — Successful performances that build self-efficacy.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Bandura: personal success is the strongest source of self-efficacy.
- Interpretation matters: success that feels easy builds less efficacy than overcoming difficulty.
