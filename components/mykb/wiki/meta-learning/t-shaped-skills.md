# T-Shaped Skills

## Summary

T-Shaped Skills — Deep expertise in one area plus broad ability across many.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- The vertical bar is deep specialty; the horizontal bar is breadth for collaboration.
- Popular in product, engineering, and consulting hiring.
- Breadth enables integration; depth earns credibility.
- A practical resolution of the generalist-specialist debate.
