# Procrastination Research

## Summary

Procrastination Research — Study of voluntary delay of intended action despite expected costs.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Procrastination is a self-regulation failure, not a time-management skill gap.
- Trait procrastination correlates with impulsivity and weaker conscientiousness.
- Interventions: implementation intentions, environment design, and self-compassion.
- Explained formally by temporal motivation theory.
