# Fitts-Posner Model

## Summary

Fitts-Posner Model — Three stages of skill acquisition: cognitive, associative, autonomous.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Cognitive: instruction-heavy, error-prone; associative: refinement and chunking; autonomous: fluent, low-attention.
- Duration of stages varies by task and practice quality.
