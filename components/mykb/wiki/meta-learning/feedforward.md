# Feedforward

## Summary

Feedforward — Feedback reframed as guidance for the next attempt.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Feedforward states what to do next rather than judging what was done.
- Keeps attention on growth and reduces defensiveness.
- Works best alongside accurate diagnosis of the past attempt.
- Common in coaching and design critique.
