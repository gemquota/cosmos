# Emotional Regulation

## Summary
Emotional regulation is the set of processes that shape emotional experience and expression. Reappraisal (changing interpretation) and distraction are among the most effective strategies; suppression works less well and costs memory. It underpins learning, collaboration, and long-term persistence.

## Details
- **Strategy taxonomy** — situation selection, modification, attention, reappraisal, and response modulation.
- **Evidence** — reappraisal reduces negative affect with fewer cognitive costs than suppression.
- **Development** — regulation improves with practice and age; it is trainable in adults.
- **In mykb** — failure reviews should regulate emotions first, extract lessons second.
