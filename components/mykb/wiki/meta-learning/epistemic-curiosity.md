# Epistemic Curiosity

## Summary

Epistemic Curiosity — Desire for knowledge that reduces uncertainty and fills understanding gaps.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Epistemic curiosity targets information gaps and drives deep, exploratory learning.
- Distinct from perceptual curiosity, which targets novel sensory stimulation.
- Information-gap theory: curiosity peaks when the gap between known and unknown is intermediate.
- Supports inquiry-based learning and question generation.
