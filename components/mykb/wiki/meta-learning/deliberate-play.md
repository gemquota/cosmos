# Deliberate Play

## Summary

Deliberate Play — Playful, self-directed practice that still targets skill development.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Deliberate play combines enjoyment with skill-building constraints.
- Common in sports and language learning where motivation flags.
- Complements deliberate practice by sustaining engagement.
- Especially effective for children and early skill phases.
