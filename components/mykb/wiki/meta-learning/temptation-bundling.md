# Temptation Bundling

## Summary

Temptation Bundling — Pairing an indulgence with a needed but disliked behavior.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Milkman's method: only do the indulgence while doing the target behavior.
- Example: favorite podcast only while exercising.
- Bridges immediate reward to delayed-benefit tasks.
- Related to reward timing in habit loops.
