# Behavioral Engagement

## Summary

Behavioral Engagement — Visible participation: attendance, effort, and on-task conduct.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Measured via attendance, participation, time-on-task, and persistence.
- Necessary but not sufficient for deep learning.
- First target of interventions; often easiest to observe and log.
- In systems, behavioral signals feed learning analytics.
