# Intelligent Tutoring Systems

## Summary

Intelligent Tutoring Systems — AI-driven tutors that model learners and adapt instruction.

## Details

- Intelligent tutoring systems (ITS) deliver one-on-one-style instruction by modeling the learner, the domain, and pedagogy. Classic architecture: a domain model (expert knowledge), a student model (what the learner knows, often via knowledge tracing), a tutoring model (when to hint, explain, or advance), and an interface.
- Evidence: meta-analyses show ITS outperforming traditional classroom instruction in many domains, approaching human tutoring in effect size, especially for well-structured skills like math and programming.
- Worked example: a student solving algebra gets a hint sequence — a minimal nudge, then a worked step, then an explanation — with each hint gated by the student model's estimate of what the learner already knows.
- Modern systems add natural language, generative explanations, and mastery-based pacing; challenges include authoring cost, model brittleness, and the 'expert blind spot' in content.
