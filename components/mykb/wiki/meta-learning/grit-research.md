# Grit Research

## Summary
Grit research proposes that sustained passion and perseverance predict achievement beyond talent and IQ. Early claims were large; later analyses found modest effects once conscientiousness and demographics are controlled. It remains a useful folk concept and a contested scientific one.

## Details
- **Construct** — consistency of interest plus perseverance of effort.
- **Predictive power** — modest incremental validity over conscientiousness in most replications.
- **Criticism** — construct overlap, measurement issues, and cultural specificity.
- **Practical reading** — persistence matters, but domain selection and strategy matter at least as much.
