# Generalist vs Specialist

## Summary

Generalist vs Specialist — Trade-offs between breadth and depth in knowledge and skill.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Specialists go deep fast but adapt slowly; generalists flex across domains.
- Modern complexity often rewards T-shaped hybrids.
- Polymaths contribute via cross-domain analogies.
- Choice depends on goals, rate of change, and information access.
