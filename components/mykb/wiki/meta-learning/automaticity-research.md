# Automaticity Research

## Summary

Automaticity Research — Study of behaviors that become fast, effortless, and attention-free with practice.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Automatic processes run without intent, effort, or capacity limits.
- Stroop interference and skilled typing show automatic components.
- Automaticity frees attention for higher-level control.
- Develops through extensive consistent practice, per skill-acquisition models.
