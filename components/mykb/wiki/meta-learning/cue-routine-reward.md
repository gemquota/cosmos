# Cue-Routine-Reward

## Summary

Cue-Routine-Reward — The three-part behavioral pattern underlying habit formation.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Cues trigger routines that deliver rewards; the loop strengthens with repetition.
- Context stability is the strongest predictor of habit formation.
- Reward timing and salience shape how quickly the loop consolidates.
- Basis of habit-stacking and tiny-habits interventions.
