# Situational Interest

## Summary

Situational Interest — Short-lived interest sparked by features of the immediate environment.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Triggered by novelty, surprise, personal relevance, or vivid presentation.
- Can be externally supported and is the entry point to developing interest.
- Fades without maintained attention and meaningful engagement.
- Design target for lessons, talks, and documentation.
