# Habit Stacking

## Summary

Habit Stacking — Anchoring a new habit to an existing routine.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Formula: after [existing habit], I will [new habit].
- Uses established cues as reliable triggers for new behavior.
- Works best when the anchor is genuinely automatic.
- A concrete practice from behavior-design books (Clear, Fogg).
