# Educational Data Mining

## Summary

Educational Data Mining — Applying data-mining and ML methods to educational data.

## Details

- Educational data mining (EDM) applies machine learning and statistics to data from educational systems — answer logs, discussion forums, and clickstreams — to discover patterns and build models of learners and learning.
- Typical tasks: knowledge tracing (mastery prediction), student modeling (skill profiles), drop-out prediction, automated essay scoring, and discovering effective learning sequences. Methods include Bayesian networks, factorization machines, sequence mining, and deep networks.
- Worked example: mining a million practice sessions reveals that students who interleave problem types (even accidentally) show steeper learning curves — a pattern that then becomes a design recommendation.
- EDM differs from learning analytics in emphasis: modeling and discovery over reporting; in practice the fields overlap heavily.
