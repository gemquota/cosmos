# Willpower Research

## Summary
Willpower research asks how much self-control is a limited resource and how it is replenished. Classic ego-depletion findings have weakened under replication pressure, while motivation, glucose beliefs, and task framing remain influential. The field now favors resource-based and motivational accounts in combination.

## Details
- **Ego depletion** — early evidence for sequential-task self-control costs; large replications found small effects.
- **Reinterpretations** — beliefs about willpower, incentives, and rest moderate depletion.
- **Practical takeaway** — reduce reliance on raw willpower via habits, environment, and routines.
- **Relevance** — mykb's habit and volition notes should cite this contested evidence base.
