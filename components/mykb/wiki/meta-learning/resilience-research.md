# Resilience Research

## Summary
Resilience research examines why some people bounce back from setbacks while others languish. Findings emphasize modifiable factors: social support, cognitive reappraisal, routines, and growth framing. Resilience is a process, not a fixed trait, and it is trainable.

## Details
- **Process view** — resilience is adaptation over time, not absence of distress.
- **Protective factors** — relationships, meaning-making, and emotion regulation predict recovery.
- **Interventions** — CBT-style reappraisal and post-event reflection improve outcomes.
- **In mykb** — failure reviews and after-action reviews are resilience machinery.
