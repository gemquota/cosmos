# Proceduralization

## Summary

Proceduralization — Converting declarative knowledge into procedural skill.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- In ACT-R, declarative chunks compile into production rules with practice.
- Proceduralized skill is faster but less verbally accessible.
- Explains the expert's 'can't explain how' phenomenon.
- Countered by deliberate practice and reflective review.
