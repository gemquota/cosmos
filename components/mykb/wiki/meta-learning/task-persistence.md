# Task Persistence

## Summary

Task Persistence — Continuing effort on a task despite difficulty, boredom, or setback.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Persistence follows from task value, self-efficacy, and feedback loops.
- Contrasts with quitting; both can be rational under cost-benefit updates.
- Grit research measures persistence across long horizons.
- Reinforced by mastery experiences and goal clarity.
