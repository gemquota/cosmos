# Expertise Transfer

## Summary

Expertise Transfer — Whether and when skill in one domain carries to another.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Transfer is narrow: expertise is largely domain-specific.
- Some components (self-regulation, learning strategies) transfer better than content.
- Analogical transfer improves with abstraction and multiple cases.
- Important caveat for generalist claims and career pivots.
