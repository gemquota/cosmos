# Commitment Devices

## Summary

Commitment Devices — Structures that bind future behavior to current intentions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Devices raise the cost of failing a commitment (money, reputation, deadlines).
- Examples: savings locks, public pledges, app blockers.
- Effectiveness depends on credibility of the enforcement.
- Precommitment is the classic form; temptation bundling is a reward-side variant.
