# Novelty Seeking

## Summary

Novelty Seeking — Drive to explore unfamiliar stimuli, situations, and ideas.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Novelty activates dopaminergic reward circuitry and motivates exploration.
- Balanced with safety and exploitation; excessive novelty seeking links to impulsivity.
- Educational value: novel contexts raise attention and curiosity.
- Underlies curiosity-driven learning and information-seeking behavior.
