# Test Anxiety

## Summary

Test Anxiety — Worry and emotionality that impair performance in evaluative situations.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Two components: worry (cognitive interference) and emotionality (physiological arousal).
- Worry consumes working-memory capacity, harming complex tasks most.
- Interventions: preparation, reappraisal, expressive writing, and desensitization.
- Connects to retrieval-practice and to formative-assessment design.
