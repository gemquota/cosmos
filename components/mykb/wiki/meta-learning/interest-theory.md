# Interest Theory

## Summary

Interest Theory — How interest develops and drives attention, learning, and persistence.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Interest has cognitive (valuing) and affective (enjoyment) components.
- Hidi & Renninger's model: triggered situational interest can develop into enduring individual interest.
- Interest predicts attention, deep processing, and continued engagement in a domain.
- Educational design works to trigger and then cultivate interest.
