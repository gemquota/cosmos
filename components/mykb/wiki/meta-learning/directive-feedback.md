# Directive Feedback

## Summary

Directive Feedback — Feedback that directly tells the learner what is wrong and what to do.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Directive feedback states the correct answer or the needed correction explicitly.
- Efficient for well-defined skills and novice learners.
- Risk: bypasses self-correction and reasoning when overused.
- Complements error-driven learning by providing the error signal.
