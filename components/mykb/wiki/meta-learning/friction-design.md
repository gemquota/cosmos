# Friction Design

## Summary

Friction Design — Deliberately adding or removing effort to shape behavior.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Removing friction (defaults, one-click) raises desired behavior; adding it deters undesired.
- Friction includes time, clicks, attention, and cognitive load.
- Ethical question: who decides which friction is good.
- Core tool of choice-architecture and nudging.
