# Feedback Timing

## Summary

Feedback Timing — When feedback is delivered and how timing affects learning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Immediate feedback helps initial skill acquisition and error correction.
- Delayed feedback often benefits retention and transfer after a first attempt.
- Timing interacts with task type: motor skills favor immediacy, complex reasoning tolerates delay.
- Design choice for tutoring systems and formative assessment.
