# Outcome Expectancy

## Summary

Outcome Expectancy — Belief that a behavior will produce a given outcome.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Distinct from self-efficacy: efficacy is about capability, expectancy about consequences.
- Both feed the expectancy-value calculus of motivation.
