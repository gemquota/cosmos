# Habit vs Goal-Directed Control

## Summary
Habit versus goal-directed control distinguishes automatic stimulus-response routines from flexible, outcome-sensitive deliberation. Behavior shifts from goal-directed to habitual with repetition and back when outcomes change. It explains why old habits survive intentions and why environments matter.

## Details
- **Two systems** — model-free (habit) and model-based (goal-directed) action selection.
- **Outcome devaluation** — habits persist even when the reward is removed; goal-directed acts stop.
- **Shift factors** — repetition, stress, and time pressure push behavior toward habit.
- **Design** — make desired actions easy and visible so habits form in the right direction.
