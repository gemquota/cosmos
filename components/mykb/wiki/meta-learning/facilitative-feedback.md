# Facilitative Feedback

## Summary

Facilitative Feedback — Feedback that guides learners to discover corrections themselves.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Uses hints and questions rather than direct answers.
- Slower but builds reasoning and transfer.
- Works when learners have enough prior knowledge to bridge the hint.
- A key move in scaffolding and Socratic questioning.
