# Coach Feedback

## Summary

Coach Feedback — External, expert-provided feedback that guides skill improvement.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Coaches diagnose errors, prescribe drills, and set challenge levels.
- Effective feedback is immediate, specific, and actionable.
- Coach presence adds accountability and external perspective.
- Central to deliberate practice as Ericsson defines it.
