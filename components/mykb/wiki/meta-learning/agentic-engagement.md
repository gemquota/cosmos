# Agentic Engagement

## Summary

Agentic Engagement — Students' proactive, constructive contribution to their own learning environment.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Reeve's fourth dimension: students ask questions, express preferences, and shape instruction.
- Agentic behavior predicts motivational gains beyond the other engagement dimensions.
- Requires autonomy support and psychological safety.
- Mirrors self-direction goals in agent systems and RSIS3.
