# Pattern Recognition Expertise

## Summary

Pattern Recognition Expertise — Experts' ability to see meaningful patterns novices miss.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Experts chunk information into larger, meaningful units (chess positions, X-rays).
- Perceptual learning tunes feature extraction over thousands of examples.
- Enables rapid, accurate situation assessment.
- Root of intuition and of narrow-expertise effects.
