# Polymath Research

## Summary

Polymath Research — Study of multi-domain expertise and its cognitive basis.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Polymaths achieve expertise in multiple fields, often linked by shared structure.
- Cross-domain analogy and transfer are hypothesized mechanisms.
- Rare: depth in one domain already requires thousands of hours.
- Relevant to curiosity-driven learning and knowledge integration.
