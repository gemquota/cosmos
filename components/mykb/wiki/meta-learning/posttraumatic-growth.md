# Posttraumatic Growth

## Summary
Posttraumatic growth refers to reported improvements in relationships, appreciation of life, personal strength, spirituality, and new possibilities after adversity. It is distinct from resilience and from mere recovery. Reported growth is real, but it coexists with ongoing distress and can be overstated.

## Details
- **Domains** — five growth areas from Tedeschi & Calhoun's model.
- **Mechanisms** — deliberate rumination and meaning reconstruction, not the event itself.
- **Caveats** — growth self-reports correlate with positive illusions; it is not a required outcome.
- **Relevance** — mykb's reflective practice can track growth narratives without forcing them.
