# Category Learning

## Summary
Category learning is the acquisition of groupings — how to tell birds from bats, bugs from features. It proceeds through rules, prototypes, or exemplars depending on the structure and the learner. Categories organize perception, memory, and generalization.

## Details
- **Theories** — prototype, exemplar, and rule-based accounts.
- **Development** — children learn categories from labels and similarity.
- **Expertise** — experts use finer, more diagnostic categories.
- **In mykb** — taxonomy design is category learning made explicit.
