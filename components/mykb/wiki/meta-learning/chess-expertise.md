# Chess Expertise

## Summary

Chess Expertise — The classic research domain for expert memory and pattern recognition.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Chase & Simon: masters recall real board positions far better than novices, but random boards no better.
- Expertise rests on thousands of stored patterns (chunks).
- Calculation and evaluation skills also grow with practice.
- De Groot showed masters search fewer, better moves.
