# Goal Intentions

## Summary

Goal Intentions — Commitments to achieve particular outcomes.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Goal intentions specify desired outcomes but not when and how to act.
- Adding implementation intentions (if-then plans) substantially raises attainment.
- Effective goals are specific, challenging, and framed as approach.
- Complemented by habit formation for automaticity.
