# Novelty Detection

## Summary
Novelty detection is the brain's fast tagging of stimuli as new relative to stored knowledge. Novelty grabs attention, boosts encoding, and motivates exploration. Systems from intrusion detection to knowledge curation implement the same idea algorithmically.

## Details
- **Neural basis** — hippocampus and midbrain respond to novelty with enhanced encoding.
- **Behavior** — novelty seeking and curiosity follow detection.
- **Computational use** — anomaly and outlier detection models.
- **In mykb** — dedup and gap detection flag novel versus known content.
