# Performance Goals

## Summary

Performance Goals — Goals focused on demonstrating competence relative to others.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Performance-approach goals can aid simple performance but add anxiety and surface strategy use.
- Performance-avoidance goals reliably predict worse engagement and outcomes.
