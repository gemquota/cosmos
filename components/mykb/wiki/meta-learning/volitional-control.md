# Volitional Control

## Summary

Volitional Control — Will-based processes that translate intentions into action and protect them.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Volition covers action initiation, persistence, and shielding from distraction.
- Kuhl's theory separates volitional control from motivational choice.
- Implementation intentions automate volitional control at trigger points.
- The bridge between goal-intentions and realized behavior.
