# Cognitive Engagement

## Summary

Cognitive Engagement — Psychological investment and strategy use in learning tasks.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Involves self-regulation, deep processing, and willingness to exert mental effort.
- Distinct from mere time-on-task; quality of processing matters.
- Promoted by autonomy support, challenge, and meaningful tasks.
- Bridges to self-regulated learning and to cognitive load management.
