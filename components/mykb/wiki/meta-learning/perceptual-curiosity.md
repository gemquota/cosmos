# Perceptual Curiosity

## Summary

Perceptual Curiosity — Drive for novel sensory stimulation and surprise.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Perceptual curiosity motivates exploration of new sights, sounds, and environments.
- Thought to be rooted in arousal and novelty detection.
- Shorter-lived than epistemic curiosity, which sustains longer inquiry.
- Animal research on novelty-seeking informs the human account.
