# Task Initiation

## Summary

Task Initiation — Getting started on a task despite competing options and friction.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Starting is often the hardest step; momentum then carries action.
- Small first steps and implementation intentions lower the initiation barrier.
- Environmental cues (visible triggers) support reliable starts.
- Links to tiny-habits and to friction-design.
