# Praise Research

## Summary

Praise Research — Evidence on how praise affects motivation and learning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Praise type matters more than amount: process praise beats person praise.
- Effort praise can backfire if it implies low ability.
- Sincere, specific, and informational praise supports intrinsic motivation.
- Foundational to mindset and attribution research.
