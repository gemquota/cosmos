# Vicarious Experience

## Summary

Vicarious Experience — Learning and confidence from observing similar others succeed.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Seeing peers master a task raises observer efficacy, especially when the model is similar.
- Also the basis of modeling-based instruction and social learning theory.
