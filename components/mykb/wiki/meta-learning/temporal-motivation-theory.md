# Temporal Motivation Theory

## Summary

Temporal Motivation Theory — Expectancy-value model with time discounting to predict task motivation.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Motivation = (expectancy × value) / (delay × impulsiveness).
- Explains deadline effects and why distant rewards fail to motivate.
- Unifies expectancy-value theory with hyperbolic discounting.
- Diagnostic for procrastination and task design.
