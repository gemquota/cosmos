# Choice Architecture

## Summary

Choice Architecture — Designing the context in which people make decisions.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Defaults, order, salience, and framing shape choices without forbidding options.
- Thaler & Sunstein's nudge program builds on bounded rationality.
- Effects are real but context-dependent; transparency matters.
- Applies to learning platforms, dashboards, and system defaults.
