# Precommitment

## Summary

Precommitment — Binding oneself in advance to resist future temptation.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Classic examples: Ulysses and the Sirens, Christmas savings accounts.
- Works by removing the tempting option or raising its cost.
- Strongest when the constraint is hard to reverse.
- Foundational strategy in behavioral self-control research.
