# Self-Grading

## Summary

Self-Grading — Learners scoring their own work against model answers.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Self-grading with model answers is a strong retrieval and feedback exercise.
- Accuracy improves when criteria are explicit and examples are provided.
- Risk: inflated grades without checkpoints.
- Used in testing-effect research to deliver immediate feedback.
