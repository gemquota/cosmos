# Engineering Intuition

## Summary

Engineering Intuition — Expert engineers' gut sense for what will work and what will break.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Intuition in engineering is compressed experience: pattern-matched cases.
- Reliable in familiar territory, dangerous outside it.
- Complemented by analysis, testing, and peer review.
- Develops through varied projects and post-mortems.
