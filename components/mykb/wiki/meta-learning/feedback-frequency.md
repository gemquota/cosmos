# Feedback Frequency

## Summary

Feedback Frequency — How often feedback is given and its effects on learning.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- High-frequency feedback speeds early gains but can create dependency.
- Reducing feedback frequency forces internal error monitoring.
- Optimal frequency depends on learner expertise and task difficulty.
- Pairs with fading strategies in skill training.
