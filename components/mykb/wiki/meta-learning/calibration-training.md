# Calibration Training

## Summary

Calibration Training — Practice that improves the match between confidence and accuracy.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Methods: confidence ratings with immediate outcome feedback, bracketing, and review.
- Training transfers when feedback is frequent and domains are stable.
- Overconfidence is sticky; debiasing must be sustained.
- Serves forecasting, self-assessment, and decision quality.
