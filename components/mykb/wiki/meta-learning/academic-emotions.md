# Academic Emotions

## Summary

Academic Emotions — Emotions experienced in achievement and learning settings.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Include enjoyment, hope, pride, anxiety, boredom, shame, and anger.
- Control-value theory links appraisals of control and value to specific emotions.
- Emotions affect attention, strategy use, and persistence.
- Domain-general versus domain-specific patterns differ across subjects.
