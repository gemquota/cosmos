# Formative Assessment

## Summary

Formative Assessment — Ongoing assessment used to adjust teaching and learning while it happens.

## Details

- Formative assessment gathers evidence of learning during instruction to adjust teaching and guide learners — the 'assessment for learning' loop. It contrasts with summative assessment, which certifies learning at the end. Black and Wiliam's review found formative practices among the most effective classroom interventions.
- Core moves: share learning intentions, elicit evidence (questions, exit tickets, hinge questions), give feedback that moves learning forward, and activate learners as owners of their progress.
- Worked example: a hinge question after a lesson segment — if under 90% answer correctly, reteach; the evidence, not the schedule, decides the next move.
- Digital systems scale formative assessment: adaptive quizzing, knowledge tracing, and dashboards turn every interaction into evidence.
- mykb relevance: statuses (stub → growing) and gap reports are formative assessment for the knowledge base itself.
