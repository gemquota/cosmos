# Self-Control Research

## Summary
Self-control research measures the ability to override impulses in favor of longer-term goals and links it to outcomes like health, grades, and finances. Trait measures predict real-world behavior better than lab tasks do. Interventions shift the environment more reliably than they strengthen the trait.

## Details
- **Measurement gap** — questionnaires and behavioral tasks correlate weakly; each predicts different outcomes.
- **Trait vs situation** — situations dominate: nearby temptations predict lapses better than trait scores.
- **Interventions** — implementation intentions, habit design, and friction reduction outperform willpower training.
- **In mykb** — the self-regulation notes should distinguish trait, state, and environment effects.
