# Attention and Cognition

## Summary

Attention and Cognition — How selective attention allocates processing resources and shapes cognition.

## Details

- Attention is the set of mechanisms that select information for privileged processing — the gateway to working memory, learning, and conscious perception. It has spatial, feature-based, object-based, and temporal forms, and is controlled by both bottom-up salience and top-down goals.
- Resource models treat attention as limited capacity: divided attention degrades performance, sustained attention wanes with time, and attentional failures (blinks, blindness) reveal the limits. Attention also shapes memory: attended material is encoded better, and attention at encoding predicts later recall.
- Worked example: studying with notifications on divides attention, slowing encoding and flattening later recall — even when the interruptions are brief and the total time is equal.
- Applications: interface design (reduce distraction), study hygiene, and agent systems (attention mechanisms in transformers are computational cousins). Attention is trainable through practice but remains the core bottleneck.
- mykb relevance: cognitive-offloading and note-taking practices are attention management strategies for knowledge work.
