# Posttraumatic Growth

## Summary

Posttraumatic Growth — Positive psychological change reported after struggling with adversity.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Growth domains: appreciation of life, relationships, new possibilities, personal strength, spirituality.
- Growth and distress can coexist; reporting growth does not mean absence of suffering.
- Self-reports may be inflated by retrospective bias and coping narratives.
- Related to autobiographical reasoning and meaning-making.
