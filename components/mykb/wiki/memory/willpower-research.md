# Willpower Research

## Summary

Willpower Research — Scientific study of self-control as a limited or flexible resource.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Baumeister's ego-depletion model treated self-control as a depletable resource; replication attempts have weakened the effect.
- Alternative accounts: motivation, glucose beliefs, and task-switching explain apparent depletion.
- Trait self-control predicts better life outcomes across health, finance, and relationships.
- mykb relevance: willpower claims should be cited with current meta-analytic status.
