# Self-Control Research

## Summary

Self-Control Research — Empirical study of overriding impulses in favor of longer-term goals.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Core paradigms: delay of gratification, temptation resistance, and trait self-report scales.
- Mischel's marshmallow studies link early delay ability to later outcomes, with strong SES confounds.
- Self-control is trainable through habits, implementation intentions, and environment design.
- Bridges decision theory, motivation, and behavior change.
