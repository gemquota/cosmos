# Scene Construction

## Summary

Scene Construction — Building rich mental scenes as the basis of remembering and imagining.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Hassabis & Maguire: the hippocampus constructs spatially coherent scenes, reused for recall and imagination.
- Scene construction explains why amnesics struggle to imagine novel futures, not only recall pasts.
- Imagery vividness varies across people and links to episodic detail.
- Central to constructive-episodic-simulation accounts of mental time travel.
