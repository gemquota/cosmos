# Evergreen Notes

## Summary
Evergreen notes are permanent, atomic, linked, and written in your own words so they can be reused across many future contexts. They are the core unit of modern personal knowledge management and the target state of a digital garden.

## Details
- **Properties** — one idea, own words, precise title, links to related notes, and maintenance over time.
- **Why** — reusable units compose: new projects assemble existing notes instead of rewriting knowledge.
- **Agent relevance** — mykb pages with stable titles and typed links are evergreen by design; RSIS3 reuses them across pulses.
