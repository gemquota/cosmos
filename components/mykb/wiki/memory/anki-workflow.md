# Anki Workflow

## Summary

Anki Workflow — End-to-end practice of importing, reviewing, and maintaining cards in Anki.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Typical flow: capture in notes, convert to Q/A cards, review daily, prune cards that never fail.
- SM-2 and FSRS schedulers set intervals from your per-card grades.
- Add-ons and syncing make Anki a durable external memory, but card hygiene decides quality.
- In mykb terms, Anki is one operationalization of spaced retrieval for the memory layer.
