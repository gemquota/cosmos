# Retroactive Interference

## Summary

Retroactive Interference — New memories impair retrieval of older, similar material.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Learning list B after list A hurts later recall of list A.
- Strongest when the interfering material is similar to the target.
- Sleep and consolidation reduce retroactive interference by stabilizing new traces.
- A key reason restudy before sleep outperforms cramming into new material.
