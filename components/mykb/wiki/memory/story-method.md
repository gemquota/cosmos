# Story Method

## Summary

Story Method — Mnemonic that weaves list items into a single narrative.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Items are linked into a coherent story, giving each a role in a remembered sequence.
- The narrative provides retrieval order and contextual cues at recall time.
- Vivid, unusual imagery makes the story more memorable.
- Useful for shopping lists, speeches, and any ordered sequence.
