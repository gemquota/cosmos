# Internal Models

## Summary
Internal models are the brain's stored representations of regularities — of the body, environment, and other minds — used for prediction and control. Forward models predict outcomes of actions; inverse models compute required actions. They are learned and continuously updated.

## Details
- **Types** — forward and inverse models.
- **Functions** — prediction, control, and simulation.
- **In mykb** — documented models support debugging of reasoning.
