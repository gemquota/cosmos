# Bayesian Brain

## Summary
The Bayesian brain thesis holds that the brain encodes probability distributions over states of the world and updates them with evidence, integrating priors with likelihoods. It explains perceptual illusions, cue combination, and learning. It is a framework, not a claim that the brain literally computes equations.

## Details
- **Core** — priors plus likelihoods yield posteriors.
- **Evidence** — cue combination and perceptual biases fit Bayes.
- **In mykb** — the Bayesian frame clarifies belief updating.
