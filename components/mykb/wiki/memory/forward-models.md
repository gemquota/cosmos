# Forward Models

## Summary
Forward models predict the sensory outcome of an action before it happens, enabling fast correction and distinguishing self-caused from external sensations. They are central to motor control and to perceiving agency. Prediction failures produce surprises and symptoms.

## Details
- **Use** — efference copy and sensory prediction.
- **Agency** — matching predictions mark self-generated events.
- **In mykb** — expected-outcome logs are forward models.
