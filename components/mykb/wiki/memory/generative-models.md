# Generative Models

## Summary
Generative models capture how data are produced, enabling prediction and simulation of outcomes. The brain is thought to run generative models of the world, and AI systems use them for generation and planning. They contrast with discriminative models that only classify.

## Details
- **Function** — predict and simulate; support planning.
- **Cognitive role** — world models are generative.
- **In mykb** — explicit models enable counterfactual reasoning.
