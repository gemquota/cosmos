# Concept Map Research

## Summary

Concept Map Research — Studies on concept maps as learning, assessment, and knowledge-organization tools.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Concept maps represent knowledge as labeled nodes and linking phrases.
- Research shows benefits for meaningful learning when students construct, not just copy, maps.
- Used as assessment: map structure correlates with expert knowledge organization.
- Novak's work anchors the field; digital tools make maps live artifacts.
