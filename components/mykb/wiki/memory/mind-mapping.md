# Mind Mapping

## Summary
Mind mapping draws a central idea with branching topics and sub-topics radiating outward, capturing associations the way a brainstorm produces them. It excels at overview and divergence, less at precise citation.

## Details
- **Shape** — center = theme; primary branches = major aspects; secondary branches = details; colors and images aid recall.
- **Strength** — fast, visual, and good for planning or lectures where hierarchy is clear.
- **Limitation** — one central topic per map; cross-cutting connections need links, which is where graph notes take over.
