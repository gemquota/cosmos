# Evergreen Note-Taking

## Summary

Evergreen Note-Taking — Writing durable notes that are independently meaningful and develop over time.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Evergreen notes are written for permanence: standalone, title-like-a-claim, revision-friendly.
- They are maintained, not collected — editing matters as much as capturing.
- Andy Matuschak's framing ties them to spaced repetition and writing as thinking.
- mykb's growing/stub statuses mirror the evergreen lifecycle.
