# Grit Research

## Summary

Grit Research — Study of perseverance and passion for long-term goals.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Duckworth's grit predicts achievement in some domains beyond talent and IQ.
- Critiques: high overlap with conscientiousness and weak incremental validity in many samples.
- Perseverance matters, but strategy, practice quality, and context may matter more.
- Related to deliberate practice and to growth-oriented motivation research.
