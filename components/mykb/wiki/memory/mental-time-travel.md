# Mental Time Travel

## Summary

Mental Time Travel — Projecting consciousness into the past and future using episodic memory.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Tulving's term for the capacity to re-experience the past and pre-experience the future.
- Shared neural substrates support remembering and imagining — the constructive-episodic-simulation view.
- Distinguishes episodic memory from mere semantic knowledge.
- Foundation for planning and for episodic-future-thinking research.
