# Digit Span Test

## Summary

Digit Span Test — Classic working-memory measure: repeat back digit sequences of increasing length.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Forward span measures storage; backward span adds manipulation, a better WM indicator.
- Typical adult forward span is 7±2 digits; backward span is usually 1-2 fewer.
- Central to intelligence batteries and a standard lab measure of short-term storage.
- Links to working-memory-capacity and chunk-size-effects.
