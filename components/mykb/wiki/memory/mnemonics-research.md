# Mnemonics Research

## Summary

Mnemonics Research — Evidence base for memory aids that elaborate, organize, or peg information.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Mnemonic techniques improve recall by adding structure, imagery, or meaning to otherwise arbitrary material.
- The keyword method and method of loci show robust gains in paired-associate learning studies.
- Benefits are strongest for serial or list material; benefits for conceptual understanding are weaker.
- Modern research frames mnemonics as a form of elaborative encoding and dual coding.
