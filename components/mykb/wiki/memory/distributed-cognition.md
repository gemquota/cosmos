# Distributed Cognition

## Summary

Distributed Cognition — Cognition spread across people, tools, and environments.

## Details

- Distributed cognition treats cognitive processes as spread across individuals, artifacts, and social arrangements — a cockpit crew with instruments, or a design team with whiteboards — rather than contained in single heads. Hutchins' studies of ship navigation showed that the task's cognitive work is done by the whole system.
- The framework analyzes information flow, representational states, and coordination; the unit of analysis is the system, and cognition is observed in its transformations and propagations.
- Worked example: navigating a ship uses the helm, charts, compass, and officers' coordinated readings — no single person holds the full cognitive state; the distributed system does.
- Design implications: interfaces and workflows are cognitive architecture; improving them improves the thinking they support. It connects to extended mind, collective intelligence, and transactive memory.
- mykb relevance: a wiki plus search plus graph engine is distributed cognition — the knowledge is in the network, not the reader.
