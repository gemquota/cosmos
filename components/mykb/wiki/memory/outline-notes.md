# Outline Notes

## Summary

Outline Notes — Hierarchical indented note-taking that mirrors source structure.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Hierarchy preserves the source's organization and makes review scanning fast.
- Risk: passive copying without transformation, which weakens encoding.
- Effective outlines add own-words summaries and questions at each level.
- The default structure behind many digital note tools.
