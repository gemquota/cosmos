# Childhood Amnesia

## Summary

Childhood Amnesia — The near-total lack of episodic memories from the first years of life.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Most adults' earliest memories date to ~3-4 years, with sparse recall before that.
- Candidates: pre-linguistic encoding, immature hippocampus, and emerging self-concept.
- Gradual offset in early childhood tracks language and narrative development.
- Pairs with reminiscence-bump as a lifespan asymmetry in autobiographical memory.
