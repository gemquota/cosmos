# Precision Weighting

## Summary
Precision weighting scales prediction errors by their reliability: reliable signals drive large updates, noisy ones small updates. It connects attention to belief updating — attention increases precision. It is central to predictive-coding accounts of attention and to Bayesian models.

## Details
- **Mechanism** — error gain depends on estimated noise.
- **Attention link** — attention modulates precision.
- **In mykb** — weight evidence by source reliability.
