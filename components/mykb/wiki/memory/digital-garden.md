# Digital Garden

## Summary
A digital garden is a personal knowledge base published as a web of linked, evolving notes — part blog, part wiki, part notebook. Notes are seeds or seedlings first, then mature through repeated revision, which values process over polish.

## Details
- **Metaphor** — seeds (raw ideas) grow into seedlings (drafts) and then evergreens (stable, linked notes); nothing is finished, everything is tended.
- **Contrast with blogs** — chronological, finalized posts vs growing, interlinked notes.
- **Agent relevance** — mykb's status field (`stub` to `growing`) is a garden lifecycle: stubs are seeds awaiting cultivation by curation passes.
