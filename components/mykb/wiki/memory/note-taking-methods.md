# Note-Taking Methods

## Summary
Note-taking methods are the structured practices used to capture information so it can be reviewed, connected, and reused later. Their choice shapes how well knowledge survives the move from page to memory.

## Details
- **Major families** — outline (linear hierarchy), visual (mind maps, Cornell layouts), and networked (atomic notes with links, Zettelkasten).
- **Fit matters** — lectures favor Cornell's cue column; reading favors margin summaries; long-term knowledge favors linked atomic notes.
- **Agent relevance** — mykb's wiki is a note-taking method: every page captures one idea with frontmatter and links for later retrieval.
