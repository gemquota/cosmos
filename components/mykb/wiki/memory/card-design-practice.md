# Card Design Practice

## Summary

Card Design Practice — Concrete heuristics for writing high-quality spaced-repetition cards.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Heuristics: one fact per card, concrete prompts, minimal extra cues, and a why-connection.
- Periodic card audits (delete, split, or reword) keep decks effective.
