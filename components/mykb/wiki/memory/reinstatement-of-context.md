# Reinstatement of Context

## Summary

Reinstatement of Context — Restoring study context to improve retrieval of learned material.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Physically or mentally returning to the original context reinstates cues for recall.
- Mental reinstatement (imagining the study setting) shows similar benefits to physical return.
