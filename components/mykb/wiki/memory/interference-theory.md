# Interference Theory

## Summary

Interference Theory — Forgetting caused by competing memories rather than simple decay.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- New learning disrupts old memories (retroactive) and old learning disrupts new (proactive).
- Evidence includes paired-associate experiments where similar lists collide.
- Interference explains why similar material is harder to keep separate than dissimilar material.
- Central to forgetting-curve and spaced-repetition-systems design.
