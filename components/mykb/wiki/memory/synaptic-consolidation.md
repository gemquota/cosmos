# Synaptic Consolidation

## Summary

Synaptic Consolidation — Rapid cellular strengthening of memory traces within minutes to hours.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Involves LTP-like changes: receptor trafficking, gene expression, and structural change at synapses.
- Completes within hours and is disrupted by protein-synthesis inhibitors.
- Distinct from systems consolidation, which reorganizes storage locations over time.
- Basis of the claim that early sleep helps fix what was encoded that day.
