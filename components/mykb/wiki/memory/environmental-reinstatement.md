# Environmental Reinstatement

## Summary

Environmental Reinstatement — Returning to the physical environment of learning to cue recall.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Godden & Baddeley's diving study: recall was best when study and test environments matched.
- Environmental cues are strongest for incidental details; benefits can be modest in real classrooms.
