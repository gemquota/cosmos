# Knowledge Map Research

## Summary

Knowledge Map Research — Evidence on maps of knowledge: concept maps, mind maps, and argument maps.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Knowledge maps externalize structure: concepts, relations, and arguments.
- Effect sizes vary by task; maps help when they require active construction and use.
- Digital knowledge graphs inherit many of the same promises and pitfalls.
- mykb's wiki graph is a knowledge map; its edges matter as much as its nodes.
