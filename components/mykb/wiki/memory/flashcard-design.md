# Flashcard Design

## Summary

Flashcard Design — Principles for writing flashcards that trigger retrieval instead of recognition.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Cards should be atomic: one prompt, one answer, no compound questions.
- Avoid cloze overuse and answer-side cues that let recognition replace recall.
- Design for the failure mode: a card that is too easy teaches nothing, too hard discourages review.
- Good prompts are specific enough to test the exact fact, general enough to transfer.
