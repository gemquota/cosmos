# Narrative Identity

## Summary

Narrative Identity — Identity constructed through the internalized life story.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- McAdams: people make meaning by integrating life episodes into a coherent narrative.
- Narrative identity develops in adolescence and is revised across the lifespan.
- Culture supplies story templates that shape which memories are told and how.
- mykb relevance: long-running agent projects also benefit from a maintained self-narrative.
