# Model-Based Cognition

## Summary
Model-based cognition is thinking that relies on internal models — simulations of how the world works — to predict, plan, and decide. It enables flexibility and counterfactual reasoning at a computational cost. It spans motor control, decisions, and social cognition.

## Details
- **Contrast** — model-free habits use cached values.
- **Examples** — mental simulation, planning, imagination.
- **In mykb** — models make knowledge usable for prediction.
