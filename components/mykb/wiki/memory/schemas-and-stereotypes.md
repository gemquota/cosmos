# Schemas and Stereotypes

## Summary

Schemas and Stereotypes — Knowledge structures about groups that shape perception, memory, and judgment.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Stereotypes are group schemas: they economize processing but bias inference and recall.
- People remember stereotype-consistent information better and explain away inconsistencies.
- Activation can be automatic, though outcomes depend on motivation and context.
- A caution for source-memory and for fair evaluation in agent systems.
