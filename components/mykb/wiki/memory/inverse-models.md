# Inverse Models

## Summary
Inverse models compute the motor or cognitive commands required to achieve a desired state — the inverse of forward prediction. They enable goal-directed action and are learned through practice. They pair with forward models in control architectures.

## Details
- **Function** — desired state to required action.
- **Learning** — refined by error correction over practice.
- **In mykb** — procedures are inverse models for goals.
