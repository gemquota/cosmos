# Incremental Reading

## Summary

Incremental Reading — Reading texts progressively through spaced, extractable chunks.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Developed in SuperMemo: readings are broken into excerpts that appear on a spaced schedule.
- Readers extract, reformulate, and convert passages into cards as they go.
- Turns a linear text into a personalized, memory-driven curriculum.
- Demands strong card hygiene to avoid an unmanageable queue.
