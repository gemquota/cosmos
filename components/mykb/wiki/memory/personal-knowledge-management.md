# Personal Knowledge Management

## Summary
Personal knowledge management (PKM) is the deliberate system a person uses to capture, connect, and retrieve what they learn. It spans tools, methods, and habits — from paper index cards to AI-assisted wikis like mykb.

## Details
- **Loop** — capture → organize → retrieve → create; tools serve whichever step is weakest.
- **Methods** — Zettelkasten, PARA, progressive summarization, and evergreen notes are the common playbooks.
- **Agent relevance** — mykb is PKM for an agent: RSIS3 captures sessions, curates concepts, and retrieves memories the same way a human would tend a slip box.
