# Resilience Research

## Summary

Resilience Research — Why some people adapt well under adversity and how resilience develops.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Resilience is ordinary adaptation, not rare heroism: most children of adversity function well.
- Protective factors include relationships, meaning-making, and self-regulation skills.
- Interventions can build resilience but effects depend on risk context.
- Links to posttraumatic growth and to emotional regulation.
