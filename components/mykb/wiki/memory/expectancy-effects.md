# Expectancy Effects

## Summary

Expectancy Effects — Prior expectations shaping perception, memory, and performance.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Expectations guide attention and interpretation, producing confirmation-prone processing.
- In memory, expected events are reconstructed rather than remembered verbatim.
- Expectancy also drives placebo and experimenter effects in performance research.
- Overlaps with schema effects and top-down processing.
