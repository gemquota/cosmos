# Digital Garden Practice

## Summary

Digital Garden Practice — Publishing notes in progressive states of maturity instead of finished posts.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Gardens grow: seeds (stubs) become seedlings, then evergreens, through public iteration.
- Emphasizes linking, revision dates, and partial completion over polished outputs.
- Creates accountability and reuse pressure that improve note quality.
- mykb's status field is a private version of the same maturity gradient.
