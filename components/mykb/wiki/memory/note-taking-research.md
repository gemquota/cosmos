# Note-Taking Research

## Summary

Note-Taking Research — Evidence on how note-taking affects encoding, storage, and review.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Notes help learning through encoding (organizing during class) and external storage (review later).
- Generative formats outperform verbatim transcription for conceptual learning.
- Handwritten vs typed trade-offs are studied under distraction and verbatim-copy accounts.
- Findings inform tools from Cornell notes to matrix notes to digital gardens.
