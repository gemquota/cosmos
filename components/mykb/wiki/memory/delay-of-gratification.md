# Delay of Gratification

## Summary

Delay of Gratification — Choosing larger later rewards over smaller immediate ones.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Mischel's marshmallow paradigm measured preschool delay ability and its longitudinal correlates.
- Reanalysis suggests family background explains much of the apparent predictive power.
- Delay improves with age, strategy use, and trust in the delayed reward.
- Modeled in decision theory as temporal discounting.
