# Knowledge Articulation

## Summary

Knowledge Articulation — Making tacit understanding explicit through language, diagrams, or explanation.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Writing, teaching, and diagramming are the main articulation channels.
