# Reminiscence Bump

## Summary

Reminiscence Bump — Disproportionate recall of autobiographical events from adolescence and early adulthood.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- People over-50 recall more from ages ~10-30 than adjacent decades.
- Explanations include identity formation, novelty, and self-schema consolidation during that period.
- Observed across cultures, with some shifts for life transitions.
- Relates to narrative-identity and memory-and-identity.
