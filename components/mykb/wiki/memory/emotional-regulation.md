# Emotional Regulation

## Summary

Emotional Regulation — Processes by which people influence which emotions they have and how they express them.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Gross's process model: situation selection, modification, attention, reappraisal, and suppression.
- Reappraisal generally beats suppression for well-being and memory outcomes.
- Regulation skill predicts social, academic, and health outcomes.
- Pairs with mood-congruent memory and test-anxiety research.
