# Handwritten vs Typed Notes

## Summary

Handwritten vs Typed Notes — Whether pen or keyboard better supports encoding and later recall.
This stub frames the concept for expansion in the Cognition, Learning & Meta-Knowledge cluster.

## Details

- Studies (e.g., Mueller & Oppenheimer) found handwritten notes improve conceptual performance, partly due to more paraphrase and less verbatim capture.
- Typing wins on speed and searchability; losses come from shallower processing, not the medium itself.
- Best practice may be typed capture plus handwritten-style elaboration and review.
- The encoding-retrieval match matters more than the medium.
