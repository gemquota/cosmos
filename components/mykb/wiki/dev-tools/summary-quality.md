# Summary Quality

## Summary
A good Summary answers in a few sentences: what the concept is, where it sits in the system, and why it matters — readable on its own as a mini-article.

## Details
- The Summary is the highest-value prose on the page: most readers and all link previews consume it first.
- Quality markers: no jargon without definition, no claims the Details do not support, and a clear hook for the rest of the article.
- For mykb, summary quality is part of the content score and is reviewed at promotion time, not at stub time.
