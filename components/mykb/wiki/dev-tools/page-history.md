# Page History

## Summary
Page history is the complete revision record of an article: every edit, its author, timestamp, summary, and diff.

## Details
- History makes the wiki auditable — any state can be inspected, compared, or restored.
- History hygiene is about summaries and structure: clear summaries make history navigable, and merges/splits need history notes.
- For mykb, page history is the raw material for revision-compare, blame, and the recent-changes feed.
