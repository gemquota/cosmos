# Incident Command

## Summary
Incident command assigns explicit roles — incident commander, scribe, subject-matter experts — so a crisis has a single decision-maker and a clear record. It borrows from emergency response to keep teams coordinated under pressure.

## Details
- The commander owns decisions and communication; they do not debug. SMEs report findings and follow direction.
- A scribe keeps a live timeline of actions, hypotheses, and evidence for the postmortem.
- Declare incidents early and announce in the status channel; undeciding later costs less than confusion now.
- mykb relevance: agent incidents benefit from the same structure — a coordinator, a recorder, and clear handoffs.
