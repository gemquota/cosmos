# Diff Viewing

## Summary
Diff viewing compares two revisions of an article and shows exactly what changed — the core tool of edit review.

## Details
- Reading a diff is a skill: hunks show context, and a good review looks at what changed and why it changed.
- Diffs are how reviewers check edits quickly, and how revert decisions are justified.
- For mykb, diff viewing is the review-sprint workhorse and pairs with edit summaries.
