# Revision Compare

## Summary
Revision compare shows two revisions of an article side by side, highlighting inserted, deleted, and changed lines.

## Details
- It is the tool for answering 'what changed between then and now' and for auditing how an article evolved.
- Comparisons are most useful with edit summaries and page history, which supply the why around the what.
- For mykb, revision compare is the review tool used in patrolled-edits and revert decisions.
