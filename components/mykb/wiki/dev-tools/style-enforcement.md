# Style Enforcement

## Summary
Style enforcement converts the wiki's style guide into rules a linter can check: heading format, title case, list punctuation, link placement, and tag conventions.

## Details
- Enforcement is a contract with the writer: the rules are public, the linter reports violations with locations, and exceptions are documented.
- The harder half of style — voice, tone, clarity — stays with human review; machines enforce the mechanical layer only.
- For mykb, style enforcement keeps hundreds of articles readable as a corpus, not just individually.
