# Coverage Gauges

## Summary
Coverage gauges turn raw coverage reports into trend lines, badges, and per-package breakdowns so teams can see whether coverage is improving or rotting. They make quality visible without blocking a merge.

## Details
- Trend lines matter more than single snapshots: a 95% reading means little if it is falling.
- Per-module breakdowns find cold spots; badges in the README keep the number in front of contributors.
- Gauges become gaming targets when tied to bonuses — pair them with mutation score instead.
- mykb relevance: article coverage gauges could show which knowledge clusters are densest.
