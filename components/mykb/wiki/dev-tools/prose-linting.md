# Prose Linting

## Summary
Prose linting catches mechanical writing problems: overlong sentences, passive voice at scale, repeated words, and jargon-heavy phrasing.

## Details
- Linters are blunt instruments for style, so prose rules should flag patterns, not opinions — the final call stays with a human reviewer.
- Combined with spellcheck, prose linting catches the embarrassing errors that damage reader trust faster than any content issue.
- For mykb, prose linting supports the clarity and conciseness scores without pretending a machine can judge writing quality.
