# Title Case Rules

## Summary
Title case rules define which words in an article title are capitalized: typically major words capitalized and minor conjunctions, articles, and prepositions lowercased.

## Details
- The rule set must be written down because reasonable people capitalize differently — and consistency beats any particular choice.
- Titles are also the graph labels, so casing affects link display text everywhere the article is referenced.
- For mykb, title-case rules are part of style enforcement and are checked by linting.
