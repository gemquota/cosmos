# Estimation Techniques

## Summary
Estimation converts uncertainty about work into forecasts teams can plan with. Techniques range from story points and planning poker to t-shirt sizes, ideal days, and evidence-based forecasting.

## Details
- Relative sizing (points) beats absolute guesses; ranges beat point values.
- Throughput-based forecasting uses historical cycle time instead of intuition.
- RSIS3 relevance: the wiki's backlog of stubs is estimated by expansion effort during curation.
