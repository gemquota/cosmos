# Story Points

## Summary
Story points measure the relative size of work — combining effort, complexity, and uncertainty into one fuzzy number. Their power is that they stay stable across people and time, letting teams derive velocity and forecast without pretending to know hours.

## Details
- Points are relative: a 5 is roughly twice a 2, in that team's scale — never convert points to hours.
- Consistency comes from anchoring: reference stories and team memory calibrate the scale.
- Velocity (points per iteration) turns sizes into a rough throughput forecast — a trend, not a target.
- Points are for planning, not for performance review; gaming points destroys their meaning.
- Keep the scale coarse: more than a handful of values invites false precision.
- For the mykb bundle, a stub is 1 point, a full article 3, a new capture integration 8 — a rough but workable scale.
- Worked example — the wiki backlog: 40 stubs at 1 point, 12 fulls at 3, one integration at 8; velocity of 15 points/week forecasts about 6 weeks of curation work.
