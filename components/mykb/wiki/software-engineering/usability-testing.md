# Usability Testing

## Summary
Usability testing watches representative users attempt real tasks while observers note where they struggle. A handful of sessions finds most major problems, making it the highest-ROI method in the UX toolbox.

## Details
- Five users catch most issues; more sessions add diminishing returns — run small rounds, fix, repeat.
- Give realistic tasks, not instructions: 'find the article about raft' beats 'click the search box'.
- Watch what users do, not what they say; think-aloud explains intent but behavior is the evidence.
- Moderated sessions explore why; unmoderated tools scale to many users cheaply.
- Every finding needs a severity and a fix owner, or the test becomes an archive.
- For the mykb bundle, usability testing covers the wiki reading interface and the curator tooling.
- Worked example — a wiki search test: three of five users type the full article title and never discover facets; the fix moves facets above the fold, re-tested with the same tasks.
