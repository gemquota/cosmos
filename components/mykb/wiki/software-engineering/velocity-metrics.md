# Velocity Metrics

## Summary
Velocity is the number of story points a team completes per iteration, used to forecast how much future work fits. It is a planning tool, not a productivity score — and its value depends on stable estimates and honest counting of done work.

## Details
- Velocity is a trailing measure: average over several iterations to smooth randomness.
- It only means something with consistent point scales and a shared definition of done.
- Using velocity as a target invites inflation: points and 'done' both get gamed.
- Forecasting with velocity gives ranges: backlog points divided by velocity yields a rough timeline with variance.
- Track it alongside other health signals (cycle time, defect rate), never alone.
- For the mykb bundle, velocity forecasts article throughput: how many stubs and fulls per week.
- Worked example — three sprints of 12, 16, 14 points give a velocity band of 12-16; the backlog's 60 points imply 4-5 sprints of curation work.
