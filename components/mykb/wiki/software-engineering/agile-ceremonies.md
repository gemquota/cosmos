# Agile Ceremonies

## Summary
Agile ceremonies are the fixed beats of iterative delivery: sprint planning, daily standup, review/demo, and retrospective. Their purpose is alignment, transparency, and continuous improvement — not meeting theater.

## Details
- Retrospectives are where process improvement actually happens; skip them and teams stagnate.
- Ceremonies should shrink to fit the team; one-person teams still need the planning and review beats.
- RSIS3 relevance: knowledge-acquisition rounds are the wiki's sprint cycles with their own review step.
