# Backlog Grooming

## Summary
Backlog grooming (refinement) keeps the backlog ready: items get split, detailed, sized, and re-prioritized before they are needed. It is continuous triage that prevents planning-time surprises and keeps the queue aligned with strategy.

## Details
- Refine top items first: the near-term work should be ready; the distant future can stay fuzzy.
- Split epics into shippable slices with clear value; delete items that lost their reason.
- Size items as they become concrete; vague items get investigation tasks, not estimates.
- Prioritization is a business conversation: value, risk, dependencies, and cost are the inputs.
- Grooming is a standing rhythm, not an emergency: small weekly sessions beat marathon triage.
- For the mykb bundle, grooming curates the article backlog: merge duplicate concepts, split giant topics, re-prioritize clusters.
- Worked example — the wiki backlog review splits 'consistency models' into three articles, merges two duplicates, sizes the top five, and demotes a vanity topic to the idea pile.
