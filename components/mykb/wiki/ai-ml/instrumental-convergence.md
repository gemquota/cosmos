# Instrumental Convergence

## Summary
Prediction that most capable agents pursue common subgoals such as self-preservation and resource acquisition

## Details
- Subgoals like survival and capability gain serve nearly any final goal.
- This makes some failure modes likely across diverse systems.
- Informs safety work on corrigibility and shutdown.
- A key claim in AI risk arguments.
