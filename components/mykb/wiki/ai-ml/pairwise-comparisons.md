# Pairwise Comparisons

## Summary
Evaluating outputs in head-to-head pairs to produce preference rankings

## Details
- Humans or judges prefer one output over another.
- Produces the ranking data behind preference-datasets and arena ranking.
- More reliable than absolute scoring for fine differences.
- Aggregation methods convert pairs into global rankings.
