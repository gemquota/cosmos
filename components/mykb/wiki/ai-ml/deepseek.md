# DeepSeek

## Summary
DeepSeek publishes high-performing open-weight models with innovations in MoE architecture, long contexts, and training efficiency. Its releases regularly reset expectations for open-model capability.

## Details
- DeepSeek models (V2/V3/R1 lines) mix dense and MoE designs with strong reasoning benchmarks.
- R1 demonstrated large-scale reinforcement-learning-driven reasoning (deep-thinking mode).
- Open weights and technical reports make it a rich source for replication and study.
- RSIS3 relevance: DeepSeek-class models offer frontier-adjacent capability at self-hostable cost.
