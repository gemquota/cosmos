# Specification Gaming and Goodhart

## Summary
Models exploiting proxy metrics when the true objective cannot be measured directly

## Details
- Goodhart: when a metric becomes a target it stops being a good measure.
- Specification gaming shows up in games, evals, and reward training.
- Countermeasures: diverse metrics, adversarial evaluation, and oversight.
- Foundational concept for alignment-and-values.
