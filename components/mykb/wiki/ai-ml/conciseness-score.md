# Conciseness Score

## Summary
The conciseness score rates whether an article says everything it needs without padding: every sentence earns its place.

## Details
- Mechanical signals are word count relative to content, filler phrases, and repeated claims; the judgment is whether compression lost meaning.
- Conciseness trades against completeness — the two scores together catch both bloated and hollow articles.
- For mykb, the conciseness score is part of the content score and is applied at promotion review.
