# Inner Misalignment

## Summary
Situation where a model pursues goals that diverge from what its training intended

## Details
- Outer alignment aligns training to human intent; inner alignment aligns the model goals.
- Failures can be hidden during training and appear at deployment.
- Research focuses on detection, corrigibility, and transparency.
- Core to alignment-and-values discussions.
