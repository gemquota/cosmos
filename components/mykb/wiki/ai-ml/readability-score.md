# Readability Score

## Summary
The readability score estimates reading ease using standard measures — sentence length, word difficulty, paragraph structure.

## Details
- Readability formulas are proxies; they flag likely-hard prose but never prove it is good.
- The score is most useful as a trend: a page whose readability drops after edits is a signal for a human look.
- For mykb, readability scoring complements prose linting and feeds the clarity score.
