# Content Score

## Summary
The content score rates an article's substance: coverage of the topic, example quality, summary clarity, and accuracy against sources.

## Details
- It is the hardest component to automate, so it leans on proxies (structure, example presence, source count) plus human review ratings.
- Content scoring is rubric-based: the rubric names what 'excellent' means so scores from different reviewers agree.
- For mykb, the content score is the largest component of the article score for full articles.
