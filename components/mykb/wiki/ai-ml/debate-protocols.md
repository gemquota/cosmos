# Debate Protocols

## Summary
Oversight method where models argue opposing positions so judges identify the truth

## Details
- Two agents debate; a judge or second model evaluates arguments.
- Debate can expose errors that single answers hide.
- Requires strong judges and adversarial honesty to work.
- Along with amplification, a scalable oversight proposal.
