# Retention Score

## Summary
The retention score estimates whether readers remember what they read, drawing on learning research — retrieval practice, examples, and structure aid retention.

## Details
- Retention is hard to measure in a wiki; the score uses proxies like example presence and summary structure, and it is partly a design goal.
- Articles written for retention include worked examples and explicit takeaways, which cost little and pay off.
- For mykb, the retention score guides how technical articles are written, not just what they contain.
