# Iterated Amplification

## Summary
Building superhuman judgments from human-level ones through recursive question decomposition

## Details
- Hard questions are decomposed into subtasks answered and then combined.
- Assumes decomposition errors do not compound catastrophically.
- Motivates debate-protocols and recursive reward modeling.
- One of the main scalable oversight proposals.
