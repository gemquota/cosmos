# Embedding Alignment

## Summary
Making embeddings from different sources or modalities comparable in one space

## Details
- Alignment via shared training, projections, or canonicalization.
- Enables cross-modal and cross-model retrieval.
- Quality depends on training pairs and metric choice.
- Underpins cross-modal-retrieval.
