# Clarity Score

## Summary
The clarity score rates whether an article communicates without confusion: defined terms, explicit structure, and prose a domain outsider can follow.

## Details
- Clarity is partly mechanical (jargon density, sentence length) and partly human (is the logic actually followable?).
- It is the component most tied to reader outcomes, so it carries weight in the article score.
- For mykb, clarity scoring combines readability metrics with review ratings and feeds rewrite-needed decisions.
