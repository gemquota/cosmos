# Synthetic Data Recipes

## Summary
Reproducible procedures for generating synthetic training or eval data with LLMs

## Details
- Recipes specify generator model, prompts, filters, and diversity controls.
- Good recipes produce data that improves rather than degrades models.
- Documentation makes synthetic pipelines auditable.
- Basis of much instruction-data production.
