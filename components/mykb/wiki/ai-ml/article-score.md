# Article Score

## Summary
The article score is a single composite number that rates an article's quality, rolled up from the component scores with recorded weights.

## Details
- Its purpose is ranking and triage — which articles to promote, refresh, or review first — not judging individual worth.
- A composite hides its components, so the score is always published with the breakdown attached.
- For mykb, the article score drives the health dashboard and the promotion queue ordering.
