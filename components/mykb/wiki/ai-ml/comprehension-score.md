# Comprehension Score

## Summary
The comprehension score estimates whether readers come away understanding — measured directly by testing or indirectly by readability, examples, and structure proxies.

## Details
- Direct measurement (quiz a reader) is the gold standard and rarely practical; the wiki uses proxies plus review judgment.
- It is the ultimate quality metric for explainers and the most honest check of whether an article achieved its purpose.
- For mykb, the comprehension score weights heavily for explainer-format articles.
