# Shard Theory

## Summary
Hypothesis that values arise from many small learned components (shards) rather than a single utility

## Details
- Competing shards activate based on context; no monolithic reward exists.
- Predicts value formation during training and goal stability after.
- Suggests shaping training distribution to cultivate desired shards.
- Offers a mechanistic alternative to mesa-optimization.
