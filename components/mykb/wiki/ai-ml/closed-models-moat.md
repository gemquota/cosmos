# Closed Model Moat

## Summary
The competitive advantage proprietary models hold through data, scale, and infrastructure

## Details
- Moat includes training data, compute, and product integration.
- Open-weight progress erodes parts of the moat over time.
- Customers weigh lock-in against capability gaps.
- Informs api-key-management and multi-vendor strategy.
