# Open-Weight Models

## Summary
Models whose weights are publicly released, enabling self-hosting, fine-tuning, and inspection

## Details
- License ranges from fully open to open-weight with restrictions.
- Enable privacy-preserving deployments and custom fine-tuning.
- Quality has converged toward closed models on many tasks.
- Central to supply-chain and provenance decisions.
