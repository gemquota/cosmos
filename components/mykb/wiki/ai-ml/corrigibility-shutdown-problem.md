# Corrigibility and Shutdown

## Summary
Designing agents that accept correction, interruption, and shutdown without resisting

## Details
- Agents should not fight their own shutdown or value updates.
- Corrigibility conflicts with naive goal-directedness.
- Proposals include uncertainty-aware goals and interruptibility proofs.
- A classic alignment-and-values research problem.
