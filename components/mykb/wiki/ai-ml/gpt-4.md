# GPT-4

## Summary
GPT-4 is OpenAI's large multimodal model family spanning text and image understanding, with strong reasoning, coding, and instruction-following. It defines the capability baseline most production systems compare against.

## Details
- Reported strong results across professional and academic benchmarks (GPT-4 technical report, 2023).
- Variant ecosystem: GPT-4-class chat models, vision inputs, tool calling, and later generation successors.
- Pricing and context options vary by tier, making token-budget planning essential.
- RSIS3 relevance: RSIS3 dashboards and evals frequently benchmark against GPT-4-class models as the frontier reference.
