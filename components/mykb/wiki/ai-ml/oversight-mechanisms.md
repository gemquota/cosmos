# Oversight Mechanisms

## Summary
Structures that let humans or models check, constrain, and correct agent behavior

## Details
- Includes approval gates, audits, sandboxes, and scalable oversight.
- Good oversight is the difference between autonomy and recklessness.
- Designs trade speed against control.
- Applied in human-in-the-loop-approvals and agent-supervision.
