# Outer Alignment

## Summary
Whether the training objective and reward signal themselves match human intentions

## Details
- Even perfect optimization of a flawed objective fails alignment.
- Specification gaming is the classic outer-alignment failure.
- Designs use debate, amplification, and careful reward shaping.
- The first half of the alignment problem.
