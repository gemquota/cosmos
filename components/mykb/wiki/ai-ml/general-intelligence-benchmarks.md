# General Intelligence Benchmarks

## Summary
Suites aiming to measure broad cognitive skill rather than single tasks

## Details
- Examples: MMLU, ARC, GPQA, and agentic generalist suites.
- Breadth reduces overfitting to any one task.
- Scores correlate with practical usefulness but imperfectly.
- Used in capability assessment and leaderboards.
