# Data Labeling Workflows

## Summary
Processes for producing labels, ratings, or annotations on training and eval data

## Details
- Spans instructions, preferences, safety labels, and eval judgments.
- Combines human annotators, model-in-the-loop, and automated rules.
- Label quality and consistency gate downstream training.
- Plays into human-feedback-collection for RLHF.
