# Qwen and Gemma

## Summary
Open-weight model families from Alibaba and Google with strong multilingual and small-model variants

## Details
- Qwen spans dense and MoE models across sizes and languages.
- Gemma offers compact, permissively usable checkpoints.
- Smaller variants fit on-device-llm deployments.
- Both broaden the open-weights landscape.
