## Genefunction

GeneFunction appears in 1 session(s) categorized as API, Mobile, Shell. Related topics: android, api, bash, cli.

GeneFunction describes the role a gene plays in a biological simulation: the effect its encoded value has on the traits of an organism or cell. In simulation terms, a gene function maps genotype to phenotype, determining measurable properties such as growth rate, color, behavior, or metabolism.

In genetic algorithms and evolutionary simulations, individuals are described by a genome, and a fitness function evaluates how well the phenotype performs in the environment. Gene functions define the space of possible phenotypes, so their design shapes what evolution can discover. Mutation perturbs individual genes, crossover recombines genomes, and selection biases reproduction toward fitter individuals.

Implementation details matter: gene values may be continuous or discrete, gene expression may interact across loci, and the mapping from genotype to phenotype is often where realism or abstraction lives. Because simulations must be reproducible, gene functions are usually pure: the same genome and environment yield the same phenotype, and randomness is confined to explicitly seeded mutation and drift.
