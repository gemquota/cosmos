# App Store Review

App Store review evaluates every submission against Apple guidelines: safety, performance, business model, and design. Review time varies; guideline violations cause rejection with a resolution center back-and-forth.
- Prepare privacy nutrition labels, entitlements, and test accounts.
- Common rejections: broken links, misleading metadata, guideline 4.x design issues.
- Appeal or request expedited review for time-sensitive fixes.
- Internal and external TestFlight tracks bypass full review for testing.
