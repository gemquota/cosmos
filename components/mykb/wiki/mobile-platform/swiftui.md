# SwiftUI

SwiftUI is Apple declarative UI framework: views are a function of state, re-rendered automatically, with previews and multiplatform reach across Apple devices. It is the modern alternative to UIKit.
- Declarative view trees with @State, @Observable, and bindings.
- Previews, canvas, and Swift Playgrounds speed iteration.
- Works with WidgetKit, watchOS complications, and visionOS.
- UIKit interop via UIViewControllerRepresentable for gaps.
