# App Store Optimization

App Store Optimization (ASO) improves a store listing visibility and conversion: title, keywords, screenshots, ratings, and reviews. It is the organic growth channel for mobile apps.
- Keyword research informs titles and subtitles (Android) and keyword fields (iOS).
- Screenshots and preview videos drive conversion from listing visits.
- Ratings and review responses affect ranking and trust.
- Localize listings for international markets.
