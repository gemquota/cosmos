## Harmonica Harmonic Explorer

Harmonica Explorer — a music exploration project for touchscreen devices. Allows sliding between notes with chromatic sequencing.

## Overview

Harmonica Harmonic Explorer is a music exploration project for touchscreen devices that lets a player slide between notes with chromatic sequencing. Instead of committing to a fixed melody, the interface exposes the harmonic space directly: the player moves a finger across a surface and hears the pitch change in controlled steps, making the instrument exploratory rather than performance-oriented.

Chromatic sequencing means the playable steps follow the twelve-tone chromatic scale, so sliding across the surface moves through every semitone in range. This design is forgiving for beginners because every position on the surface produces a valid note, and it is interesting for experimenters because unusual intervals become easy to reach. The touchscreen interaction model — press, slide, release — maps naturally to continuous pitch control.

## Key Properties

- Interaction: slide-based input rather than discrete keys or buttons.
- Scale: chromatic steps give uniform semitone movement across the range.
- Accessibility: any position yields sound, lowering the barrier to play.
