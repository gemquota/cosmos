# Software Craftsmanship

## Summary
Software craftsmanship extends the agile manifesto with a fourth value: not just working software, but well-crafted software — and not just responding to change, but steadily adding value. It frames development as a profession of continuous learning, apprenticeships, and pride in the work.

## Details
- The manifesto of software craftsmanship: working software and well-crafted software, responding to change and steadily adding value.
- Craft emphasizes technical excellence: clean code, tests, refactoring, and deliberate practice.
- Apprenticeship and mentorship carry the craft: juniors learn from seniors through pairing and review.
- Craftsmanship counters 'done means shipped': done means shipped and maintainable, with tests and documentation.
- It is a complement to agile process: process organizes work, craft raises the standard of the work itself.
- For the mykb bundle, craftsmanship shows in consistent frontmatter, verified sources, and articles written to a standard.

Worked example — a craftsman finishing a wiki article treats it like code: frontmatter validated, sources curl-verified, links resolved, and a follow-up planned for promotion from stub to full.
