# Standup Practices

## Summary
The standup is a short daily meeting where the team synchronizes: what moved, what is blocked, and what happens next. Its purpose is coordination and unblocking — not status to management, and not problem-solving on the spot.

## Details
- Keep it short (15 minutes max) and on topic; detailed discussions continue in smaller groups after.
- The three questions (yesterday, today, blockers) are a starting template, not a script.
- Blocker escalation is the point: a raised hand becomes a conversation after the circle.
- Remote teams need async-friendly standups: written updates and a shared board replace the physical circle.
- Anti-patterns: manager status theater, deep debugging in the meeting, and attendance without listening.
- For the mykb bundle, the curation standup syncs what captured, what verified, and what is blocked on sources.
- Worked example — a wiki standup: two captures landed, link verification hit a rate limit (blocker), and the sync fix is queued; the blocker gets a sidebar after the standup.
