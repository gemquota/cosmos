# Tech Lead Roles

## Summary
The tech lead is the engineer who owns a team's technical direction: architecture decisions, code standards, delivery quality, and technical risk. The role combines hands-on work with leadership — and its hardest part is balancing both.

## Details
- Tech leads own the what and why of technical decisions, while delegating the how with clear guardrails.
- They are the team's technical spokesperson: tradeoffs explained, risks surfaced, and standards defended.
- The role splits across archetypes: architect-lead (design), delivery-lead (execution), and people-lead variants.
- Success requires staying close to code — a lead who stops writing code loses credibility and context quickly.
- Responsibilities include onboarding, review load, incident response, and knowledge distribution.
- For the mykb bundle, the tech lead owns the acquisition pipeline's architecture and the wiki's linking conventions.

Worked example — a tech lead on the wiki team decides the bundle adopts an outbox pattern for sync, writes the ADR, pairs with the implementer, and reviews the migration before rollout.
