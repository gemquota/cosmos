# Edge Inference

## Summary
Running models on user-adjacent devices or servers to cut latency and preserve privacy

## Details
- Includes on-device LLM and small dedicated servers.
- Reduces network dependency and data exposure.
- Constraints: memory, compute, and model size.
- Pairs with quantization and distillation.
