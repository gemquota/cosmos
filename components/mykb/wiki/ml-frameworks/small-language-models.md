# Small Language Models

## Summary
Compact models tuned for efficiency, speed, and on-device deployment

## Details
- Trade raw capability for cost, latency, and portability.
- Strong results via distillation and curated training data.
- Often sufficient for routing and narrow tasks.
- Complement large models in routing-models systems.
