Epoch 1 is the original program: one lineage of intelligence grown from a
production-grade surface (Phases 1–5) to decade-scale maturity (Phases
46–50). It spans ten five-phase sequels and three maturity arcs — the
same three that defined the roadmap from the start:

- **Phases 1–15 — Operational → Governed → Distributed Autonomy**: build,
  communicate, secure, persist, observe, operate, self-retune (1–5);
  remember collectively, verify, measure economics, govern, predict
  (6–10); generalize, collaborate, federate, attest, persist (11–15).
- **Phases 16–30 — Open → Ecosystem → Sovereign Autonomy**: audit,
  standardize, port, probe, open (16–20); identify, exchange, coordinate,
  govern, survive (21–25); meta-govern, sustain, self-direct, steward,
  endure (26–30).
- **Phases 31–50 — Generational → Symbiotic → Global → Meta-Science
  Autonomy**: inherit, archive, succeed, migrate, endure (31–35); explain,
  delegate, co-design, calibrate, collaborate (36–40); standardize,
  federate, steward, respond, cooperate (41–45); measure, hypothesize,
  experiment, prove, transcend (46–50).

## Cross-roadmap invariant

> Autonomy is cumulative but never unconditional: every expansion of
> capability must inherit the memory, verification, cost, policy,
> provenance and observability controls established by preceding phases.

This invariant is the first law of the epoch. Each sequel below lists the
preceding phases it depends on; none may skip them.

## Telemetry & dashboard contract

Every phase in Epoch 1 ships, alongside its deliverables: (1) new
telemetry events registered in the contract and surfaced in
`ecosystem.json` / `dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a dashboard/web UI
update that makes the phase's output visible — no silent phases (T0).
The dashboard "Roadmap" tab and `roadmap.json` snapshot make this
contract legible: every phase appears in the program registry with its
status.
