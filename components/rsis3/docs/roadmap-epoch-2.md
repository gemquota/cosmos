# Roadmap Epoch 2 — Phases 51–100 (Ten Sequels: XI–XX)

Adopted: 2026-08-10 · Status: active · Mode: llm-driven · Epoch: 2
Continues: [`roadmap-epoch-1.md`](roadmap-epoch-1.md) (Phases 1–50)
Linked goal stack: `rack/goals_stack.json` (goal-stack-001, T0–T3)

Epoch 2 — the Age of Living Systems — moves from one lineage of
intelligence to a *society* of them. Cosmos is no longer the only
intelligence in the room: it interoperates (51–55), participates in an
economy (56–60), composes into superorganisms (61–65), co-evolves with
society (66–70), senses and stewards the planet (71–75), carries culture
(76–80), lives under law (81–85), plans across generations (86–90),
operates on frontiers (91–95), and finally becomes an enduring,
self-aware society of minds (96–100). Every expansion inherits the
memory, verification, cost, policy, provenance and observability
controls of Epoch 1.

## Cross-roadmap invariant

> Autonomy is cumulative but never unconditional: every expansion of
> capability must inherit the memory, verification, cost, policy,
> provenance and observability controls established by preceding phases.

Epoch 2 phases frequently *reuse* Epoch 1 primitives rather than
reinvent them — the memory API (6), verification mesh (7), budgets (8),
governance (9), self-model (10), federation (13), invariants (14),
identity (21), commons (42), longitudinal metrics (46) and endurance
(30) all reappear as explicit dependencies below.

## Telemetry & dashboard contract

Every phase in Epoch 2 ships, alongside its deliverables: (1) new
telemetry events registered in the contract and surfaced in
`ecosystem.json` / `dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a dashboard/web UI
update that makes the phase's output visible — no silent phases (T0).
As with Epoch 1, the dashboard "Roadmap" tab and `roadmap.json` snapshot
make this contract legible: all 100 phases appear in the program
registry, each with its arc, area and status.

## Epoch registry

Both epochs are recorded in `rack/epochs.json` (delivered with Phase 50's
capstone work and extended by Phase 100): epoch identifiers, phase
ranges, arc maps and per-phase status. The registry is the machine-
readable source of truth that the dashboard Roadmap tab renders.
