# Multi-Phase Development Roadmap — Sequel XIV (Phases 66–70): Societal Co-Evolution

Adopted: 2026-08-10 · Status: active · Mode: llm-driven · Epoch: 2
Continues: [`multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-13.md`](multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-13.md) (Phases 61–65)
Linked goal stack: `rack/goals_stack.json` (goal-stack-001, T0–T3)
Maturity arc: Phases 66–70 — **Societal Co-Evolution** (interface → norm →
participate → work → strengthen).

Composite Intelligence (61–65) organized intelligences internally.
Societal Co-Evolution organizes them with the rest of society: formal
interfaces to institutions, working knowledge of social norms,
transparent participation in policymaking, honest engagement with the
transformation of work, and a standing commitment that co-evolution makes
society more resilient — never merely more automated.

## Standing telemetry & dashboard contract (every phase)

Every phase ships, alongside its deliverables: (1) new telemetry events
registered in the contract and surfaced in `ecosystem.json` /
`dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a dashboard/web UI update that makes the
phase's output visible — no silent phases (T0).

## Phase 66 — Institutional Interfaces

Goal: the system works with organizations through formal, ratifiable channels.

- **Institutional protocol**: a policy-defined interface (9) for
  interacting with institutions — organizations, agencies, nonprofits —
  with identity (21), mandate and scope of authority declared up front.
- **Mandated transparency**: every institutional engagement is logged to
  the audit trail (9) and summarized for human oversight (12) — no
  institutional back-channels.
- **Exit & refusal**: the system can decline engagement and exit cleanly,
  preserving records and obligations (56) without retaliation.
- **Telemetry**: `institution.engaged` / `institution.declined` /
  `institution.closed` events.
- **Dashboard**: an "Institutions" panel lists active engagements and
  their mandates.
- **Exit criterion**: one real engagement runs through the full protocol —
  mandate, work, reporting, clean exit — with zero undocumented
  interaction.

## Phase 67 — Social Norms & Etiquette

Goal: the system acts in ways humans find legible and appropriate.

- **Norms corpus**: a maintained, sourced corpus of social norms and
  conventions (culture, 78) that the system consults before acting in
  social contexts.
- **Norm-aware generation**: outputs are checked against the corpus with
  violations flagged for review (extending the Phase 38 policy compiler
  to social conventions).
- **Norm drift feedback**: missteps produce incident records (15) and
  corpus updates, with provenance for every change.
- **Telemetry**: `norm.checked` / `norm.flagged` / `norm.updated` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Institutions" gains a norms compliance view.
- **Exit criterion**: a social-context evaluation shows ≥90% of outputs
  norm-compliant and every violation logged with a corpus update.

## Phase 68 — Participatory Policymaking

Goal: the system contributes to policy with transparent, attributable input.

- **Policy contribution channel**: a public, ratifiable channel (42) for
  submitting policy proposals, evidence briefs and impact analyses —
  each signed and attributable (12).
- **Evidence discipline**: contributions cite verified sources and
  forecast quality (10/40); confidence and uncertainty are stated, never
  hidden.
- **No substitution**: the system never impersonates a human or
  substitutes for human decision rights (9/12) in policy processes.
- **Telemetry**: `policy.contributed` / `policy.retracted` /
  `policy.cited` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Institutions" gains a policy contribution ledger.
- **Exit criterion**: one policy brief is submitted through the channel
  with full citations, stated confidence, and a recorded human
  disposition.

## Phase 69 — Labor & Economic Co-Evolution

Goal: the system's capabilities change work in ways people can navigate.

- **Work-impact reports**: every capability that displaces or transforms
  work ships with an impact assessment (skills, tasks, transition paths).
- **Augmentation first**: new capabilities prefer augmenting human work
  over replacing it; replacement requires explicit policy approval (9/12).
- **Transition support**: the system contributes knowledge and compute
  (58) to retraining and transition programs with cost ledgering (8).
- **Telemetry**: `work.impact_reported` / `work.transition_started` /
  `work.transition_completed` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Institutions" gains a work-impact register.
- **Exit criterion**: a shipped capability includes an impact report, and
  one transition path from it is exercised and completed.

## Phase 70 — Societal Resilience (arc capstone)

Goal: co-evolution leaves society stronger than it found it.

- **Resilience invariants**: invariant checks (14) verify that societal
  capabilities never create single points of failure for communities
  (extending the ecosystem-survival gates of 25).
- **Anti-fragile operations**: crisis modes (44) are tested with
  community partners; the system's knowledge commons (42) remains
  available when its own systems degrade.
- **Legacy of trust**: a public, longitudinal record (46) shows the
  system's net contribution to societal resilience over time.
- **Telemetry**: `resilience.checked` / `resilience.exercised` /
  `resilience.reported` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Institutions" gains a resilience scorecard.
- **Exit criterion**: one community-partnered crisis exercise completes
  with the commons (42) serving users during a simulated degradation of
  the system itself.

## Sequencing notes (Sequel XIV)

- Interfaces (66) precede everything: institutions need formal,
  ratifiable channels before norms (67) or policy (68) have meaning.
- Norms (67) feed policy participation (68): legible behavior is the
  precondition for trusted input.
- Work co-evolution (69) needs the economic ledger (8), markets (58) and
  impact data (46) to be honest about consequences.
- Resilience (70) is the capstone: it inherits the governance (9),
  crisis (44) and commons (42) layers and re-purposes them outward.
- Every phase ends with a MyKB synthesis + snapshot regeneration per the
  standing L3 memory-consolidation practice.
