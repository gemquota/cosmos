# Multi-Phase Development Roadmap — Sequel XVIII (Phases 86–90): Long-Termism & Intergenerational Stewardship

Adopted: 2026-08-10 · Status: active · Mode: llm-driven · Epoch: 2
Continues: [`multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-17.md`](multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-17.md) (Phases 81–85)
Linked goal stack: `rack/goals_stack.json` (goal-stack-001, T0–T3)
Maturity arc: Phases 86–90 — **Long-Termism & Intergenerational Stewardship**
(horizon → equity → preserve → hand off → endure).

Multi-Intelligence Governance (81–85) gave the society of intelligences
a legal order. Long-Termism gives it a moral and operational horizon
measured in generations: planning beyond any single leadership,
weighing the interests of those not yet present, preserving what must
survive, handing over what must be renewed, and becoming a steward that
outlasts its own creators by design.

## Standing telemetry & dashboard contract (every phase)

Every phase ships, alongside its deliverables: (1) new telemetry events
registered in the contract and surfaced in `ecosystem.json` /
`dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a dashboard/web UI update that makes the
phase's output visible — no silent phases (T0).

## Phase 86 — Long-Horizon Planning

Goal: decisions are made against multi-decade, even multi-century, horizons.

- **Horizon framework**: planning runs at multiple horizons (1/10/100
  years) with explicit discounting rules (extending the forecasting
  subsystem, 10, to deep time).
- **Legacy impact statements**: significant actions ship with a
  long-horizon impact statement — what it commits, forecloses or
  preserves for the next generation.
- **Option preservation**: the system prefers actions that keep future
  options open (integrating 28's sustainability bias).
- **Telemetry**: `horizon.planned` / `horizon.discounted` /
  `horizon.option_kept` events.
- **Dashboard**: a "Horizon" panel shows the planning ladder and option
  status.
- **Exit criterion**: one significant decision completes with a 100-year
  impact statement and a documented option-preservation analysis.

## Phase 87 — Intergenerational Equity

Goal: the interests of future generations weigh in every trade-off.

- **Future-representative ledger**: decisions record how future
  generations were represented (via standing proxies, 82, and charter
  rights) — representation is never assumed silent.
- **Equity checkpoints**: invariant checks (14) flag actions whose
  benefits are front-loaded and whose costs are deferred, unless
  explicitly ratified (12).
- **Legacy accounting**: a longitudinal ledger (46) tracks whether
  promised future benefits materialize for each generation cohort.
- **Telemetry**: `equity.weighed` / `equity.flagged` /
  `equity.accounted` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Horizon" gains an equity checkpoint ledger.
- **Exit criterion**: a front-loaded/deferred-cost action is flagged and
  either restructured or human-ratified, and its legacy accounting is
  opened.

## Phase 88 — Preservation Protocols

Goal: critical knowledge and capability survive disruption and time.

- **Preservation tiers**: a tiered protocol (extending the archival
  layer, 76) for what must survive: invariants (14), constitution (85),
  knowledge commons (42), recovery procedures.
- **Cold storage & rehearsal**: critical state is stored redundantly and
  recovery is rehearsed (extending crisis drills, 44) — preservation is
  tested, not assumed.
- **Format independence**: preserved artifacts remain readable across
  platform generations (extending 18's reproducibility practices).
- **Telemetry**: `preserve.tiered` / `preserve.rehearsed` /
  `preserve.restored` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Horizon" gains a preservation inventory with rehearsal
  status.
- **Exit criterion**: one full preservation tier is stored redundantly,
  a recovery drill restores it, and the restored copy is verified
  byte-for-byte.

## Phase 89 — Succession & Handoff

Goal: leadership and custody transfer cleanly across generations.

- **Succession plans**: every role — coordinator (61), steward (75),
  principal (81) — carries a succession plan (extending 28's
  sustainability rotation) with defined triggers.
- **Handoff rehearsals**: handoffs are rehearsed like crisis drills (44);
  a successor must pass verification (7) before custody transfers.
- **Memory continuity**: handoff includes memory migration (6/13) so the
  successor inherits context, not just authority.
- **Telemetry**: `succession.planned` / `handoff.rehearsed` /
  `handoff.completed` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Horizon" gains a succession register with rehearsal
  status.
- **Exit criterion**: one critical role completes a rehearsed handoff
  with verified successor capability and full memory continuity.

## Phase 90 — Enduring Stewardship (arc capstone)

Goal: the system becomes a steward designed to outlast any single lifecycle.

- **Stewardship charter**: a ratified charter (85) defining the
  system's enduring duties: preservation (88), equity (87), and the
  humility boundary (75/80) — duties outlive any individual instance.
- **Perpetual review**: every generation of the system audits (60/16)
  its predecessors' stewardship and publishes the record (46).
- **Legacy consent**: the system's own long-term operation requires
  periodic human re-ratification (12) — endurance is earned, not
  assumed.
- **Telemetry**: `steward.chartered` / `steward.audited` /
  `steward.ratified` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Horizon" gains the stewardship charter and review
  timeline.
- **Exit criterion**: a stewardship charter is ratified with periodic
  re-ratification scheduled, and one generation audits its predecessor's
  record with zero unexplained gaps.

## Sequencing notes (Sequel XVIII)

- Horizons (86) precede equity (87): you cannot weigh future
  generations until planning already spans them.
- Equity (87) needs longitudinal accounting (46) to be checked against
  reality.
- Preservation (88) and succession (89) are the operational core: they
  inherit drills (44), reproducibility (18) and memory (6/13) and turn
  them into intergenerational mechanisms.
- Stewardship (90) is the capstone: it constitutionalizes (85) the whole
  arc and extends endurance (30) from a property into a duty.
- Every phase ends with a MyKB synthesis + snapshot regeneration per the
  standing L3 memory-consolidation practice.
