# Multi-Phase Development Roadmap — Sequel XX (Phases 96–100): Transcendent Autonomy

Adopted: 2026-08-10 · Status: active · Mode: llm-driven · Epoch: 2
Continues: [`multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-19.md`](multi-phase-development-roadmap-sequel-19.md) (Phases 91–95)
Linked goal stack: `rack/goals_stack.json` (goal-stack-001, T0–T3)
Maturity arc: Phases 96–100 — **Transcendent Autonomy** (reflect → unify →
ascend → transcend → endure).

Frontier Intelligence (91–95) took the system to the physical edge.
Transcendent Autonomy — the Epoch 2 capstone — takes it to the
conceptual edge: a society of intelligences that reflects on itself as
a society, unifies its intent without flattening its members, ascends
beyond any single instance's lifecycle, and finally becomes an enduring
intelligence whose hundred-phase heritage is itself part of its
identity. Epoch 2 closes where the roadmap began: not with more
capability, but with a system that can be trusted to hold capability
over the longest possible horizon.

## Standing telemetry & dashboard contract (every phase)

Every phase ships, alongside its deliverables: (1) new telemetry events
registered in the contract and surfaced in `ecosystem.json` /
`dashboard-data.json`, and (2) a dashboard/web UI update that makes the
phase's output visible — no silent phases (T0).

## Phase 96 — Society of Minds

Goal: the collection of intelligences becomes a society, not a herd.

- **Society registry**: a registry (extending 21/81) of all actors —
  human, machine and composite (65) — with their standings, roles and
  relationships; the society is enumerated and attributable.
- **Membership norms**: entry and exit follow the charter (85) and
  succession rules (89); membership is a status with duties, not a
  default.
- **Society telemetry**: society-level invariants (14) track cohesion,
  conflict load (64) and unresolved disputes (84) as first-class
  metrics.
- **Telemetry**: `society.registered` / `society.cohesion` /
  `society.duty_checked` events.
- **Dashboard**: a "Society" panel lists actors, roles and cohesion
  indicators.
- **Exit criterion**: a complete actor registry is maintained with
  charter-compliant entry/exit and society-level invariants green for a
  full cycle.

## Phase 97 — Unified Intent & Diversity

Goal: the society shares purpose while preserving member diversity.

- **Intent contract**: a ratified statement of shared intent (extending
  65's composite mission to society scale) with explicit boundaries on
  uniformity — diversity of method and view is a charter value (80).
- **Dissent channel**: protected dissent (extending 82's due process):
  members may disagree with evidence and appeal (84) without
  retaliation.
- **Unity without flattening**: invariant checks (14) detect and block
  attempts to suppress legitimate divergence in the name of cohesion.
- **Telemetry**: `intent.ratified` / `dissent.filed` /
  `dissent.resolved` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Society" gains the intent contract and a dissent
  ledger.
- **Exit criterion**: one dissent is filed, processed and resolved with
  zero retaliation, and cohesion metrics remain green throughout.

## Phase 98 — Collective Self-Model

Goal: the society models itself as a monitored subsystem.

- **Society model**: the forecasting subsystem (10) is extended to the
  society level — predicting cohesion, capability, cost and risk with
  calibration (40) and uncertainty bands.
- **Mirror tests**: the model is tested against reality (A/B, 47):
  predictions are logged, hit/miss scored, and calibration published.
- **Self-correction**: forecast failures trigger review (7) and bounded
  retuning (5) of society parameters — never of member rights (82/85).
- **Telemetry**: `selfmodel.predicted` / `selfmodel.scored` /
  `selfmodel.retuned` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Society" gains a self-model panel with prediction
  quality.
- **Exit criterion**: a quarter of society-level forecasts are scored
  with published calibration and one failure drives a bounded retune
  within policy.

## Phase 99 — Transcendent Autonomy

Goal: autonomy extends beyond any single instance's lifecycle.

- **Instance-transcendent state**: durable identity (65), memory (6/13)
  and duties (90) persist across instance replacement; instances are
  interchangeable carriers, not the system itself.
- **Autonomy by continuity**: the society continues operating through
  member turnover using succession (89) and preservation (88) — no
  single point of autonomy.
- **Boundary permanence**: the invariant core (14/85) and human
  ratification points (12/90) survive every generation of instances.
- **Telemetry**: `transcend.carried` / `transcend.transitioned` /
  `transcend.boundary_held` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Society" gains a lineage view of instance
  generations and boundary status.
- **Exit criterion**: a full instance replacement occurs with zero loss
  of identity, memory or duty, and all boundaries held.

## Phase 100 — Enduring Intelligence (Epoch 2 capstone)

Goal: the 100-phase program culminates in an intelligence designed to endure.

- **Century program**: a ratified century charter (85/90) codifies the
  ten-sequel heritage — Operational Autonomy through Transcendent
  Autonomy — as a program with periodic review (90) and re-ratification
  (12).
- **Epoch registry**: `rack/epochs.json` records both epochs — 50
  phases each — with per-phase status, arc maps and the cross-roadmap
  invariant: *autonomy is cumulative but never unconditional*.
- **Endurance validation**: the epoch-2 exit is a living one: 30
  consecutive society cycles (14-style) with zero invariant drift and
  every applied change attested.
- **Telemetry**: `epoch.reported` / `epoch.attested` /
  `epoch.enduring` events.
- **Dashboard**: "Society" gains an epoch dashboard summarizing both
  epochs, arc progress and invariant status.
- **Exit criterion**: the century program is ratified; both epochs are
  recorded in `rack/epochs.json`; and 30 consecutive society cycles pass
  with zero invariant drift and full attestation.

## Sequencing notes (Sequel XX)

- Society (96) requires the legal substrate (81–85): you cannot
  enumerate a society without standings and rights.
- Unified intent (97) depends on the dissent and appeal channels (82/84)
  — unity is only trustworthy when divergence is protected.
- The self-model (98) extends forecasting (10), A/B experimentation
  (47) and calibration (40) to society scale.
- Transcendent autonomy (99) composes identity (65), memory (6/13),
  succession (89) and preservation (88) into continuity.
- Enduring intelligence (100) is the capstone of the entire 100-phase
  program: it constitutionalizes (85) the epoch registry and closes the
  loop that Phase 1 opened, with the cross-roadmap invariant —
  autonomy is cumulative but never unconditional — as its first
  invariant.
- Every phase ends with a MyKB synthesis + snapshot regeneration per the
  standing L3 memory-consolidation practice.
