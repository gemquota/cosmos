## Round 1: Process and Cadence

### Open-Ended 6.1.1
**What development process or methodology best fits this project? How should work be planned and tracked?**

Write freely. Describe a typical week or iteration. Consider how requirements arrive and how progress is measured.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) Sprint-based agile (Scrum) — fixed-length iterations with formal ceremonies
- b) Continuous flow (Kanban) — pull-based with WIP limits, no fixed iterations
- c) Lean/startup — build-measure-learn cycles, just-in-time planning

### Open-Ended 6.1.2
**What is the expected team size, composition, and structure?**

Write freely. List the roles needed and how many people fill them.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) Solo or pair — 1–2 people, all roles combined
- b) Small team — 3–6 people with distinct roles (dev, design, PM)
- c) Multiple teams — 7+ people across 2+ squads with coordination overhead

## Round 2: Quality and Review Practices

### Open-Ended 6.2.1
**What code review, testing, and quality assurance practices should be followed?**

Write freely. Describe what quality means for this project and how it's enforced.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) Light — ad-hoc reviews, manual testing, minimal automation
- b) Standard — mandatory reviews, unit + integration tests, CI gates
- c) Rigorous — pair programming, TDD, property-based testing, full automation

---

### Open-Ended 6.2.2
**How should the team handle technical debt, refactoring, and code quality?**

Write freely. Describe the attitude toward perfection vs. pragmatism.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) Opportunistic — clean as you go, no formal tracking
- b) Budgeted — allocate X% of each sprint to debt reduction
- c) Formal — tracked backlog with dedicated quality improvement cycles

## Round 3: Communication and Collaboration

### Open-Ended 6.3.1
**How should the team communicate, share knowledge, and manage decisions?**

Write freely. Consider time zone distribution, communication tools, and feedback loops.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) Async-first — written docs, Slack/Teams, minimal meetings
- b) Balanced — regular standups, weekly syncs, supplemented by async
- c) Sync-heavy — daily standups, frequent pairing, co-located or synchronous remote

---

### Open-Ended 6.3.2
**What is the decision-making and escalation process? How are architectural choices made?**

Write freely. Describe who decides what and how disagreements are resolved.

**After answering, choose one:**
- a) BDFL — one person makes final decisions with input
- b) Consensus-driven — team agrees, with escalation to lead if deadlocked
